# Curve Fever Pro Tournament Hub — Handoff

Current state only, kept short: read it at the start of any real work session. History and the reasoning behind decisions live in `HANDOFF_LOG.md`.

## What this is
A web app for setting up and running Curve Fever Pro tournaments: organisers load a roster, generate rounds, enter scores, resolve ties and manage players or teams live; a shareable link lets everyone else follow the scoreboard, bracket and rankings as they happen. It is a React/TypeScript rebuild of an earlier vanilla-JS app, kept in git at the tag `legacy-app` (`git show legacy-app:legacy/js/<file>`). The rebuild ported the legacy rules faithfully, so that code is a valid reference for "what should this do". Log entries dated before 2026-09-10 describe the legacy app.

## Core value
Handle "uncomfortable" registration counts automatically. Whatever the headcount — 31, 37, 43, 53 — the app works out room distribution, advancement numbers and lucky losers without the organiser having to think about it. Testing for it: `AGENTS.md`, "Design forks and scoping".

## Rules summary
Details and edge cases: `docs/rules.md`.
- **Formats**: `ffa-individual` (rooms 6–8), `individual-1v1`, and team formats built from a rule, players per team (2–5) × teams per room (2–5), at most 10 players a room (so `team-2v2v2v2` has rooms of 3–4 teams, `team-3v3v3` 2–3, and the two-team ones, 2v2 to 5v5, are head-to-head). Head-to-head formats have rooms of 2 and an odd-count strategy defaulting to "Bye".
- **Pooling phases**: none (2 warm-up rounds), qualification table, Swiss and Group Stage (head-to-head only).
- **Bracket phases**: single elimination, double elimination (head-to-head race), shared-Final double elimination (multi-unit), Kings Valley (room ladder, not for head-to-head), layered bracket (FFA after a qualification table: the app writes a Custom bracket in two or three levels of equal rooms, sized from 75-85% of those expected to play, planned again while the table runs and offered as a card only when the bracket's shape changes), upper and lower bracket (built in, after any pooling phase: the qualifiers split by pooling place into two or four rooms, worked out from the head count and again when it changes while pooling runs, overrides "Advance to bracket" and "Upper rooms"; a Custom bracket at runtime), waterfall (organiser-authored routing, optionally fed by the qualification table; played by dependency play: a round starts as soon as every round that routes into it is sent on, Next Round sends on every finished round, several rounds can be in play, "← Previous" undoes or reopens the last press; a tournament stored without `dependencyPlayVersion` plays by columns).
- **Scoring** (pooling standings): "Points" summed over the counted rounds, higher is better: Standard points (a table by the largest room, 10, 8, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 for 8, else the largest room size down to 1; a smaller room reads the table scaled to its size, in half points; versioned by `gamemodeConfig.pointsVersion`, a tournament without it keeps the earlier unscaled rule; a bye counts as a win, a missed round earns 0; under match results a Swiss or qualification bye is a Tie Win) or a Custom points table, or, in a Group Stage or in Swiss or a qualification table of head-to-head rooms of two, **Match results** (per game a Win / Tie Win / Tie Loss / Loss / Tie by the gap against a tie margin, a Best of 3 as one match; `docs/rules.md`, "Match results"); the tie-break is the score relative to the room average. A new Group Stage ranks its groups by **points per match played** (a bye is not a match, a match against an absent or removed unit is void for the other, a no-show gets a counted 0, a reserve takes over the seat's line) and breaks ties by head to head, then the room average, then the organiser ("Group stage standings"). Every round sums raw scores over 1–4 games or is a Best of N (3, 5 or 7 by stage, plus a per-round override; a custom-bracket round's own format can be a Best of N 3–9): first to (N + 1) / 2 game wins, ordered by wins, then total; across rooms the relative score of the total counts. A Custom bracket round of one room of two (and the Final of a head-to-head upper and lower bracket) can play **uneven wins** (`WINS`): each unit needs its own number of game wins, typed on the band into the round or by column. Team formats pick a team scoring rule: sum of members, Save your Buddy (defender only: only the defender's score is entered) or One score per team.
- **Seeding**: rank tiers with rematch avoidance (`tieredSeed`), smaller-room seats spread evenly over every mixed-size room round (results first once units are eliminated); the Bracket shows admins the expected draw of undrawn rounds (a Custom bracket round once its own feeders are finished, else the next column once the round in play is scored); viewers see the slot labels until the draw. See `docs/seeding.md`.
