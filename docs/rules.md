# Tournament rules

Current rules of the app's tournament engine (`src/domain/tournament/`). Read when a task touches generation, advancement, scoring, formats or phases. Build history and reasoning: search `HANDOFF_LOG.md` for the entry names cited below.

## Game formats
`formats.ts`, `GAME_FORMATS`. A "unit" is a player (individual formats) or a team.

Two individual formats are written out, and the team formats are built from a rule: **players per team `t` (2–5) × teams per room `k` (2–5)**, with `t × k` at most 10, the game's hard cap on players in one room (`HARD_ROOM_PLAYER_CAP`, `formats.ts`, shared with `validateRoomCap`). The key is `team-` plus `k` copies of `t` joined by `v` (`team-2v2v2`), and the label is the same without `team-`; `teamFormatKey()` and `teamsPerRoomOptions()` hold the rule, and Setup offers every valid combination with none ranked above another. The five formats that existed before the rule (`ffa-individual`, `team-2v2v2v2`, `team-3v3v3`, `team-3v3`, `individual-1v1`) keep their keys and traits exactly, so saved tournaments need no migration.

| Format | Unit | Room size | Odd-count strategies (default) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ffa-individual` | player | 6–8, ideal 8 | — |
| `individual-1v1` | player | 2 (head-to-head) | `none`/`bye` (`bye`) |
| `team-2v2` | team of 2 | 2 (head-to-head) | `none`/`bye`/`flex` (`bye`) |
| `team-2v2v2` | team of 2 | 2–3, ideal 3 | — |
| `team-2v2v2v2` | team of 2 | 3–4, ideal 4 | — |
| `team-2v2v2v2v2` | team of 2 | 4–5, ideal 5 | — |
| `team-3v3` | team of 3 | 2 (head-to-head) | `none`/`bye`/`flex` (`bye`) |
| `team-3v3v3` | team of 3 | 2–3, ideal 3 | — |
| `team-4v4` | team of 4 | 2 (head-to-head) | `none`/`bye` (`bye`) |
| `team-5v5` | team of 5 | 2 (head-to-head) | `none`/`bye` (`bye`) |

With several teams per room (`k ≥ 3`) rooms hold from `k − 1` to `k` teams (`defaultRoomSize`); with two teams the format is head-to-head (`idealRoomSize: 2`, default strategy `bye`). A definition also carries `teamsPerRoom` (team formats) and `shortLabel` ("FFA", "1v1", "4v4"), which the Tournaments list shows; an unknown key shows as itself there and generation refuses it with "This game format is not available yet." (`GameFormatKey` is ``'ffa-individual' | 'individual-1v1' | `team-${string}` ``).

FFA counts that can't fill rooms of 6–8 are seated in smaller rooms by design (9 → [5,4], 17 → [6,6,5]). Planned advancement counts (what a schedule chooses itself) are always seatable; counts the organiser enters are seated as they come.

An unset odd-count strategy means the format's default. "Flex" lets a head-to-head team format seat a 3-team room (room size 2–3); it is offered only where three teams still fit the cap (`3 × t ≤ 10`), so for `team-2v2` and `team-3v3`, not for `team-4v4` and `team-5v5`. A Semis/Final size override is checked against the cap too (a 5v5 Final of 3 teams is 15 players and is refused). "None" refuses to generate on a count that can't be split evenly.

Room counts and sizes for any headcount come from `distributeRooms()` (`room-distribution.ts`); a round's advancement target splits into `advPerRoom` per room plus `luckyCount` lucky-loser slots (`splitAdvancement`). When removals leave a round with a different headcount than planned, `fitRoundToPool` reshapes it at advance time.

## Pooling phases (`poolingPhase`)
- `none` — 2 no-elimination warm-up rounds.
- `qual-table` — qualification rounds (3 by default) feeding one cumulative standings table; the top `qualAdv` advance. With a waterfall bracket the table is a source of the graph and `qualAdv` is derived from it (see "Waterfall bracket").
- `swiss` — 3–7 fold-paired rounds, same standings engine as `qual-table`. Every Swiss round is `floor(n/2)` rooms of 2 plus `n % 2` byes, whatever the odd-count strategy.
- `group-stage` — round-robin groups (single or double), top N per group advance. Pairings are known at generation (`round.matches`/`round.groupByes`).

Rules:
- Swiss and Group Stage are **head-to-head only** (`individual-1v1` and the two-teams team formats, 2v2 to 5v5), gated in Setup and in `generateTournament()`: fold-pairing and round-robin always pair exactly 2 units.
- **Non-counting rounds** (qual-table/Swiss only): a tournament can mark its leading N rounds `excludeFromStandings`; they're played and reseed normally but `computeQualificationStandings` skips them.
- **Reserve admission** (`addReserveUnit`, `mutations.ts`): a reserve or walk-up can join until the pooling phase ends (`reserveOpen`). **Before Confirm & Start** nobody but admins has seen the draw, so a joiner is part of it: with no schedule generated yet the reserve just joins the roster (`confirmedCount` follows); with one, the schedule is redrawn for the new head count from the settings it was generated with (`regenerateForRoster`, `pre-start-roster.ts`; `state.settings`, not the Setup form's current values) and the joiner plays Round 1. Generation is the only judge then: the group-stage, fixed-draw, pooling-less-waterfall and `'strict-room'` refusals below apply only after the start, and when generation refuses the new count the add is refused with its message (`'generation'`). A tournament generated before settings were stored keeps the rules below. A **brand-new team** can be added in a team format (`addWalkUpTeam`: one `TeamName, Player1, Player2` line; refused with no member, with a name already used in `players` or `reserves`, or with an unstorable name) and joins like any reserve. **After the start:** while a pooling round is in play (warm-up, qualification or Swiss) the round stays as drawn: the joiner sits it out (0 points if it counts) and is drawn in with everyone else when the round advances, so no over-full room is ever created, in any bracket format. At that advance a no-elimination pooling round takes in every roster unit not already advancing (`withUndrawnRosterUnits`, `transitions.ts`), except at the last qualifying round, where the standings decide and the joiner, ranked last with 0 points, is normally cut. From 2 missed counted rounds (`missedCountedRounds`: qualification/Swiss rounds up to and including the one in play, not the leading non-counting ones) the admin confirms first (`confirm-late-join`). The odd-count strategy "None" refuses a joiner who would leave a count that is not a multiple of the room size (`'strict-room'`). After the start, group stage (`'group-stage'`), a fixed draw (`'fixed-draw'`) and a waterfall without a pooling phase (`'waterfall-bracket'`) still refuse. In a tournament with no pooling phase the window ends with its first bracket round: a reserve added then is seated in that round, in the least-full room (the over-cap confirm, `'strict-room'` under "None"); during a warm-up the joiner is drawn into the first bracket round. **First-round seeding:** a joiner's level is unknown, so in the round they enter they are seeded as an average unit (see `docs/seeding.md`): in the middle tier of the round they missed in the room-based reseed, at the end of the fold in Swiss so they meet a middle-ranked unit, and never given the bye (a bye counts as a win; if only joiners are left to take one, the usual rule applies). From their second round on their own results seed them, and their standings keep counting the missed rounds as 0. In a waterfall, a join or removal during qualification also reshapes the pooling rounds not yet reached for the new head count at once (`refitPoolingRound`, `pooling.ts`), so the Bracket shows the room sizes the next draw will use; the graph rounds and the last qualifying round's `standingsRoutes` are left alone.
- **Draw publication** (qual-table/Swiss, Setup "Draw publication"): `adaptive` (default) reseeds from live results; `fixed` publishes the whole pooling schedule at generation — see `docs/seeding.md`.

## Bracket phases (`scheduleLogic`)
- **`single-elimination`** — room-based cuts to Semis and a Final. **Semis size**: two rooms of the ideal size (FFA 16), or everyone entering the bracket when that is fewer, down to two rooms of the format's *smallest* room (FFA 12: 14 → [7, 7], 13 → [7, 6], 12 → [6, 6]; 2v2v2v2 6; 3v3v3 4; head-to-head 4); the Final stays at its room size (FFA 8), so 4 per Semis room. When everyone entering already fits the Semis there is no elimination round (`singleEliminationBracketPhase`); explicit targets still add rounds. A "Semis size override" wins over the default. Below the floor generation **refuses** (it never raises the request): registered count under the floor, "Advance to bracket" under the floor (`Advance to bracket (11) is below the minimum of 12 for single elimination (Semis needs 2 rooms of at least 6).`), and a Group Stage that sends fewer units than the floor to the bracket (`Group stage sends 3 players to the bracket; …`). A request above the confirmed count still clamps down to everyone.
- **"Advance to bracket" below a bracket format's floor is refused in every format**, naming the floor: single elimination 2 × smallest room, Kings Valley and shared-Final double elimination 2 × ideal room (FFA 16), race double elimination 4; waterfall has no floor here (its graph decides). Group Stage ignores the field (its entry is groups × qualifiers per group), so a leftover hidden value there never refuses generation.
- Single elimination and the shared-Final variant below accept optional explicit round targets and per-round seeding overrides (Setup → "Advanced round overrides"). The targets (and "Advance to bracket", `qualAdv`) stay editable live, in the Admin "Correct current round" panel (`live-advancement-edit.ts`):
  - **While pooling runs** (qual-table, Swiss, no-pooling warm-ups; single elimination and shared-Final): the bracket is rebuilt from scratch like Setup does, pooling rounds untouched (fixed draws included). In single elimination the Semis size follows the new "Advance to bracket" (`followsEntryCount`) unless the tournament has a Semis size override. The targets list is fully editable, length included (blank = automatic); with seeding overrides its length must stay the same (they are index-aligned). `qualAdv` (qual-table/Swiss only) is refused, not clamped, outside the format minimum and the roster count. A stale standings-cutoff tie resolution (`qual-cutoff`) is dropped when `qualAdv` changes.
  - **Single elimination mid-bracket**: values only, from the current round (until advanced, scores allowed) to the last round before Semis; the number of rounds never changes. The current round keeps its rooms; later rounds are rebuilt (`eliminationRound`, existing seeding overrides kept) and Semis is refitted to the last value. Tie resolutions stay (they order equal scores, wherever the cut moves).
  - Shared-Final double elimination is editable only until its bracket starts; race double elimination, Kings Valley, waterfall and group stage are not editable. A saved edit writes the settings snapshot (`settings.qualAdv`, `settings.eliminationRoundTargets`, `gamemodeConfig.explicitTargets`) so "Copy settings" copies what was played; an automatic curve edited mid-bracket becomes an explicit list.
- **`double-elimination`** — head-to-head race bracket (winners bracket, losers bracket, grand final). Not available with Flex.
- **`double-elimination-shared-final`** — multi-unit double elimination feeding one shared Final. A unit dropped from the winners bracket reaches a losers-bracket round within at most 2 winners-bracket rounds (`MAX_WB_ROUNDS_BEFORE_LB`, `double-elimination.ts`).
  - **Losers-bracket round sizes are always seatable.** When a losers round's count (previous survivors plus pending drops) can't be seated, three levers are tried in order: (1) the previous losers round's survivor target moves; (2) the round is deferred, up to the 2-round limit above; (3) the feeding winners round's target moves, keeping the curve strictly decreasing and the next winners round seatable. Each takes the nearest value that works, the smaller cut on a tie; organiser-set targets (`eliminationRoundTargets`) are never changed. A few small FFA configurations (17–19 entrants with 3–4 LB qualifiers) can't be fixed by any lever and fall back to smaller rooms by design.
- **`kings-valley`** — see below.
- **`waterfall-bracket`** — see below.

### Kings Valley (`kings-valley.ts`)
A room ladder: rooms are ranked top to bottom. Each round, a room's top finishers promote to the room above, its bottom finishers demote to the room below, the rest stay; the lowest room with a real match eliminates its bottom finishers instead of demoting them. Room 1's promote band stays in room 1.
- Promotions are `max(1, round(size × 0.25))` per room (`KINGS_VALLEY_MOVE_FRACTION`). The bottom room's cut is the one nearest half of it (`KINGS_VALLEY_ELIMINATION_FRACTION = 0.5`) that leaves a **seatable** total (every room within the format's minimum and maximum), the smaller cut on a tie. Demotions are then sized top-down so the moves land exactly on the next round's rooms (promotions are only lowered when a room couldn't supply its demotions). One function, `kingsValleyRoundMoves` (bands plus next room sizes), drives generation, the real advance and the Bracket exit chips, so chips, placeholders and the real seating agree. Room sizes are the `distributeRooms()` layout of the survivors; a room below the format's minimum is never planned.
- A room with fewer than 2 units (only after a removal) **holds**: nobody in it moves or is cut, and the cut comes from the lowest room that still has a real match (the plain per-room maths, no seatable-cut solving).
- The ladder ends in a single-room Final, capped at `MAX_KINGS_VALLEY_ROUNDS = 14` rounds.
- **Size limit.** Past the cap the forced Final would exceed the room maximum, so generation refuses a field above `kingsValleyMaxEntrants(roomSize)`: 24 teams with rooms of 2–3 (2v2v2, 3v3v3), 32 with rooms of 3–4 (2v2v2v2), 40 with rooms of 4–5 (2v2v2v2v2), 60 players in FFA (see `docs/open-items.md`). The message names the limit and the 14 rounds, and suggests lowering "Advance to bracket" or adding a qualification phase. The re-plan after a reserve (`fitKingsValleyTail`) is not refused: it may still force a larger Final.
- **Accepted:** a removal can leave the current round (and rarely the next) with a room below the minimum; the organiser can fill it with a reserve.
- If a removal or reserve makes the survivor count differ from the plan, the rest of the ladder (Final included) is re-planned at the next advance (`fitKingsValleyTail`, `transitions.ts`); room movement uses `sequentialSeed`.
- **Unavailable for head-to-head formats** (`individual-1v1` and 2v2 to 5v5, Flex included): with rooms of 2 it cuts one unit per round and can't reach a Final within the cap above 16 units. Hidden in Setup and refused by generation; head-to-head Kings Valley tournaments generated earlier still play out.
- No Setup fields: the organiser tunes nothing.

### Waterfall bracket (`waterfall-bracket.ts`)
Shown in the app as **Custom bracket** ("Custom bracket (organiser-authored)", the CUSTOM BRACKET GRAPH panel); the code and these docs keep the name waterfall. Organiser-authored rank-band routing: a graph (a `ROUNDS:`/`ROUTES:` text format, edited through a click-to-route table in Setup, `src/features/admin/waterfall/`; the text itself is not shown in the app) in which each room's finishers split by rank into bands, each routed to any later round or eliminated. No byes or lucky losers; adding a reserve after the start is blocked when there is no pooling phase (before the start the schedule is redrawn for it, see Reserve admission; with a pooling phase, a late joiner is taken, see Reserve admission; a swap, which keeps the head count, is always allowed).

**Unrouted ranks are eliminated.** A room's `ROUTES:` line may leave ranks unrouted, the same as the `Qual` line: a rank no band covers is eliminated, and a room with no line at all is all-eliminated (`buildRoomDestinations`, `validateAndOrderWaterfallGraph`). Bands must still not overlap or name a rank outside the room, and explicit `->eliminated` bands still parse. A non-Final round must send at least one rank on (`Round "R2" sends nobody on -- route at least one rank to a later round, or make it the Final.`), so a forgotten route fails loudly; a forgotten route *into* a round is still caught by "a round's declared size must equal what routes into it". The editor, the saved graph text, generation and the live "Edit graph" all follow this one rule.

**The qualification table as a source (`Qual`).** After a `qual-table` pooling phase the graph's `ROUTES:` has a reserved `Qual` line that routes the final standings by rank like a room (`Qual: 1-16->R1, 17-24->R1C`): ranks 1–16 go to R1 (2 rooms of 8), 17–24 to R1C, everyone else is eliminated. `Qual` has no room letter, is never declared in `ROUNDS:` (a round named `Qual` is an error in every pooling phase), and a `Qual` line under any other pooling phase is refused at generation. Ranks the line doesn't route are eliminated, explicit `->eliminated` bands (also in the middle) are allowed, and the eliminated tail grows or shrinks with the head count (graph made at 26 routing 1–16: 10 out; at 31: 15). A graph with no `Qual` line (older saved graphs, the examples, copied settings) is read as `Qual: 1-<entry round total> -> <entry round>`; this is done in one place, `validateAndOrderWaterfallGraph(…, { standingsSource: true })`, and the editor's draft load uses the same reading.
- **"Advance to bracket" is derived**, not entered: `cfg.qualAdv` = the highest non-eliminated routed Qual rank (the form's value is ignored). Generation refuses a graph that routes more ranks than there are players (`The graph sends qualification rank 24 on, but only 20 players are registered …`); every declared round must be fed by `Qual` or another round, and a round's declared size must equal what routes into it (Qual ranks count).
- **Where the routing lives**: `standingsRoutes` on the last qualification round (index = standings rank − 1, absolute round index or `'eliminated'`, same convention as `waterfallRoutes`; length = highest routed rank). At the pooling exit (`advanceFromStandings`, `routeStandings`) the ordered table (cut-off tie orders applied) is routed from the standings, not from the top-`qualAdv` list, with no bye logic and no `tieredSeed`: the next round is drawn from its pool by `seedRoundFromPendingPool` like every other waterfall round, so a band sent to a multi-room round is spread over its rooms, ranks 1 and 2 in different rooms. A round fed by a lower band waits in `pendingBracketSeeds` until its turn; Rankings counts those units as alive.
- **Ties at every band boundary** need a tie-break: one cluster per distinct tied standing that straddles a boundary (a position whose destination differs from the next one's, eliminated counting as a destination). Its resolution fully orders it, so one cluster settles every boundary inside it. Key `qual-cutoff` for the cluster holding the last routed rank, `qual-band-<first rank>` for the others; the Bracket banner names the destinations ("Qualification tie between R1 and R1C").
- **Head-count shortfall**: if withdrawals during qualification leave fewer ranked units than the highest routed rank, Next Round is blocked with a message naming both numbers and pointing to "Edit graph" in the Admin tab. A late joiner during qualification does not trigger it: the waterfall's rounds are not re-planned (that would discard live graph edits) and the exit routes the standings by rank, so an extra unit only lengthens the eliminated tail (graph routing 24 at 37 registered plus a joiner: 14 out). A reserve can also replace an absentee one for one (swap).
- **Withdrawals once the bracket is running: the empty seat ripples down.** A waterfall round accepts fewer units than the graph declares, never more (more is `'malformed-waterfall-round'`, pointing to "Edit graph"). The same holds for the first round after a pooling exit without a Qual source (Swiss, group stage, warm-ups). A round that arrives short keeps its room count; the missing seats come out of the last rooms first, one per room in turn, so room sizes differ by at most 1 (2 rooms of 8 with 15 units: [8, 7]; with 14: [7, 7]; with 13: [7, 6]). `round.rooms`/`players` hold the real shape, `advTotal` counts the ranks the real rooms still send on, and `declaredRooms` keeps the graph's own room sizes while the round is short (`fitWaterfallRound`, `waterfall-bracket.ts`; never `distributeRooms`). Each room routes the ranks it has, so a gap in a bottom band that is `eliminated` is absorbed there and the next round arrives full; a gap in a band that routes on arrives one short in the destination, and so on down the graph. A room left with one unit routes its rank-1 band uncontested; a round nobody reaches, or a Final below 2, blocks ("too-few-units"). No next-in-line unit moves up. Seated and waiting withdrawals follow the same rule (removal also clears the unit from every pending pool, see Roster changes). The head-count shortfall above applies only to withdrawals during qualification.

- **Round names** may use letters, digits, spaces and dashes (up to 30 characters in the editor), starting and ending with a letter or digit: "Round 5 Upper", "Semi-B". Runs of spaces collapse to one and names match case-insensitively. `.`, `,`, `:`, `>` and `=` stay out (graph syntax), and `Qual` and `eliminated` are reserved (the editor refuses to rename a round to `Qual` too). One normaliser, `normalizeRoundLabel`, is applied wherever a label is read (ROUNDS, ROUTES addresses, every destination, rename), so the saved text round-trips.
- **Columns: rounds played together.** Every waterfall round belongs to a column, numbered from 1 (the qualification table is column 0). Rounds of one column are drawn together when the column is entered, scored in any order, and advanced by one Next Round; NOW sits on the column. `assignWaterfallColumns` (`waterfall-bracket.ts`) is the one function that numbers them, for validation, generation, the live edit and the editor.
  - **Automatic.** A round with no number of its own goes in column 1 when no waterfall round routes into it (the entry round, or a round fed only by `Qual`); otherwise in the column after the latest column of the rounds that route into it. The graph of tournament `1792226417678` (names spaced): [Round 5 Upper, Round 5 Lower], [Semi A, Round 6], [Round 7], [Semi-B], [Final]; the 32-player example: [5], [SemiA, 6B, 6C], [7A], [SemiB], [Final]. The Final is always alone in its column.
  - **Manual.** A ROUNDS line may end with `COLUMN <n>` (a whole number from 1), alone or with `FINAL`, in either order, each once, any letter case: `Semi A = 4 COLUMN 3`. It must be later than the column of every round that routes into it. Refused with a message, never moved: `Round "B" can't be in column 2: round "A" routes into it and is in column 2.` and `Column 4 has no round -- renumber the later columns or set a round to column 4.` (a gap between 1 and the last column). A numbered round is stored as such (`TournamentRound.waterfallColumnSet`), so a number set equal to the automatic column still pins the round against a later routing change.
  - **Play order** is the routing walk's order (today's order) stably sorted by column, so no existing graph changes its order and every column is contiguous in `state.rounds`. A live edit's reached rounds come first, as given.
  - **Stored at generation**: `TournamentRound.waterfallColumn` (and `waterfallColumnSet`) on every waterfall round, written by `waterfallBracketPhase`. A tournament generated before columns existed has none: it keeps playing one round at a time, and once its bracket has started a `COLUMN` number in a live edit is refused (`This tournament's rounds are played one at a time -- columns can't be set once its bracket has started.`). Before the bracket is entered, a save gives it columns. `round-columns.ts` holds the helpers every `curRound` comparison goes through (`currentColumn`, `isCurrentRound`, `isRoundReached`, `isRoundDone`, `nextColumnStart`, `previousColumnStart`, `roundInPlayOf`); `curRound` is the first round of the current column.
  - **Playing a column.** `enterNextWaterfallColumn` (`transitions.ts`) folds the arrivals into the pools, fits and draws every round of the next column (a round that doesn't fit leaves the state unchanged) and sets `curRound` to its first round. `advanceWaterfallBracket` routes every round of the current column, and a tie anywhere in it blocks Next Round (`hasPendingColumnTies`). Next Round does not require every room to be scored: the Bracket asks first, see `docs/views.md`. Withdrawals, swaps and defender changes for a unit apply to the round of the column that holds it (`roundInPlayOf`).
  - **Live edit with columns.** A reached round (the whole current column counts) keeps its column; a different `COLUMN` number on it is refused (`Round "A" has already started in column n.`); an unreached round can't use a started column (a manual number there: `Column n has already started -- choose column <first free> or later.`; an automatic one goes to the first column after the current one). If the edit changes the line-up of rounds of the current column, each is re-drawn while it has no score and a score in any of them refuses the edit, naming it; the saved-graph message names several re-drawn rounds joined with " + ".

The graph can still be corrected from the Admin tab while the tournament runs (`RunningWaterfallPanel.tsx`, `waterfall-live-edit.ts`): any round's routing can change, including already-played rounds, as long as replaying the edited routing reproduces every played round's recorded line-up exactly. A reached round (index ≤ the current round) keeps its label, room count, room size and Final flag -- only its routing can change; an unreached round can be freely resized, renamed, added or removed. If the current round's own line-up would change under the new routing, its rooms are re-drawn -- but only while it has no score yet; once a score is entered there, a line-up-changing edit is refused. With a Qual source, the Qual block is editable too: while qualification runs a save rewrites the last qualification round's `standingsRoutes` and `cfg.qualAdv` (the highest rank is checked against the units still in the roster, not the registered count), and once the bracket is entered the Qual routing is replayed into the pools and the first waterfall round's line-up is compared like any other reached round. `state.rounds` is the source of truth for what's actually running, not the `settings.waterfallGraph` snapshot (kept in sync on every save, so "Copy settings from a past tournament" still copies the corrected graph). No edit history -- a mistaken edit can only be corrected with another edit, not undone.

After a withdrawal the replay stays exact. At the Qual exit the final standings are stored on the last qualification round (`standingsExitOrder`: name and room share in rank order, tie resolutions applied); once the bracket is entered the replay routes that stored order, not a table recomputed from the current roster (whose ranks would shift when a unit is removed and pull the next rank into the gap). A tournament that exited before this was stored falls back to the recomputed table. Names in recorded line-ups that are no longer registered are ignored on both sides of the comparison and dropped from rebuilt pools; a unit that was swapped out maps to its replacement (`WithdrawnUnit.replacedBy`, followed through further swaps). The shape check of a reached round compares the declared room sizes (`declaredRooms ?? rooms`), so a round drawn short can still be saved with its graph shape, and its `advTotal` is recounted from the edited bands.

## Semis and Final
Both support 1–4 games, summed ("Semis format"/"Finals format"). Final scores live in `finalScores`; multi-game Semis use room score keys with a `-g{n}` segment. A plain (non-grand-final) Final can have games flagged anonymous (`round.anonymousGames`): those games are scored under `Finalist-N` placeholders, then "Connect & reveal" merges them onto the real finalists. Not available for grand-final race Finals or team formats.

## Lucky losers
Two cuts exist, by tournament. A tournament generated with `gamemodeConfig.cutVersion: 2` (generation writes it) makes the **fair cut** (`fair-cut.ts`). A tournament without the field keeps the **earlier cut** below, round by round: a running or archived tournament never changes rule.

**The fair cut** covers the rounds that otherwise cut by "so many per room plus lucky losers": single-elimination rounds and Semis (the bracket after a pooling phase included), double-elimination winners and losers rounds (shared Final and race alike; the units not kept drop to the losers bracket, or go out from a losers round), and head-to-head flex rounds (a room of 3 among rooms of 2). Kings Valley, waterfall, the pooling cut, the Final and the grand final keep their own rules. `usesFairCut(state, roundIndex)` is the one switch everything asks.
1. Byes and lone units (a room of one unit, or one with a single present unit, see "Absentees") advance. The round's target (`advTotal`) less those is S, the units to pick from the contested rooms' U present units; E = U − S go out. If E ≤ 0 everyone present advances and the next round is short. The absentees of the round go out first and are never part of U.
2. The contested rooms are grouped by the units present (`presentUnitCount`: the line-up less the absentees, so a room that lost a unit, or has absentees, is a smaller room). Each size loses its fair share of E, `floor(E × units of the size ÷ U)` in integers; what the shares leave over are the spare cuts.
3. **A room's winner always advances.** While S is at least the number of contested rooms, a size's share is capped so it keeps one unit per room, and the eliminations the cap removes become spare cuts. When S is below the room count some winner must go: the cap is off, each size still takes its fair share, and with several sizes the best S winners by relative score do not all advance.
4. Each size is ranked on its own: by place in the room (manual tie-breaks included, so a 3rd of 4 never passes a 2nd of 4), then by score relative to the room's average (score ÷ room total × scored units, 1.00 = average), then by the lower room number. It keeps its best units.
5. The spare cuts go one at a time to the size whose next in line (its last kept unit) ranks lowest by **percentile**: the share of all relative scores of rooms of that size, from round 1 up to and including the round being cut (pooling, warm-up and elimination rounds alike; only rooms of two or more units with a total above 0), that lie below the unit's, equal values counting half. Then lower relative score, then the later room. Eligible first: sizes with a fractional share that have not taken a spare cut and keep more than their room count; only if there are none, any size that keeps more than its room count.
6. The result per size: places 1…`direct` of every room always advance; the places beyond are lucky-chance places, whose advancing units are the lucky losers (★). Before the scores are in only a range is known ("2–3 spots, rooms of 4"): `fairCutShape` gives it from the line-ups alone, and with several room sizes it can differ per size.

A room whose scores are all zero offers no lucky loser (its places through still advance and its spot stays empty) and always loses a spare-cut contest. The cut is a pure function of the scores of the rounds up to the one being cut, so a re-draw after Previous or a score correction recomputes it from the corrected scores. With one room size it advances exactly the earlier cut's set. A fair-cut round's stored `advPerRoom` and `luckyCount` are still written (the line-up edit guard "Room would have N units but M advance" reads them, and is stricter than the cut needs) but the cut and its display ignore them. Why a fixed number per room was replaced: `HANDOFF_LOG.md`, "Fair elimination cut".

**The earlier cut** (a tournament without `cutVersion`, and a round the fair cut does not cover): in an elimination round with `luckyCount > 0`, each room's best non-advancing unit is a candidate, ranked by its score relative to its room's average (score ÷ room total × units in the room, 1.00 = average), so room size gives no head start in the ranking; the top `luckyCount` advance (`advancement.ts`). A room whose scores are all zero offers no candidate. Candidates with equal shares are split by room order. Every room advances the same number (`splitAdvancement`), which favours units in smaller rooms.

## Scoring (pooling standings only)
Selectable per tournament when `poolingPhase !== 'none'`; applies to qual-table, Swiss and Group Stage standings. Semis/Final use raw score sums.
- **Standard points** (default; `'fairpoints'` in persisted state): each counted round a unit earns the points of its place in its room, from a table that depends on `maxRoomSize`, the tournament's `gamemodeConfig.roomSize.max` (`standardPointsTable`, `scoring.ts`): 10, 9, …, 1 for 10; 10, 8, 7, …, 1 for 9; the community's 10, 8, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 for 8 (FFA at its largest); `maxRoomSize`, `maxRoomSize − 1`, …, 1 for 7 or fewer (2v2v2v2: 4, 3, 2, 1; 3v3v3: 3, 2, 1; head-to-head: win 2 / loss 1). **Scaled points in smaller rooms** (`scaledRoomPoints`, `scoring.ts`): the largest room (`maxRoomSize` units) reads the table as it is; a room of n < `maxRoomSize` units reads it at its share of opponents beaten: place p sits at table position 1 + (`maxRoomSize` − 1)(p − 1)/(n − 1), interpolated linearly and rounded to the nearest half point, half up. First place always earns the table's first value and last place its last, so beating nobody earns the same in every room (FFA at 8: a room of 7 earns 10, 7.5, 5.5, 4.5, 3.5, 2, 1; a room of 6, 10, 7, 5, 4, 2.5, 1; 2v2v2v2: a room of 3 earns 4, 2.5, 1). n is the units present in the room (its line-up less the absentees; `presentUnitCount`, `advancement.ts`). `roomPointsByPlace(state, roomSize)` (`advancement.ts`) is the one place that gives the points of each place in a room of that size; standings, the explanation text and the room exit pop-up all read it. **Versioned by `gamemodeConfig.pointsVersion`**: generation writes `pointsVersion: 2`, and only a tournament with it scores as described. A tournament without it (generated before the scaled rule) keeps the earlier rule: Standard points `maxRoomSize + 1 − place` (floored at 0) whatever the size of the room (last of a room of 3 in 2v2v2v2 earns 2, like 3rd of 4), a Custom table read place by place, no scaling. A running or archived tournament is never regenerated, so it keeps its points. Points are **summed** over the counted rounds, higher is better; a counted round the unit didn't play earns 0, so a late arrival is not compensated. A seat in a smaller room is worth extra points only under the earlier rule, but the seats are still spread evenly over the counted pooling rounds in both (`docs/seeding.md`, "Smaller-room seats"); a late joiner comes last for them in the round they enter.
- **Custom points table** (`'positional-points'`): an organiser table (`positionalPointsTable`, highest place first, e.g. `10,8,6,5,4,3,2,1`, must cover the largest room and never increase), summed like Standard points, higher is better. Under `pointsVersion` 2 only its first `maxRoomSize` entries are used (the explanation text and pop-ups list no more), and a smaller room reads them scaled like the Standard table (Custom 6, 4, 2, 0 at 4: a room of 3 earns 6, 3, 0). Its first value is also what a bye earns.
- **Names**: players only ever see "Points" / "Pts" (the totals are whole numbers unless a room shared points or a smaller room scaled to a half or a quarter; display rounds to one decimal). Setup names the two options "Standard points (FFA: 10, 8, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1; other formats: 1st = largest room size, −1 per place; smaller rooms scaled)" and "Custom points table" (`SCORING_SYSTEM_OPTION_LABELS`, `scoring.ts`); the Admin settings recap names Standard points that way for a tournament with `pointsVersion` and as "Standard points (1st = largest room size, −1 per place)" for one without (`scoringSystemLabel`). The persisted keys `'fairpoints'` / `'positional-points'` and the `totalFP` field keep their old names.
- **A bye counts as a win.** Each counted round, a unit with a bye (`state.byes[round]`, or `round.groupByes` in Group Stage) and a unit alone in a room (`isUncontestedRoom`: its opponent was removed, or an odd unit was left alone) earns 1st-place points under both systems, with no score and no share entry (`walkoverNames`, `advancement.ts`). Only once the round has begun (`roundHasBegun`: an earlier round, or the current one once the tournament has started), so a schedule that hasn't started has no points. A Group Stage bye goes to the unit's own group, and only when that group plays a match that round (a group whose round robin ran out is idle, not on a bye). Advancement is unaffected: a lone unit already advances.
- **Team scoring** (team formats, chosen in Setup, fixed at generation): *Sum of all members* adds the members' scores; *Save your Buddy* reads only the designated defender's score; *One score per team* stores one score per team under the individual key (`r{ri}-rm{room}-p{pos}`, `game{n}-{team}` in the Final). `scoredTeamSize()` (`scoring.ts`) is the single switch between per-member and per-unit score keys.
- **Shared points.** When units in a pooling room score the same, the admin can **share the points** instead of picking who ranks next (a "Share points" button on the room tie card, next to the "ranks next" buttons). Never automatic.
  - *Where:* every pooling round (`isPoolingRound`, `pooling.ts`): warm-ups, qualification table, Swiss and group stage (`isQual || isSwiss || isNoElim`, never Semis or the Final). Never an elimination round, Kings Valley, a waterfall graph round or a standings cut-off tie: a cut needs an order.
  - *Who:* every tied unit not yet ranked. The admin can rank some first and share the rest (A, B, C tied at 2nd: "A ranks next", then share: A 2nd, B and C =3rd).
  - *Value:* the exact average of the points the covered places earn (`pointsForSharedPlace`, `advancement.ts`), from Standard points or the Custom table: FFA with a largest room of 8, 2nd and 3rd shared in a room of 7, each earns (7.5 + 5.5) / 2 = 6.5 (a shared place in a room of 3 in 2v2v2v2 earns (2.5 + 1) / 2 = 1.75). Under the earlier rule (no `pointsVersion`) the places are read place by place. The average of the already rounded half points is not rounded again. Nothing is rounded in the state; displays show one decimal only when fractional (`formatStandingValue`). Head-to-head, a share is a draw: 1.5 each under Standard points, the average of the table's first two values under Custom.
  - *Places:* `orderRoomWithPlaces` (`scoring.ts`) gives ranked units their own places, then the sharing units the next place together (`sharedBy` > 1), the next unit the place after them all. A shared round result carries `shared: true`; the room card shows "=2", the Standings and Rankings "=2nd".
  - *Seeding:* shared units go to the tier of the shared place (`buildAdvancementTiers`), in every pooling round, warm-ups included (where no points exist: both are 2nd for the next draw).
  - *Stored* in the tie's list as `[...ranked, '#shared', ...shared]` (`SHARED_TIE_MARK`; a unit name cannot contain `#`); only `scoring.ts` reads it (`readTieResolution`; `tieResolutionList` returns the ranked part) and `shareTiedPoints` (`mutations.ts`) writes it, only for the exact set of unranked tied units. Resolved for Next Round (`isTieResolved`).
  - *Cleared* like a "ranks next" pick when a score edit changes the tie (`invalidateStaleTieResolutions`), and also when another unit joins the tied score: a share holds only while the shared and ranked names are exactly the tied units. The tie-break above still orders units on equal points.
- **Tie-breaker: room share relative to the room average.** Units with equal points are ordered by the higher average *relative room share* (`roomShare` on `TournamentStanding`): per round played in a room, the unit's score ÷ its room's total × the number of scored units in the room (1.00 = an average score in that room; an all-zero room gives everyone 1.00), averaged over those rounds; byes and lone-room wins add no entry. A raw room share (score ÷ room total) would favour small rooms (about 33% against 25% on average), so it is not used. It changes who qualifies and Swiss/qual reseeding order. Older saved standings snapshots have no `roomShare`; there, equal points count as equal. The legacy app had no such tie-breaker.
- **Per-round detail.** `TournamentStanding.rounds` (`StandingRoundResult`: `roundIndex`, `score`, `rank` (the place in the room), `points`, `bye`, and `shared` only when the place was shared) lists every counted round with a result, in round order; a bye has a null score and rank. A counted round without a result has no entry. The Standings table is built from it (`docs/views.md`).
- **Shared ranks and cut-off ties.** `rankStandings()` gives units a shared rank only when points **and** share are equal (`sameStanding()`; both compared within a tolerance of 1e-9, because a total that includes a shared place is a floating-point sum: `samePoints`, `sameRoomShare`). A cut-off tie cluster (the entries `sameStanding` with the boundary unit) exists, and needs the admin's pick in Bracket, only then. With a waterfall Qual source there is one such cluster per band boundary a tie straddles (see "Waterfall bracket"). A unit the admin placed never shares a rank with a neighbour (A, B, C tied, only A picked: A first, B and C share the next rank).
- **Displays count a current-round room only once it's fully scored.** `describeStandings()` (the single source for the Standings tab, Bracket column, follow banner and Rankings badge) computes standings with `liveRound: curRound`: a contested room of the current round counts only when every assigned unit has a score; past rounds count as scored. Real advancement is unchanged and still uses whatever is scored.

## Line-up corrections
The Admin "Correct current round" panel (`lineup-edit.ts`) fixes the round about to be played: **move** a unit to another room, **eliminate** a unit by hand, **reinstate** a unit eliminated in the previous round. Applied as one draft (`applyLineupEdit`), validated as a whole, so a head-to-head room of 2 is corrected by "eliminate X + reinstate Y" together.
- **Scope**: only the current round and only while it has no score (scores are keyed by room position; history stays as played). Pooling rounds (qual-table, Swiss, no-pooling warm-ups) of any bracket format: moves only, no elimination (use Remove), nobody reinstatable; fixed-draw pooling rounds and group stage are refused. Bracket rounds (elimination, Semis, Final): single elimination only.
- **Rules**: the round's `advTotal` and room count never change; no room is emptied; byes can be eliminated but nobody moves into or out of a bye; a room is refused only if the edit made it too small (under 2, or no more than `advPerRoom`) or too big (over `roomSize.max`), so existing odd states (a lone room after a removal, an over-cap reserve) don't block unrelated edits; in an elimination round the total must stay above `advTotal` (otherwise lower the count via the advancement edit); the Final keeps at least 2. Reinstating is limited to units present in the previous round's line-up, absent from this one and still in the roster (`computeRankings` derives elimination from "not in the next round's assignments", so rankings follow with no change).
- **Marker**: a unit placed, moved or reinstated this way carries `RoundAssignment.manual: true` and shows a ✋ badge in Bracket ("Set by organiser"). `roomHistory` for the round is rewound and re-recorded.

## Going back a round
"← Previous" only moves the view back one round; nothing is undone, so the later round stays drawn. "Next Round" from the earlier round then does one of two things (`drawnAheadStatus()`, `redraw.ts`), and the Bracket says which in a note above the action bar:
- **Re-draw**: when the next round is drawn and nothing in any drawn later round has a score, Next Round discards those draws and seeds the next round again from the current results. With nothing changed the result is identical; after a score correction it is exactly the draw a first advance from the corrected results would have made, in every format, double elimination and waterfall included. `discardDrawsAfter()` first undoes the discarded draws' `roomHistory` entries (not those of a fixed-draw round, which are folded in at generation), pooling-bye counts, byes and tie resolutions, and rebuilds the pending pools (`pendingBracketSeeds`) and lucky losers of every later round by replaying the recorded results of the rounds before the corrected one (`replayBracketPools()`, `bracket-replay.ts`): a double elimination or waterfall round sends each of its units to one destination, computed from its stored scores, byes and routing, and the qualification exit sends the table through the Qual source, so the pools are a function of those results. A unit that withdrew is left out of the replay and a swapped unit is its replacement, as in the live pools.
- **Steps forward unchanged** ("locked"): when any drawn later round has a score. Nothing is re-drawn or re-routed, so a correction in the earlier round does not change the later draw. It also steps forward when the recorded results do not explain the pools as they are (a removal made while viewing an earlier round, a hand-edited database): `replayExplainsPools()` compares what every later round holds (its pool, line-up and byes, less the units of the rounds about to be advanced from again) with the replay, and any difference locks.

Pending ties in the current round still block Next Round in both cases.

With columns, "round" means column. "← Previous" returns to the first round of the previous column; Next Round re-draws every column after it from that column's results when no round of a drawn later column has a score (`drawnAheadStatus`, `discardDrawsAfter`: the drawn column counts as one). A score in any round of a drawn later column locks it.

## Absentees
Registered units that don't show up are common. A tournament generated with `gamemodeConfig.absenceVersion: 1` (generation writes it, beside `pointsVersion` and `cutVersion`) asks about each score of 0; one without the field behaves as before (no question, no mark, no per-player weighting, vacant team slots included). `usesAbsences(state)` (`absences.ts`) is the one switch.
- **The question.** Each 0 typed in the column in play asks: did this unit (or member) play, or was it absent? A blank score never means absent, and the Final and the grand final never ask. The answer lives in `state.absences`, keyed by the score key the 0 is stored under (`r{ri}-rm{room}-p{pos}`, with `-g{n}` in a multi-game round and `-m{i}` for a member; no name, so a rename leaves it alone). An answer counts only while that key's score is still 0 (`isMarkedAbsent`): a changed score deletes it (`setRoundScore`), so a corrected 0 asks again; Back to draft, Clear roster, Previous's discarded draws, a swap (the outgoing seat) and a line-up edit delete the answers they touch. The bar on the Bracket asks (`docs/views.md`); an unanswered 0 blocks Next Round (`pending-absences`, "Answer every score of 0 before advancing.") in every mode but One score per team, where it shows without blocking and counts as played.
- **Who is absent from a room.** An individual or a Survival team: its key is marked. Sum of members and Save your Buddy: every member that exists (a vacant slot has no key) is marked. In a multi-game room (Semis) only a unit absent in every game; absent in some games it is present, with 0 for those games (ABS). In every team mode a team whose members are all absent is an absent team. Survival members can also be marked by hand from the ABSENT panel, for display only (scoring never reads it).
- **Present units are scored as the room they really were.** `scoreRoom` returns the present units only, so places, points (the table read at the present size, `presentUnitCount`), ties, lucky losers and the fair cut are worked out over them. The relative score of a sum-of-members room is per player in play: `(score ÷ players) ÷ (room total ÷ room players)`, where a team's players are its members that exist and were not absent (a vacant slot counts like an absent member); with equal players it is the same number as before. A tie at 0 is held back while any of its units still has a question, and an answer re-checks the room's tie decisions.
- **The absentee itself.** In a points round it gets a missed round: 0 points, and the round is left out of its tie-break average (the standings cell reads ABS, display only). A room with exactly one present unit is a walkover for it (a win, no score, no room share: `isWalkoverRoom`; `isUncontestedRoom` keeps its line-up meaning for the displays); a room with none gives nobody a win. In a round nobody is cut from (a warm-up, a qualification or Swiss round that is not the last) it stays in the draw. In an elimination round it never takes a spot the cut decides: the fair cut sends it to the units that go out (a winners round of a double elimination drops it to the losers bracket), a spare spot goes to a lucky loser, and when there are more absentees than eliminations everyone present advances and the next round is short. Kings Valley moves it down like a last place; a custom bracket (waterfall) ranks it after every present unit of its room and routes it by that rank (`roomFinishOrder`, `buildAdvancementTiers`). Head-to-head: one side absent is a walkover for the other, both absent gives no winner and the fair cut fills the spot from a lucky loser; in a group stage the present unit takes the walkover win and the absentee a missed round.
- **Smaller-room seats.** The present units of a room with absentees count a smaller-room seat, the absentees none (`docs/seeding.md`).
- **Rankings.** An eliminated absentee ranks last of its round, below every present unit eliminated in it, in any room (an explicit sort key after the Kings Valley room; organiser's decision 2026-10-29). Absentees of the same round follow the pooling standings and otherwise share a rank. Its where line ends "· absent". In STILL IN a unit absent from its room in the round in play reads the same.
- **Left to the organiser.** Removing or replacing an absentee stays manual (✕ Remove, ⇄ Swap). Going back is the way to answer a 0 of an earlier round (Previous).

## Roster changes during a tournament
Rename, swap, remove and reserve fill live in Rankings (`mutations.ts`). Removing a unit also drops it from every `pendingBracketSeeds` pool, so a unit that was only waiting for a later round is never drawn into it. Renaming a player (`renameIndividual`) changes the name everywhere it is stored (`renameInSchedule`, `rename-unit.ts`): the roster, every round's rooms (a fixed draw's unreached rounds included), byes, `pendingBracketSeeds`, groups, their fixtures and standings, room history (pairs re-sorted), lucky losers, tie decisions, Final scores, anonymous finalists and the confirmation mark, so the old name never comes back in a later round. A team keeps its id when renamed or when a member is, so team renames have no such reach to make. A swap is the one-for-one reserve substitution of an absentee, and works in every format, group stage and waterfall included: the replacement takes the outgoing unit's seat in the current round (a unit on a group bye included), or, for a unit already routed to a later round but not drawn into it yet (waiting in a `pendingBracketSeeds` pool), the slot it earned there (`tierRank`, `pct`); its future group fixtures are relabelled too. A swap of a unit marked absent keeps its seat's 0 and absent mark: the replacement missed that round too; typing its real score clears the mark (the ABSENT panel lists that absence under neither name). A swap is refused once a round that eliminated someone has been advanced (`swapWindowOpen`: `computeRankings` lists at least one eliminated unit with a rank; a unit shown eliminated while its round is still in play has no rank yet and does not close the window), in every format. A unit sent to a losers' bracket or waiting in a pool is not eliminated. A swap also works for a late joiner of the pooling round in play, who sits in no room yet (`replaceLateJoiner`): the substitute is the late joiner and is drawn in at the next advance. The duplicate-name check of a swap ignores the replacement's own reserve entry, so a reserve team can be swapped in (it still refuses a name used by another reserve or roster team). Adding a reserve as an extra unit is allowed until the pooling phase ends, with the restrictions of Reserve admission above; **before Confirm & Start a removal redraws the schedule too** (`removeUnitBeforeStart`, `pre-start-edits.ts`; the unit is still recorded as absent from the start), and when generation can't redraw for the smaller roster (below the format's minimum, a graph built for the exact count) the plain removal stands, Round 1 keeps a short room, and the organiser is told why; a removal is never refused. Reloading the roster in Setup after Generate redraws the schedule for the new roster, or drops it when it can't (the format changed, a count below the minimum, no stored settings): `reloadRosterBeforeStart`. The Setup form's "Confirm & Start" is disabled while round 1's seats are not exactly the roster (`scheduleMatchesRoster`); filling a vacant team slot, rename and remove are not covered by the swap window. Duplicate player and team names are refused everywhere a name is entered (team member names excepted). Names are Firebase keys, so a name containing `.`, `#`, `$`, `/`, `[`, `]` or a control character is refused everywhere one is typed (`findUnstorableNames`, `roster.ts`; team member names included): the roster load in Setup, rename, swap, walk-up and team edits in Rankings. The domain mutations (`renameIndividual`, `swapIndividual`, `swapTeam`, `updateTeam`, `fillTeamSlot`) return the state unchanged and `addWalkUpIndividual` returns `'unstorable-name'`; `updateTeam` only looks at names the update introduces, so a name stored earlier does not lock its team. Names already stored in older tournaments are not migrated. A removed or swapped-out unit that played at least one match shows as "DNF" in final standings (`state.withdrawnUnits`); one that never played (`hasPlayedAnyMatch`: a seat counts only with a score the unit was not absent for) appears only in the admin ABSENT panel. In a fixed-draw tournament, a removal or swap patches the unreached published rounds (an orphaned opponent gets a bye, rooms are renumbered).

**Player confirmation** (`confirmations.ts`, `state.confirmations`). The organiser marks players as confirmed in the organiser tools; viewers never see it. The mark is stored by name (a player's, a team member's or a reserve's, never a team id), so it belongs to the person: it survives reloading the roster in Setup, Back to draft, a reserve joining and a swap (the incoming name brings its own mark; the outgoing name keeps its mark). A rename moves it (`renameIndividual`, `renameTeamMember`); removing a unit leaves its mark in place, so the same name added back is still confirmed; Clear roster clears every mark. Nothing else reads it: generation, room distribution, advancement, Setup's counts and the roster counts ignore it. A score also confirms a player, worked out from the stored scores and never saved (`scoredPlayers`): an individual score above 0 in any game of a pooling, group, bracket or Semis room, or of a Final (an anonymous Final game counts for the finalist behind the alias), in individual formats and in team formats scored as "sum of members", where it counts for whoever holds that member slot now. A 0 does not count (an absentee is often typed as 0), and One score per team and Save your Buddy (defender only) confirm nobody by score. A player confirmed by a score cannot be unmarked; the manual mark, if any, is kept underneath and shows again if the score is corrected to 0. Back to draft keeps the manual marks but clears the scores, so the confirmations that came from scores go with them.
