# Tournament rules

Current rules of the app's tournament engine (`src/domain/tournament/`). Read when a task touches generation, advancement, scoring, formats or phases. Build history and reasoning: search `HANDOFF_LOG.md` for the entry names cited below.

## Game formats
`formats.ts`, `GAME_FORMATS`. A "unit" is a player (individual formats) or a team.

Two individual formats are written out, and the team formats are built from a rule: **players per team `t` (2–5) × teams per room `k` (2–5)**, with `t × k` at most 10, the game's hard cap on players in one room (`HARD_ROOM_PLAYER_CAP`, `formats.ts`, shared with `validateRoomCap`). The key is `team-` plus `k` copies of `t` joined by `v` (`team-2v2v2`), and the label is the same without `team-`; `teamFormatKey()` and `teamsPerRoomOptions()` hold the rule, and Setup offers every valid combination with none ranked above another. The five formats that existed before the rule (`ffa-individual`, `team-2v2v2v2`, `team-3v3v3`, `team-3v3`, `individual-1v1`) keep their keys and traits exactly, so saved tournaments need no migration.

| Format | Unit | Room size | Odd-count strategies (default) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ffa-individual` | player | 6–8, ideal 8 | — |
| `individual-1v1` | player | 2 (head-to-head) | `none`/`bye` (`bye`) |
| `team-2v2` | team of 2 | 2 (head-to-head) | `none`/`bye`/`flex` (`bye`) |
| `team-2v2v2` | team of 2 | 2–3, ideal 3 | — |
| `team-2v2v2v2` | team of 2 | 3–4, ideal 4 | — |
| `team-2v2v2v2v2` | team of 2 | 4–5, ideal 5 | — |
| `team-3v3` | team of 3 | 2 (head-to-head) | `none`/`bye`/`flex` (`bye`) |
| `team-3v3v3` | team of 3 | 2–3, ideal 3 | — |
| `team-4v4` | team of 4 | 2 (head-to-head) | `none`/`bye` (`bye`) |
| `team-5v5` | team of 5 | 2 (head-to-head) | `none`/`bye` (`bye`) |

With several teams per room (`k ≥ 3`) rooms hold from `k − 1` to `k` teams (`defaultRoomSize`); with two teams the format is head-to-head (`idealRoomSize: 2`, default strategy `bye`). A definition also carries `teamsPerRoom` (team formats) and `shortLabel` ("FFA", "1v1", "4v4"), which the Tournaments list shows; an unknown key shows as itself there and generation refuses it with "This game format is not available yet." (`GameFormatKey` is ``'ffa-individual' | 'individual-1v1' | `team-${string}` ``).

FFA counts that can't fill rooms of 6–8 are seated in smaller rooms by design (9 → [5,4], 17 → [6,6,5]). Planned advancement counts (what a schedule chooses itself) are always seatable; counts the organiser enters are seated as they come.

An unset odd-count strategy means the format's default. "Flex" lets a head-to-head team format seat a 3-team room (room size 2–3); it is offered only where three teams still fit the cap (`3 × t ≤ 10`), so for `team-2v2` and `team-3v3`, not for `team-4v4` and `team-5v5`. A Semis/Final size override is checked against the cap too (a 5v5 Final of 3 teams is 15 players and is refused). "None" refuses to generate on a count that can't be split evenly.

Room counts and sizes for any headcount come from `distributeRooms()` (`room-distribution.ts`); a round's advancement target splits into `advPerRoom` per room plus `luckyCount` lucky-loser slots (`splitAdvancement`). When removals leave a round with a different headcount than planned, `fitRoundToPool` reshapes it at advance time.

## Pooling phases (`poolingPhase`)
- `none` — 2 no-elimination warm-up rounds.
- `qual-table` — qualification rounds (3 by default) feeding one cumulative standings table; the top `qualAdv` advance. With a waterfall bracket the table is a source of the graph and `qualAdv` is derived from it (see "Waterfall bracket").
- `swiss` — 3–7 fold-paired rounds, same standings engine as `qual-table`. Every Swiss round is `floor(n/2)` rooms of 2 plus `n % 2` byes, whatever the odd-count strategy.
- `group-stage` — round-robin groups (single or double), top N per group advance. Pairings are known at generation (`round.matches`/`round.groupByes`).

Rules:
- Swiss and Group Stage are **head-to-head only** (`individual-1v1` and the two-teams team formats, 2v2 to 5v5), gated in Setup and in `generateTournament()`: fold-pairing and round-robin always pair exactly 2 units.
- **Non-counting rounds** (qual-table/Swiss only): a tournament can mark its leading N rounds `excludeFromStandings`; they're played and reseed normally but `computeQualificationStandings` skips them.
- **Reserve admission** (`addReserveUnit`, `mutations.ts`): a reserve or walk-up can join until the pooling phase ends (`reserveOpen`). **Before Confirm & Start** nobody but admins has seen the draw, so a joiner is part of it: with no schedule generated yet the reserve just joins the roster (`confirmedCount` follows); with one, the schedule is redrawn for the new head count from the settings it was generated with (`regenerateForRoster`, `pre-start-roster.ts`; `state.settings`, not the Setup form's current values) and the joiner plays Round 1. Generation is the only judge then: the group-stage, fixed-draw, pooling-less-waterfall and `'strict-room'` refusals below apply only after the start, and when generation refuses the new count the add is refused with its message (`'generation'`). A tournament generated before settings were stored keeps the rules below. A **brand-new team** can be added in a team format (`addWalkUpTeam`: one `TeamName, Player1, Player2` line; refused with no member, with a name already used in `players` or `reserves`, or with an unstorable name) and joins like any reserve. **After the start:** while a pooling round is in play (warm-up, qualification or Swiss) the round stays as drawn: the joiner sits it out (0 points if it counts) and is drawn in with everyone else when the round advances, so no over-full room is ever created, in any bracket format. At that advance a no-elimination pooling round takes in every roster unit not already advancing (`withUndrawnRosterUnits`, `transitions.ts`), except at the last qualifying round, where the standings decide and the joiner, ranked last with 0 points, is normally cut. From 2 missed counted rounds (`missedCountedRounds`: qualification/Swiss rounds up to and including the one in play, not the leading non-counting ones) the admin confirms first (`confirm-late-join`). The odd-count strategy "None" refuses a joiner who would leave a count that is not a multiple of the room size (`'strict-room'`). After the start, group stage (`'group-stage'`), a fixed draw (`'fixed-draw'`) and a waterfall without a pooling phase (`'waterfall-bracket'`) still refuse. In a tournament with no pooling phase the window ends with its first bracket round: a reserve added then is seated in that round, in the least-full room (the over-cap confirm, `'strict-room'` under "None"); during a warm-up the joiner is drawn into the first bracket round. **First-round seeding:** a joiner's level is unknown, so in the round they enter they are seeded as an average unit (see `docs/seeding.md`): in the middle tier of the round they missed in the room-based reseed, at the end of the fold in Swiss so they meet a middle-ranked unit, and never given the bye (a bye counts as a win; if only joiners are left to take one, the usual rule applies). From their second round on their own results seed them, and their standings keep counting the missed rounds as 0. In a waterfall, a join or removal during qualification also reshapes the pooling rounds not yet reached for the new head count at once (`refitPoolingRound`, `pooling.ts`), so the Bracket shows the room sizes the next draw will use; the graph rounds and the last qualifying round's `standingsRoutes` are left alone.
- **Individual reserves and member swaps** (team formats, `member-swaps.ts`): open **at any time**, before Confirm & Start and in every round, whatever `reserveOpen` says, because they change no schedule and no draw (a team's rooms and scores are stored by seat and slot). An individual reserve can be added and removed; a name already used anywhere in the roster (players, teams and their members, reserve teams, individual reserves), in either case, is refused. One fills a vacant slot (`fillTeamSlot`, the same name check) or replaces a member (`swapTeamMember`, ⇄ on the member): **the member who goes out becomes an individual reserve** and can be swapped back. Scores stay with the slot, so a past room shows the team's current members, as after a rename (there is no per-round member history). Confirmations stay with each person's name. The defender stays on the slot (the newcomer takes over the role; 🛡 moves it). In One score per team a hand absent mark on that slot in the round in play is cleared (the newcomer is not who was absent); answers to scored 0s stay. Whole-team swaps (`swapTeam`) keep their window, until the first elimination, and a team can't join a group stage.
- **The reserve bench.** A walk-up the draw refuses for being a group stage, a fixed draw or a custom bracket without a pooling phase (`'group-stage'`, `'fixed-draw'`, `'waterfall-bracket'`) is not dropped while the swap window is open (`swapWindowOpen`, before the first elimination): `addWalkUpIndividual` / `addWalkUpTeam` return `benched` with the newcomer in `reserves`, ready for ⇄ Swap (`swapIndividual`, `swapTeam`). The other refusals (`closed`, duplicate name, unstorable name, no members, room cap) are unchanged, and a bench entry's own add button (`addReserveUnit`) keeps its refusal. The swap-window check in `benchIfSwappable` is a safety net: `reserveOpen` closes with the first elimination round and a unit out in the round in play has no rank yet, so the two windows end together and `closed` is answered first.
- **Group stage exit** (`cfg.groupExit`, `group-exit.ts`; absent means `'per-group'`, so every stored tournament keeps its rule). `'per-group'` sends Qualifiers per group from every group. `'even-split'` and `'fair-cut'` make "Advance to bracket" (`qualAdv`) the **total** A and ignore Qualifiers per group. G is the number of groups. **Even split**: every group sends `floor(A / G)`, and the `A mod G` leftover spots go to the best of the next-placed teams (place `floor(A / G) + 1`) across the groups, one per group at most. **Fair cut**: `fairCutShape(groupSizes, A)` with the groups as its rooms: each group size loses its fair share of the eliminations, places `1…direct` of each group always go through, the chance places are decided across groups, and a spare cut falls on the size whose next in line ranks lowest. With a Custom bracket's Groups line the total (or the qualifiers per group) is derived from the line, not entered (see "Waterfall bracket"). Worked examples (the tests of `group-exit.test.ts`):

  | Groups | A | Even split | Fair cut |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 5, 4, 4 | 8 | top 2 of each, plus the best 2 of the three 3rds | top 3 of the group of 5 and top 2 of each group of 4, plus 1 spot: the 5's 4th or the better 3rd of the 4s |
  | 5, 5, 4 | 8 | top 2 of each, plus the best 2 of the three 3rds | top 2 of each, plus 2 spots: at least one 3rd of a group of 5, then the other 5's 3rd or the group of 4's 3rd (the same teams as the even split) |
  | 5, 4, 4 | 6 | top 2 of each | top 2 of the group of 5 and the winner of each group of 4, plus the best 2 of the 5's 3rd and the two runners-up |
  | 5, 5, 4 | 6 | top 2 of each | top 2 of each group of 5 and the group of 4's winner, plus 1 spot: the better 5's 3rd or the group of 4's runner-up |

  **Selection** (`groupExitSelection`): the direct places of every group first, in tiers (each tier by `compareStandings`); then the chance spots one at a time: every size class offers its next in line (its lowest place still open across its groups, the best of those by points per match, then relative score, then the organiser's order), the spot goes to the best of the offers, a class takes at most its most spots and its fewest stay reachable. Then the **refill**: while fewer units go through than the exit's entry count (groups × Qualifiers per group, or the total), a group left too short by removals (fewer units than its places) gives the spot to another group, one at a time, in every mode (per group included): each group offers the unit at its lowest open place, and the lowest place wins, then points per match, then relative score, then the organiser's order; a unit without a counted match is a candidate, last by that order. It stops when the count is reached or no group has a unit left (a total that covers every remaining unit sends them all). A refill spot decided between level units raises the usual `group-exit-cutoff` tie. Teams of different groups are compared on points per match, then relative score: groups of different sizes are compared on points per match everywhere else in the group stage, so the exit does the same (`fair-cut.ts` ranks a size's units by the percentile of their relative score within that size; this is the organiser's decision, not a difference to fix). Within a size a lower place always goes before a higher one; `fairCutShape` produces no size with two chance places today, so that rule and the fewest-spots rule are defensive and covered through an injected shape. **Ties**: inside a group at its direct count, the existing `group-cutoff-<label>` tie; at the chance spots, a unit level (points per match and relative score) with the unit a spot went to that ends up unplaced raises one `group-exit-cutoff` tie across groups (`chanceTies`; ties of several spots merge into one cluster, so with two at once the organiser picks one more than strictly needed). The organiser's list orders level units ("X goes through"). Readers: `computeGroupStageAdvancement`, `getAllTies`, the standings tables' cut, `resolveBracketEntryCount`, the pooling `seedTotal`, the live advancement edit. **Units without a counted match** (every opponent absent or removed) stay in their group's table, last, and take a place there like any unit: a group reduced to one unit sends it (one of the total's spots), and two such units level at a cut-off raise the usual cut-off tie (its value reads 0). Until some unit of the stage has a result nothing is ranked, so the cut lines are those of a stage not yet started. **Generation refuses** Qualifiers per group (per group) below 1 or at or above the smallest real group, which can be one smaller than the Setup's group size (`groupSizesOf`: 15 teams in groups of 4 make 4, 4, 4, 3, so 3 per group is refused, naming "4 groups (4, 4, 4, 3)" and the most that works), and a total below the number of groups (each winner goes through), naming "3 groups (5, 4, 4)", and then by mode (`groupExitRefusal`): an **even split** sends the same number from every group, so each group loses at least one and at most groups × (smallest group − 1) go through (9 from 5/4/4 or 4/4/4; the message points to the fair cut); a **fair cut** keeps only each group's winner safe, so a group may send all its teams (10 of 12 from 4/4/4: the top 3 of each group and the best 4th, 3/3/4) and only a total of every team is refused (at most teams − 1). The Setup help line shows the same message. The bracket-size checks then run on the total.
- **Draw publication** (qual-table/Swiss, Setup "Draw publication"): `adaptive` (default) reseeds from live results; `fixed` publishes the whole pooling schedule at generation — see `docs/seeding.md`.

## Bracket phases (`scheduleLogic`)
- **`single-elimination`** — room-based cuts to Semis and a Final. **Semis size**: two rooms of the ideal size (FFA 16), or everyone entering the bracket when that is fewer, down to two rooms of the format's *smallest* room (FFA 12: 14 → [7, 7], 13 → [7, 6], 12 → [6, 6]; 2v2v2v2 6; 3v3v3 4; head-to-head 4); the Final stays at its room size (FFA 8), so 4 per Semis room. When everyone entering already fits the Semis there is no elimination round (`singleEliminationBracketPhase`); explicit targets still add rounds. A "Semis size override" wins over the default. Below the floor generation **refuses** (it never raises the request): registered count under the floor, "Advance to bracket" under the floor (`Advance to bracket (11) is below the minimum of 12 for single elimination (Semis needs 2 rooms of at least 6).`), and a Group Stage that sends fewer units than the floor to the bracket (`Group stage sends 3 players to the bracket; …`). A request above the confirmed count still clamps down to everyone.
- **"Advance to bracket" below a bracket format's floor is refused in every format**, naming the floor: single elimination 2 × smallest room, Kings Valley and shared-Final double elimination 2 × ideal room (FFA 16), race double elimination 4; waterfall has no floor here (its graph decides). Group Stage ignores the field (its entry is groups × qualifiers per group), so a leftover hidden value there never refuses generation.
- Single elimination and the shared-Final variant below accept optional explicit round targets and per-round seeding overrides (Setup → "Advanced round overrides"). The targets (and "Advance to bracket", `qualAdv`) stay editable live, in the Admin "Correct current round" panel (`live-advancement-edit.ts`):
  - **While pooling runs** (qual-table, Swiss, no-pooling warm-ups; single elimination and shared-Final): the bracket is rebuilt from scratch like Setup does, pooling rounds untouched (fixed draws included). In single elimination the Semis size follows the new "Advance to bracket" (`followsEntryCount`) unless the tournament has a Semis size override. The targets list is fully editable, length included (blank = automatic); with seeding overrides its length must stay the same (they are index-aligned). `qualAdv` (qual-table/Swiss only) is refused, not clamped, outside the format minimum and the roster count. A stale standings-cutoff tie resolution (`qual-cutoff`) is dropped when `qualAdv` changes.
  - **Single elimination mid-bracket**: values only, from the current round (until advanced, scores allowed) to the last round before Semis; the number of rounds never changes. The current round keeps its rooms; later rounds are rebuilt (`eliminationRound`, existing seeding overrides kept) and Semis is refitted to the last value. Tie resolutions stay (they order equal scores, wherever the cut moves).
  - Shared-Final double elimination is editable only until its bracket starts; race double elimination, Kings Valley, waterfall and group stage are not editable. A saved edit writes the settings snapshot (`settings.qualAdv`, `settings.eliminationRoundTargets`, `gamemodeConfig.explicitTargets`) so "Copy settings" copies what was played; an automatic curve edited mid-bracket becomes an explicit list.
- **`double-elimination`** — head-to-head race bracket (winners bracket, losers bracket, grand final). Not available with Flex.
- **`double-elimination-shared-final`** — multi-unit double elimination feeding one shared Final. A unit dropped from the winners bracket reaches a losers-bracket round within at most 2 winners-bracket rounds (`MAX_WB_ROUNDS_BEFORE_LB`, `double-elimination.ts`).
  - **Losers-bracket round sizes are always seatable.** When a losers round's count (previous survivors plus pending drops) can't be seated, three levers are tried in order: (1) the previous losers round's survivor target moves; (2) the round is deferred, up to the 2-round limit above; (3) the feeding winners round's target moves, keeping the curve strictly decreasing and the next winners round seatable. Each takes the nearest value that works, the smaller cut on a tie; organiser-set targets (`eliminationRoundTargets`) are never changed. A few small FFA configurations (17–19 entrants with 3–4 LB qualifiers) can't be fixed by any lever and fall back to smaller rooms by design.
- **`kings-valley`** — see below.
- **`waterfall-bracket`** — see below.

### Kings Valley (`kings-valley.ts`)
A room ladder: rooms are ranked top to bottom. Each round, a room's top finishers promote to the room above, its bottom finishers demote to the room below, the rest stay; the lowest room with a real match eliminates its bottom finishers instead of demoting them. Room 1's promote band stays in room 1.
- Promotions are `max(1, round(size × 0.25))` per room (`KINGS_VALLEY_MOVE_FRACTION`). The bottom room's cut is the one nearest half of it (`KINGS_VALLEY_ELIMINATION_FRACTION = 0.5`) that leaves a **seatable** total (every room within the format's minimum and maximum), the smaller cut on a tie. Demotions are then sized top-down so the moves land exactly on the next round's rooms (promotions are only lowered when a room couldn't supply its demotions). One function, `kingsValleyRoundMoves` (bands plus next room sizes), drives generation, the real advance and the Bracket exit chips, so chips, placeholders and the real seating agree. Room sizes are the `distributeRooms()` layout of the survivors; a room below the format's minimum is never planned.
- A room with fewer than 2 units (only after a removal) **holds**: nobody in it moves or is cut, and the cut comes from the lowest room that still has a real match (the plain per-room maths, no seatable-cut solving).
- The ladder ends in a single-room Final, capped at `MAX_KINGS_VALLEY_ROUNDS = 14` rounds.
- **Size limit.** Past the cap the forced Final would exceed the room maximum, so generation refuses a field above `kingsValleyMaxEntrants(roomSize)`: 24 teams with rooms of 2–3 (2v2v2, 3v3v3), 32 with rooms of 3–4 (2v2v2v2), 40 with rooms of 4–5 (2v2v2v2v2), 60 players in FFA (see `docs/open-items.md`). The message names the limit and the 14 rounds, and suggests lowering "Advance to bracket" or adding a qualification phase. The re-plan after a reserve (`fitKingsValleyTail`) is not refused: it may still force a larger Final.
- **Accepted:** a removal can leave the current round (and rarely the next) with a room below the minimum; the organiser can fill it with a reserve.
- If a removal or reserve makes the survivor count differ from the plan, the rest of the ladder (Final included) is re-planned at the next advance (`fitKingsValleyTail`, `transitions.ts`); room movement uses `sequentialSeed`.
- **Unavailable for head-to-head formats** (`individual-1v1` and 2v2 to 5v5, Flex included): with rooms of 2 it cuts one unit per round and can't reach a Final within the cap above 16 units. Hidden in Setup and refused by generation; head-to-head Kings Valley tournaments generated earlier still play out.
- No Setup fields: the organiser tunes nothing.

### Waterfall bracket (`waterfall-bracket.ts`)
Shown in the app as **Custom bracket** ("Custom bracket (organiser-authored)", the CUSTOM BRACKET GRAPH panel); the code and these docs keep the name waterfall. Organiser-authored rank-band routing: a graph (a `ROUNDS:`/`ROUTES:` text format, edited through a click-to-route table in Setup, `src/features/admin/waterfall/`; the text itself is not shown in the app) in which each room's finishers split by rank into bands, each routed to any later round or eliminated. No byes or lucky losers; adding a reserve after the start is blocked when there is no pooling phase (before the start the schedule is redrawn for it, see Reserve admission; with a pooling phase, a late joiner is taken, see Reserve admission; a swap, which keeps the head count, is always allowed).

**Unrouted ranks are eliminated.** A room's `ROUTES:` line may leave ranks unrouted, the same as the `Qual` line: a rank no band covers is eliminated, and a room with no line at all is all-eliminated (`buildRoomDestinations`, `validateAndOrderWaterfallGraph`). Bands must still not overlap or name a rank outside the room, and explicit `->eliminated` bands still parse. A non-Final round must send at least one rank on (`Round "R2" sends nobody on -- route at least one rank to a later round, or make it the Final.`), so a forgotten route fails loudly; a forgotten route *into* a round is still caught by "a round's declared size must equal what routes into it". The editor, the saved graph text, generation and the live "Edit graph" all follow this one rule.

**Ranking matches (`RANKING`).** A ROUNDS line may end with `RANKING` (any order with `COLUMN` and a match format, once, any letter case; not with `FINAL`): a placement match for units who are all going out, such as the losers of two Lower rooms meeting for 7th and 8th (`Round 5 - Ranking = 2 RANKING`, fed by `Round 4 - Lower.A: 2->Round 5 - Ranking` and the same from room B). It is the one non-Final round allowed to send nobody on, and it must: a `RANKING` round with a route out is refused (`Round "X" is marked RANKING but routes ranks on -- a ranking match sends nobody on.`), and so is `FINAL` with `RANKING` on one line. Inside its column a `RANKING` round is placed before the column's other rounds (`validateAndOrderWaterfallGraph`, after the column sort), so in Rankings, which orders eliminated units by the round they went out in, its units rank below everyone else the column eliminates, and inside the room by place. In the organiser's 12-team graph (`waterfall-ranking.test.ts`): 3rd the Semi-Final B loser, 4th the Round 6 - Lower loser, 5th-6th Round 6 - Ranking, 7th-8th Round 5 - Ranking, then the group stage. The mark is stored on the round (`waterfallRanking`) and kept by the Setup graph editor and the live "Edit graph". In the editor it is the round's "Ranking match" checkbox, beside "This is the Final" (`setWaterfallRanking`, `waterfall-draft.ts`): ticking it sets every rank of the round out (after a confirm when some were routed) and clears the Final mark; ticking "This is the Final" clears it. A ranking match in the Final's column gets no numbered rank, like every elimination of that column.

**The qualification table as a source (`Qual`).** After a `qual-table` pooling phase the graph's `ROUTES:` has a reserved `Qual` line that routes the final standings by rank like a room (`Qual: 1-16->R1, 17-24->R1C`): ranks 1–16 go to R1 (2 rooms of 8), 17–24 to R1C, everyone else is eliminated. `Qual` has no room letter, is never declared in `ROUNDS:` (a round named `Qual` is an error in every pooling phase), and a `Qual` line under any other pooling phase is refused at generation. Ranks the line doesn't route are eliminated, explicit `->eliminated` bands (also in the middle) are allowed, and the eliminated tail grows or shrinks with the head count (graph made at 26 routing 1–16: 10 out; at 31: 15). A graph with no `Qual` line (older saved graphs, the examples, copied settings) is read as `Qual: 1-<entry round total> -> <entry round>`; this is done in one place, `validateAndOrderWaterfallGraph(…, { standingsSource: true })`, and the editor's draft load uses the same reading.
- **"Advance to bracket" is derived**, not entered: `cfg.qualAdv` = the highest non-eliminated routed Qual rank (the form's value is ignored). Generation refuses a graph that routes more ranks than there are players (`The graph sends qualification rank 24 on, but only 20 players are registered …`); every declared round must be fed by `Qual` or another round, and a round's declared size must equal what routes into it (Qual ranks count).
- **Where the routing lives**: `standingsRoutes` on the last qualification round (index = standings rank − 1, absolute round index or `'eliminated'`, same convention as `waterfallRoutes`; length = highest routed rank). At the pooling exit (`advanceFromStandings`, `routeStandings`) the ordered table (cut-off tie orders applied) is routed from the standings, not from the top-`qualAdv` list, with no bye logic and no `tieredSeed`: the next round is drawn from its pool by `seedRoundFromPendingPool`. A round fed only by the Qual source (no waterfall round routes into it) is drawn by the standings: the snake over its rooms, best against weakest (ranks 1-16 into 2 rooms of 8 are 1,4,5,8,9,12,13,16 and 2,3,6,7,10,11,14,15), then a trade with a near neighbour in the order for two units that have met (`seedPoolByStanding`); a band of one room has nothing to spread. A round fed by the Qual source and by another round keeps the wave fill of every other waterfall round. A round fed by a lower band waits in `pendingBracketSeeds` until its turn; Rankings counts those units as alive.
- **Ties at every band boundary** need a tie-break: one cluster per distinct tied standing that straddles a boundary (a position whose destination differs from the next one's, eliminated counting as a destination). Its resolution fully orders it, so one cluster settles every boundary inside it. Key `qual-cutoff` for the cluster holding the last routed rank, `qual-band-<first rank>` for the others; the Bracket banner names the destinations ("Qualification tie between R1 and R1C").
- **Head-count shortfall**: if withdrawals during qualification leave fewer ranked units than the highest routed rank, Next Round is blocked with a message naming both numbers and pointing to "Edit graph" in the Admin tab. A late joiner during qualification does not trigger it: the waterfall's rounds are not re-planned (that would discard live graph edits) and the exit routes the standings by rank, so an extra unit only lengthens the eliminated tail (graph routing 24 at 37 registered plus a joiner: 14 out). A reserve can also replace an absentee one for one (swap).
- **Withdrawals once the bracket is running: the empty seat ripples down.** A waterfall round accepts fewer units than the graph declares, never more (more is `'malformed-waterfall-round'`, pointing to "Edit graph"). The same holds for the first round after a pooling exit without a Qual source (Swiss, group stage, warm-ups). A round that arrives short keeps its room count; the missing seats come out of the last rooms first, one per room in turn, so room sizes differ by at most 1 (2 rooms of 8 with 15 units: [8, 7]; with 14: [7, 7]; with 13: [7, 6]). `round.rooms`/`players` hold the real shape, `advTotal` counts the ranks the real rooms still send on, and `declaredRooms` keeps the graph's own room sizes while the round is short (`fitWaterfallRound`, `waterfall-bracket.ts`; never `distributeRooms`). Each room routes the ranks it has, so a gap in a bottom band that is `eliminated` is absorbed there and the next round arrives full; a gap in a band that routes on arrives one short in the destination, and so on down the graph. A room left with one unit routes its rank-1 band uncontested; a round nobody reaches, or a Final below 2, blocks ("too-few-units"). No next-in-line unit moves up. Seated and waiting withdrawals follow the same rule (removal also clears the unit from every pending pool, see Roster changes). The head-count shortfall above applies only to withdrawals during qualification. Absentees make a destination arrive short the same way ("Absentees": an absentee whose seat does not lead where the room's last place goes is eliminated, so the band it would have filled arrives one short).

**The group stage as a source (`Groups`).** After a `group-stage` pooling phase the graph's `ROUTES:` has a reserved `Groups` line, the same grammar and checks as `Qual`, that routes the **group exit order** by rank (`Groups: 1-4->R1, 5-8->R1C`). The ranks are positions in `groupExitSelection(…).advancing`: every group's winner first (best first), then the runners-up, and so on, then the chance and refill picks. 3 groups of 4, fair cut 8: ranks 1-3 are the winners, 4-6 the runners-up, 7-8 the two best 3rds. `Groups` is refused under a qualification table and `Qual` under a group stage, each message naming the right one; "Groups" is a reserved round name; a graph with no `Groups` line is read as `Groups: 1-<entry round total> -> <entry round>` (one place, `validateAndOrderWaterfallGraph(…, { standingsSource: true, standingsKind: 'groups' })`).
- **"Advance to bracket" is derived** from the line: the exit total A = the highest routed rank. Even split and fair cut take A as their total. Per group, qualifiers per group = A / the number of groups; a total the groups cannot share is refused (`The Groups line sends 8 on, but 3 groups can't each send the same number -- route 6 or 9, or choose Even split or Fair cut.`). One function decides it for generation and for the live edit, `groupsLineExit` (`group-exit.ts`), and the exit's own refusals (`groupExitRefusal`, `groupExitRefusalForSizes` for the groups as they stand) still apply. Setup shows the implied value read only, and `groupExitRankLabels(groupSizes, mode, total)` gives each rank a plain label in the editor ("Group winners", "Runners-up", "3rd, group of 5", "Best 3rds").
- **Where the routing lives**: `standingsRoutes` on the last group round, same convention as `Qual`; at the exit `advanceFromStandings` routes the exit order (`standingsExitOf`) through `routeStandings` and stores it as `standingsExitOrder`, so the re-draw replay, the Bracket's expected column and Rankings follow. A stored group-stage tournament without `standingsRoutes` keeps the one entry round.
- **The draw of a Groups-fed round**: a round fed only by the pooling exit (`isFedOnlyByStandings`) is drawn by the snake over its pool in exit order, then the group swap (`avoidSameGroupInFirstBracketRound` through `seedPoolByStanding`), not the met-pairs pass. A round also fed by another round keeps the wave fill.
- **Ties at a band boundary**: one cluster per run of consecutive exit ranks level across groups (same group place, `sameStanding`) that straddles a boundary, key `group-band-<first rank>`; the boundary to eliminated stays `group-exit-cutoff`. The Bracket banner reads "Groups tie between R1 and R1C", and the Standings tab marks the units of a band tie (pending until picked).

- **Round names** may use letters, digits, spaces and dashes (up to 30 characters in the editor), starting and ending with a letter or digit: "Round 5 Upper", "Semi-B". Runs of spaces collapse to one and names match case-insensitively. `.`, `,`, `:`, `>` and `=` stay out (graph syntax), and `Qual`, `Groups` and `eliminated` are reserved (the editor refuses to rename a round to `Qual` or `Groups` too). One normaliser, `normalizeRoundLabel`, is applied wherever a label is read (ROUNDS, ROUTES addresses, every destination, rename), so the saved text round-trips.
- **Default round names** of a Custom bracket. A default name is exactly "Round <n>" (after `normalizeRoundLabel`, any letter case: a name typed like that is the numbering either way). The Setup names rounds on from the pooling rounds played before the bracket: `roundsBeforeBracket(setup, units)` (`pooling-rounds.ts`) is the qualification rounds (3, or the override), the Swiss rounds (from the head count, or the override), the group stage's rounds (the longest group, twice in a double round robin; from the roster) or, with no pooling phase, its 2 warm-up rounds; generation builds the same rounds (`startRoundNum - 1`, pinned by a test). The entry round of a blank graph is "Round <that + 1>", an added round "Round <first + the rounds that are not the Final>", bumped until free: "Round 4" after 3 qualification rounds, "Round 3" with no pooling phase. The Setup setting `waterfallNumberedFrom` records the first number the graph's defaults were given for; it is set once, by the first save of a graph that has a default name (an edit never re-marks it, so a graph edited while a group stage has no roster keeps it), and is snapshotted and copied with the graph. When the first number moves (the rounds override, the pooling phase, a group stage's or a Swiss's round count with the roster, a settings copy) every default name shifts by the difference, all at once (`defaultNameShift`: "Round 4" becomes "Round 5" while "Round 5" becomes "Round 6"; typed names such as "Round 4 - Upper" and "Final" stay), the Qual or Groups line follows, and the round notes, game notes and pictures move with the names. The shift is skipped whole when two defaults would land on one name ("Round 5" and "Round 05") or a number would fall below 1; the number still moves on, so it is not tried again. A graph saved before this ("R1" names) has no number and is never renumbered. A group stage's numbering waits for the roster. A started tournament never renumbers, and a round added in the running Admin takes the tournament's own numbering.
- **Columns: rounds played together.** Every waterfall round belongs to a column, numbered from 1 (the qualification table is column 0). Rounds of one column are drawn together when the column is entered, scored in any order, and advanced by one Next Round; NOW sits on the column. (That is column play. A tournament generated with `dependencyPlayVersion` plays by rounds, not columns: see "Dependency play" below.) `assignWaterfallColumns` (`waterfall-bracket.ts`) is the one function that numbers them, for validation, generation, the live edit and the editor.
  - **Automatic.** A round with no number of its own goes in column 1 when no waterfall round routes into it (the entry round, or a round fed only by `Qual`); otherwise in the column after the latest column of the rounds that route into it. The graph of tournament `1792226417678` (names spaced): [Round 5 Upper, Round 5 Lower], [Semi A, Round 6], [Round 7], [Semi-B], [Final]; the 32-player example: [5], [SemiA, 6B, 6C], [7A], [SemiB], [Final]. The Final is always alone in its column.
  - **Manual.** A ROUNDS line may end with `COLUMN <n>` (a whole number from 1), alone or with `FINAL`, in either order, each once, any letter case: `Semi A = 4 COLUMN 3`. It must be later than the column of every round that routes into it. Refused with a message, never moved: `Round "B" can't be in column 2: round "A" routes into it and is in column 2.` and `Column 4 has no round -- renumber the later columns or set a round to column 4.` (a gap between 1 and the last column). A numbered round is stored as such (`TournamentRound.waterfallColumnSet`), so a number set equal to the automatic column still pins the round against a later routing change.
  - **Play order** is the routing walk's order (today's order) stably sorted by column, so no existing graph changes its order and every column is contiguous in `state.rounds`. A live edit's reached rounds come first, as given.
  - **Stored at generation**: `TournamentRound.waterfallColumn` (and `waterfallColumnSet`) on every waterfall round, written by `waterfallBracketPhase`. A tournament generated before columns existed has none: it keeps playing one round at a time, and once its bracket has started a `COLUMN` number in a live edit is refused (`This tournament's rounds are played one at a time -- columns can't be set once its bracket has started.`). Before the bracket is entered, a save gives it columns. `round-columns.ts` holds the helpers every `curRound` comparison goes through (`currentColumn`, `isCurrentRound`, `isRoundReached`, `isRoundDone`, `nextColumnStart`, `previousColumnStart`, `roundInPlayOf`); `curRound` is the first round of the current column.
  - **Playing a column.** `enterNextWaterfallColumn` (`transitions.ts`) folds the arrivals into the pools, fits and draws every round of the next column (a round that doesn't fit leaves the state unchanged) and sets `curRound` to its first round. `advanceWaterfallBracket` routes every round of the current column, and a tie anywhere in it blocks Next Round (`hasPendingColumnTies`). Next Round does not require every room to be scored: the Bracket asks first, see `docs/views.md`. Withdrawals, swaps and defender changes for a unit apply to the round of the column that holds it (`roundInPlayOf`).
- **Dependency play** (`dependencyPlayVersion` 1; `waterfall-steps.ts`, `round-columns.ts`). Generation writes the version on every new tournament; it only has an effect in a Custom bracket, and a stored tournament without it plays by columns, as above, from start to finish. A round is drawn as soon as every round that routes into it is sent on, and several rounds can be in play at once. The columns stay: the Bracket's layout, the graph's validation and a hold (below).
  - **The model.** `state.waterfallSteps` holds one `{ sent, drew, reopened? }` per press of Next Round; the database drops an empty list, so `waterfallSteps ?? []` and `drew ?? []` are read. `usesDependencyPlay(state)` is true when the version is set and the round at `curRound` is a waterfall round; in the pooling rounds it is false and every helper reads as column play. **Reached**: the entry column (always drawn on entry), every round a step drew and everything before the bracket. **Done**: before the bracket, or in the `sent` of a step that is not reopened. **In play**: reached and not done (`roundsInPlay`; `undrawnRounds` is the rest). Reached rounds are not a prefix of `state.rounds`: on [U1, L1] [U2, L2] [U3, L3], L3 can be in play while U2 is not drawn, so nothing may read "index up to something" as reached. `curRound` stays, as the lowest round in play; the raw readers left are pooling-only or refuse a waterfall (`lineup-edit`, `live-advancement-edit`, `group-swap`, `fixed-draws`, `standings-display`, `rebuildFutureRounds`).
  - **Next Round** (`nextRoundPlan`, which `advanceDependencyPlay` follows, so the Bracket's panel says exactly what the press does). It sends on every finished round in play with no question (every contested room scored, a decided Best of counting as scored, no tie and no blocking absence question of that round; never the Final), routes them into the pools and draws every round whose feeders are then all sent on. When no round is finished it sends every round in play as it stands, after the Bracket's confirmation; a pending tie or a blocking absence question of those rounds blocks that press. The press is all or nothing: a round that cannot be drawn returns the input state unchanged. Rounds of a reopened step count as done again, with no routing and no draw, and the press clears their flag. A round's draw reads only the units of its own pool, sorted by place and relative score (`tieredBracketSeed`), and their history, so it does not depend on the order of the presses: every order gives the draws column play gives. A tie, an absence question or a score of 0 in any round in play is seen by the readers (`getAllColumnTies`, `zeroScoresInPlay`, `unscoredRooms` loop over `roundsInPlay`).
  - **Holds.** A round with a manual `COLUMN n` is drawn only once its feeders and every round of the earlier columns are sent on, so numbering every round gives exactly column play, the organiser's fallback per graph. The Final waits for every other round to be sent on, which keeps `finalComplete`, Finish and the Rankings reading as they do.
  - **Previous** (`goBack`, `canGoBack`). It takes the last step that is not reopened. When it is the last step, no round it drew has a score and the replay of the rounds still done explains the pools (`replayExplainsUndo`: a correction made while a step was reopened can leave the scores no longer explaining where the units are), the press is undone: its drawn rounds lose their draw (`discardRoundDraws`, the per-round body of `discardDrawsAfter`) and the pools of the undrawn rounds are rebuilt by replaying the rounds still done (`replayBracketPools` with a set of rounds); the state is what it was before the press, except that a pool may list its units in another order, which no draw reads. Otherwise the step is reopened: its sent rounds are in play and editable again, its drawn rounds stay as drawn, and a correction in the reopened rounds does not change that draw. With every step reopened, or none, Previous goes to the last pooling round (the button is hidden in a Custom bracket with no pooling phase); Next Round there is the drawn-ahead case of "Going back a round": a re-draw clears the steps, a lock keeps them and clears the flags.
  - **Rankings** (`rankingPhases`). Past is done, in play is `roundsInPlay`, and a unit waiting in a pool is listed with the round it waits for. A round of a reopened step is read by where its units were sent, so a correction there cannot put a unit out of a round it has moved on from. The order stays by round order, so it does not depend on finishing time.
  - **Live edit with columns.** A reached round (the whole current column counts) keeps its column; a different `COLUMN` number on it is refused (`Round "A" has already started in column n.`); an unreached round can't use a started column (a manual number there: `Column n has already started -- choose column <first free> or later.`; an automatic one goes to the first column after the current one). If the edit changes the line-up of rounds of the current column, each is re-drawn while it has no score and a score in any of them refuses the edit, naming it; the saved-graph message names several re-drawn rounds joined with " + ". **In dependency play** the reached rounds must be a prefix of the bracket's rounds and no step reopened, or the save is refused with a message naming the round to play first (`Can't save yet: "R5R" has not been drawn, but "SemiA" has. ...`) or the reopened rounds; a column with any drawn round counts whole (its labels and column stay); the replay repeats the rounds done (`replayBracketPools` with a set of rounds); a drawn round can't gain a feeder that is not done (`Can't save: "SemiA" is not done yet, so it can't feed "R7A", which is already drawn.`); a save made back in the pooling rounds clears the steps with the draws it drops.
- **Match format per round.** A ROUNDS line may also end with one match format, in any order with `FINAL` and `COLUMN`, each token at most once, any letter case: `BO<n>` (best of n, n odd from 3 to 9: `Semi A = 2 BO3`) or `GAMES <n>` (n games summed, 1 to 4: `R1 = 4x2 GAMES 2`); both on one line is refused (`ROUNDS line for "X" has both BO3 and GAMES 2 -- choose one match format.`). No token: one game, and the Final plays the "Finals format" (`finalsGames`). Stored as `TournamentRound.bestOf` (set only on a Best-of round) and `numGames` (the most games a room can need; a summed round with none plays one). In the Setup editor it is the **Match** select of the round ("1 game" / "Finals format (N games)", "2–4 games, summed", "Best of 3 / 5 / 7"; a format typed into the text outside that list shows as one more option). `waterfallDraftFromRounds` reads it back from the drawn rounds, so a live edit round-trips.
- **Best of N** (`match-format.ts`, the one place that knows the rule). First to W = (N + 1) / 2 game wins. A game is won by the unit with the highest score in it (its game score, whatever the team scoring rule); a top score shared by two or more units is won by nobody. A game counts once every unit of the room has a score for it, games are read in order and counting stops at the deciding game: a game typed after it counts for nothing (neither wins nor total) and its box is off, and correcting an earlier game re-opens the room (all derived, nothing stored). In a room of three or more units games go on until one unit reaches W; the places below are by wins, then by the total score of the counted games, and units still level get the room tie card with key `r{ri}-rm{room}-s{wins}-t{total}` (a summed round's key is unchanged). `numGames` = k × (W − 1) + 1 for the room with the most units k (Best of 3: 3 head to head, 4 for three teams, 5 for four; a round drawn short keeps it). A room that uses every game with no winner (only with drawn games) is complete and ordered by wins, then total. A Best-of room is complete when decided or when every game is used; a unit has played when any game of its room has its own score. The Final works the same way: complete when decided, ranked by wins then total, shown as wins ("Final · 2 games won"). Absence questions stop at the deciding game. A Best-of Final refuses anonymous games. Every round of the other formats can be a Best of N too ("Match formats" below).
- **Changing a match format live** (`match-format-change.ts`, `waterfall-live-edit.ts`). The round in play (the whole current column) and rounds not reached yet can change format; a round of a finished column is refused (`Can't save: "X" is finished, so its match format can't change.`). On the round in play the typed scores are read again under the new format. Going from one game to several moves every score key `r{ri}-rm{room}-p{pos}[-m{k}]` to `…-p{pos}-g1[-m{k}]` and back (back only with nothing above game 1 typed); absence answers keyed by a moved key move with it (the hand mark of a member of a team with one score is round-wide and stays), and every room's tie decisions are checked again. A format with fewer games than the highest game typed in any room is refused: `Can't save: "X" already has scores for game 3, so it can't be played over 2 games.` The Final keeps its `game{n}-…` keys.

The graph can still be corrected from the Admin tab while the tournament runs (`RunningWaterfallPanel.tsx`, `waterfall-live-edit.ts`): any round's routing can change, including already-played rounds, as long as replaying the edited routing reproduces every played round's recorded line-up exactly. A reached round (index ≤ the current round) keeps its label, room count, room size and Final flag -- only its routing can change; an unreached round can be freely resized, renamed, added or removed. If the current round's own line-up would change under the new routing, its rooms are re-drawn -- but only while it has no score yet; once a score is entered there, a line-up-changing edit is refused. With a Qual source, the Qual block is editable too: while qualification runs a save rewrites the last qualification round's `standingsRoutes` and `cfg.qualAdv` (the highest rank is checked against the units still in the roster, not the registered count), and once the bracket is entered the Qual routing is replayed into the pools and the first waterfall round's line-up is compared like any other reached round. The Groups block is editable the same way: while the group stage runs a save rewrites the last group round's `standingsRoutes` and the derived exit (`cfg.qualAdv`, or `cfg.qualifiersPerGroup` per group), checked by `groupsLineExit` against the real group sizes and the units still in the roster, and drops the band picks (and, if the total changed, the cut-off picks) the new routing makes stale; once the bracket is entered the exit settings stay, the line can only route ranks that left the groups (`only 8 left the groups, so the Groups line can't route rank 9`), and the replay compare decides the rest. `state.rounds` is the source of truth for what's actually running, not the `settings.waterfallGraph` snapshot (kept in sync on every save, so "Copy settings from a past tournament" still copies the corrected graph). No edit history -- a mistaken edit can only be corrected with another edit, not undone.

After a withdrawal the replay stays exact. At the Qual exit the final standings are stored on the last qualification round (`standingsExitOrder`: name and room share in rank order, tie resolutions applied); once the bracket is entered the replay routes that stored order, not a table recomputed from the current roster (whose ranks would shift when a unit is removed and pull the next rank into the gap). A tournament that exited before this was stored falls back to the recomputed table. Names in recorded line-ups that are no longer registered are ignored on both sides of the comparison and dropped from rebuilt pools; a unit that was swapped out maps to its replacement (`WithdrawnUnit.replacedBy`, followed through further swaps). The shape check of a reached round compares the declared room sizes (`declaredRooms ?? rooms`), so a round drawn short can still be saved with its graph shape, and its `advTotal` is recounted from the edited bands.

## Match formats
Every round plays **1–4 games summed** or a **Best of N** (3, 5 or 7 in Setup; the rule is "Best of N" under "Waterfall bracket"). A round's format is its **override**, else the format of its **stage**, else one game. One function decides it, `applyMatchFormats` (`round-match-formats.ts`), which `buildTournamentProgression` runs over every schedule, so generation, a rebuilt future (`rebuildFutureRounds`), the live advancement edit and the Kings Valley tail re-fit give a round the same answer; `stageMatchFields(games, bestOf, largestRoom)` (`match-format.ts`) works the round's `numGames` and `bestOf` out (a Best of N's game count is for the round's largest room; `fitRoundToPool` and `refitPoolingRound` work it out again for a re-fitted room). A round with the default one game and no override carries neither field; the Semis and the Final always carry `numGames`.
- **Stages** (`matchStage(round)`). *Pooling*: the warm-ups, qualification table, Swiss and group stage rounds (`isPoolingRound`), select "Pooling rounds". *Bracket*: the rounds before the Semis, "Elimination rounds" (single elimination), "Winners rounds" (both double eliminations), "Ladder rounds" (Kings Valley). *Losers*: the losers rounds of both double eliminations, "Losers rounds". *Semis* (single elimination, "Semis format") and *Final* ("Finals format"; every format but the race Grand Final). No stage: the graph rounds of a Custom bracket, whose format is their ROUNDS line's ("Waterfall bracket", "Match format per round"; a graph Final with no token of its own follows the Finals format), and the race Grand Final, which keeps its own win targets.
- **Settings.** `poolingGames`/`poolingBestOf`, `bracketGames`/`bracketBestOf`, `losersGames`/`losersBestOf`, `semisGames`/`semisBestOf`, `finalsGames`/`finalsBestOf` (strings, `''` = summed; one select per stage writes both) and `matchOverrides`, kept in the settings snapshot (so "Copy settings" copies them) and in `gamemodeConfig` (numbers, each written only when not the default one game / summed; the Semis and Finals games always). `matchFormatConfig(form, schedule)` is the one conversion; a setting whose select the schedule does not show is ignored (bracket and losers formats have none in a Custom bracket, losers only in double elimination, the Semis only in single elimination, the Finals not in the race). A tournament generated before they existed has none and plays one game where it did.
- **Overrides** (a round that plays a format of its own) are stored by the round's name, the one its settings note is keyed by (`roundNoteNames`, "Round 3", "WB Round 2", "Semis", "Final"). In the Setup form and the snapshot they are text, one line per round, "Round 2: BO3" or "Round 4: GAMES 2" (a name, a colon, a ROUNDS-line token read by `parseMatchFormatToken`; every Setup field is text, so setup sync and its validation stay as they are; `parseMatchOverrides`, `matchOverridesText`); in `gamemodeConfig.matchOverrides` they are a map from name to token. A line that is no format is dropped; an override whose name no round has now (a redraw renamed rounds) does nothing and is listed as an orphan.
- **Before the start**, a change of any match select or override formats a drawn, unstarted schedule at once (`withFormMatchFormats`: the config, the snapshot and the rounds, a graph Final included); a Semis or Final change used to wait for Generate. A started tournament is untouched. A redraw reads the form again.
- **Across rooms.** Inside a Best-of room the places are by wins, then total. Wherever units of different rooms are compared, the relative score is taken on the **total of the counted games**, not on the wins ("Lucky losers"), so a Best-of Semis needs no head to head and may have lucky losers; a summed round is unchanged.
- **Live change** (the running Admin's "Correct current round" → "Match format", `stage-match-edit.ts`). One select per stage the schedule has (prefilled from `gamemodeConfig`; locked once every round of the stage is finished) and one row per round not finished yet, the round in play included (first option "Stage format (X)"; a graph round of a Custom bracket and the race Grand Final are not listed). One Save, all or nothing: a stage change applies to its unfinished rounds that have no override, a row writes (or clears) that round's override; every round that plays another format goes through `changeRoundMatchFormat` (score keys move to `-g1` and back, a format with fewer games than the highest game typed is refused naming the round, absence answers move, tie resolutions are re-checked) in round order, and the first refusal stops the save with nothing written. A finished round never changes. The stage formats and the overrides are written to `gamemodeConfig` and the settings snapshot too, so "Copy settings" copies what was played and a rebuilt round (the Kings Valley tail, a live advancement edit) keeps them; a Best-of Final with anonymous games is refused.
Final scores live in `finalScores`; multi-game Semis use room score keys with a `-g{n}` segment. A plain (non-grand-final) Final can have games flagged anonymous (`round.anonymousGames`): those games are scored under `Finalist-N` placeholders, then "Connect & reveal" merges them onto the real finalists. Not available for grand-final race Finals or team formats.

## Lucky losers
Two cuts exist, by tournament. A tournament generated with `gamemodeConfig.cutVersion: 2` (generation writes it) makes the **fair cut** (`fair-cut.ts`). A tournament without the field keeps the **earlier cut** below, round by round: a running or archived tournament never changes rule.

**The fair cut** covers the rounds that otherwise cut by "so many per room plus lucky losers": single-elimination rounds and Semis (the bracket after a pooling phase included), double-elimination winners and losers rounds (shared Final and race alike; the units not kept drop to the losers bracket, or go out from a losers round), and head-to-head flex rounds (a room of 3 among rooms of 2). Kings Valley, waterfall, the pooling cut, the Final and the grand final keep their own rules. `usesFairCut(state, roundIndex)` is the one switch everything asks.
1. Byes and lone units (a room of one unit, or one with a single present unit, see "Absentees") advance. The round's target (`advTotal`) less those is S, the units to pick from the contested rooms' U present units; E = U − S go out. If E ≤ 0 everyone present advances and the next round is short. The absentees of the round go out first and are never part of U.
2. The contested rooms are grouped by the units present (`presentUnitCount`: the line-up less the absentees, so a room that lost a unit, or has absentees, is a smaller room). Each size loses its fair share of E, `floor(E × units of the size ÷ U)` in integers; what the shares leave over are the spare cuts.
3. **A room's winner always advances.** While S is at least the number of contested rooms, a size's share is capped so it keeps one unit per room, and the eliminations the cap removes become spare cuts. When S is below the room count some winner must go: the cap is off, each size still takes its fair share, and with several sizes the best S winners by relative score do not all advance.
4. Each size is ranked on its own: by place in the room (manual tie-breaks included, so a 3rd of 4 never passes a 2nd of 4), then by score relative to the room's average (score ÷ room total × scored units, 1.00 = average), then by the lower room number. It keeps its best units.
5. The spare cuts go one at a time to the size whose next in line (its last kept unit) ranks lowest by **percentile**: the share of all relative scores of rooms of that size, from round 1 up to and including the round being cut (pooling, warm-up and elimination rounds alike; only rooms of two or more units with a total above 0), that lie below the unit's, equal values counting half. Then lower relative score, then the later room. Eligible first: sizes with a fractional share that have not taken a spare cut and keep more than their room count; only if there are none, any size that keeps more than its room count.
6. The result per size: places 1…`direct` of every room always advance; the places beyond are lucky-chance places, whose advancing units are the lucky losers (★). Before the scores are in only a range is known ("2–3 spots, rooms of 4"): `fairCutShape` gives it from the line-ups alone, and with several room sizes it can differ per size.

A room whose scores are all zero offers no lucky loser (its places through still advance and its spot stays empty) and always loses a spare-cut contest. The cut is a pure function of the scores of the rounds up to the one being cut, so a re-draw after Previous or a score correction recomputes it from the corrected scores. With one room size it advances exactly the earlier cut's set. A fair-cut round's stored `advPerRoom` and `luckyCount` are still written (the line-up edit guard "Room would have N units but M advance" reads them, and is stricter than the cut needs) but the cut and its display ignore them. Why a fixed number per room was replaced: `HANDOFF_LOG.md`, "Fair elimination cut".

**The earlier cut** (a tournament without `cutVersion`, and a round the fair cut does not cover): in an elimination round with `luckyCount > 0`, each room's best non-advancing unit is a candidate, ranked by its score relative to its room's average (score ÷ room total × units in the room, 1.00 = average), so room size gives no head start in the ranking; the top `luckyCount` advance (`advancement.ts`). A room whose scores are all zero offers no candidate. Candidates with equal shares are split by room order. Every room advances the same number (`splitAdvancement`), which favours units in smaller rooms.

**A Best-of round across rooms.** Inside a room a Best-of round ranks by wins, then total ("Best of N"). Wherever units of different rooms are compared, `relativeRoomShare` reads `comparableScore` (`scoring.ts`): the total of the counted games when the entry has a `tiebreak` (a Best-of entry's `score` is its wins), the score otherwise. That covers the lucky-loser candidates and the all-zero test, the fair cut's relative score, its size pools and percentiles and `roomScored`, the standings tie-break (`roomShare`), the seeding tiers' in-tier order and the Rankings share of units out in the same round. A room whose games were all drawn with scores above 0 counts as scored. A summed round is unchanged. Two things to know when reading a Best-of Semis: with a Semis size override that leaves no room a direct place (1v1 at 31, override 6: the Final holds 2, so `advPerRoom` is 0), the room winners themselves race as lucky losers; and under the fair cut with several room sizes the lucky loser is decided by size group and then percentile, not by the best relative total overall (the earlier cut does pick the best).

## Scoring (pooling standings only)
Selectable per tournament when `poolingPhase !== 'none'`; applies to qual-table, Swiss and Group Stage standings. Semis/Final use raw score sums. A Group Stage tournament generated with `gamemodeConfig.groupAverageVersion: 2` (generation writes it, beside `pointsVersion` and `cutVersion`) ranks its groups per match played, see "Group stage standings" below; `usesGroupAverages(state)` (`group-standings.ts`) is the one switch.
- **Standard points** (default; `'fairpoints'` in persisted state): each counted round a unit earns the points of its place in its room, from a table that depends on `maxRoomSize`, the tournament's `gamemodeConfig.roomSize.max` (`standardPointsTable`, `scoring.ts`): 10, 9, …, 1 for 10; 10, 8, 7, …, 1 for 9; the community's 10, 8, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 for 8 (FFA at its largest); `maxRoomSize`, `maxRoomSize − 1`, …, 1 for 7 or fewer (2v2v2v2: 4, 3, 2, 1; 3v3v3: 3, 2, 1; head-to-head: win 2 / loss 1). **Scaled points in smaller rooms** (`scaledRoomPoints`, `scoring.ts`): the largest room (`maxRoomSize` units) reads the table as it is; a room of n < `maxRoomSize` units reads it at its share of opponents beaten: place p sits at table position 1 + (`maxRoomSize` − 1)(p − 1)/(n − 1), interpolated linearly and rounded to the nearest half point, half up. First place always earns the table's first value and last place its last, so beating nobody earns the same in every room (FFA at 8: a room of 7 earns 10, 7.5, 5.5, 4.5, 3.5, 2, 1; a room of 6, 10, 7, 5, 4, 2.5, 1; 2v2v2v2: a room of 3 earns 4, 2.5, 1). n is the units present in the room (its line-up less the absentees; `presentUnitCount`, `advancement.ts`). `roomPointsByPlace(state, roomSize)` (`advancement.ts`) is the one place that gives the points of each place in a room of that size; standings, the explanation text and the room exit pop-up all read it. **Versioned by `gamemodeConfig.pointsVersion`**: generation writes `pointsVersion: 2`, and only a tournament with it scores as described. A tournament without it (generated before the scaled rule) keeps the earlier rule: Standard points `maxRoomSize + 1 − place` (floored at 0) whatever the size of the room (last of a room of 3 in 2v2v2v2 earns 2, like 3rd of 4), a Custom table read place by place, no scaling. A running or archived tournament is never regenerated, so it keeps its points. Points are **summed** over the counted rounds, higher is better; a counted round the unit didn't play earns 0, so a late arrival is not compensated. A seat in a smaller room is worth extra points only under the earlier rule, but the seats are still spread evenly over the counted pooling rounds in both (`docs/seeding.md`, "Smaller-room seats"); a late joiner comes last for them in the round they enter.
- **Match results** (`'match-results'`, a third system, for a Group Stage (always head to head) and for Swiss or a qualification table whose rooms are always two units: a head-to-head format without Flex, `matchResultsAllowed`; `match-results.ts`). Per game of a group match: a **Win** (won by more than the tie margin), a **Tie Win** (won by the margin or less), a **Tie Loss** (lost by the margin or less), a **Loss**, or a **Tie** (equal scores, worth the average of Tie Win and Tie Loss: 1.5 by default). The gap is the difference of the two units' raw scores of that game, as stored for the team scoring rule (sum of members, the defender's for Save your Buddy, the one score for One score per team); the margin stays the same when a team plays one player short. Several games summed: each game has its own result and the round's points are their sum (2 games: up to 6). **Best of 3** is read as one match, 2–0 a Win / Loss, 2–1 a Tie Win / Tie Loss (equal wins, only with drawn games: a Tie); a room that is not fully scored gives no points yet. Settings, kept in `gamemodeConfig` (`matchResultPoints`, `tieMargin`), the settings snapshot and Setup (two text fields, "Match result points" and "Tie margin (points)"): the four points, default `3, 2, 1, 0`, numbers of 0 or more that never increase from Win to Loss; and the margin, a whole number of points of 0 or more, whose default comes from one small table by game format (`defaultTieMargin`, today only `team-4v4: 100` for target score 1400; any other format starts empty and generation refuses an empty margin, asking for one; choosing Match results or changing the game format fills an empty margin, or one that still holds the previous format's default). Generation refuses match results anywhere else (FFA, 2v2v2v2 and 3v3v3 rooms, a Flex odd-count strategy, no pooling phase; Setup offers the option only where generation accepts it), and a pooling round that is a Best of 5 or 7 (stage setting or per-round override); the Pooling rounds select lists Best of 5 and 7 as "not with match results". Each round outcome records the result codes of its games (`StandingRoundResult.results`: W, TW, TL, L, T); the places and room share are worked out as under the other systems. In Swiss and the qualification table the room's points come from the same overlay (`playedOutcomes`, none until the room is fully scored), are summed over the counted rounds (no per-match average: that is the group stage's own rule), and a bye or a room left with one present unit counts as a Win for each game of the round (`matchResultsWin`, the same value the group stage gives its walkover win). A pooling room never raises a room tie card under match results (equal scores are a Tie, 1.5 each, and the place inside the room feeds no points: room share reads the score, head to head the points); the group cut-off ties stay. Rules 1–5 of "Group stage standings" apply on top.
- **Custom points table** (`'positional-points'`): an organiser table (`positionalPointsTable`, highest place first, e.g. `10,8,6,5,4,3,2,1`, must cover the largest room and never increase), summed like Standard points, higher is better. Under `pointsVersion` 2 only its first `maxRoomSize` entries are used (the explanation text and pop-ups list no more), and a smaller room reads them scaled like the Standard table (Custom 6, 4, 2, 0 at 4: a room of 3 earns 6, 3, 0). Its first value is also what a bye earns.
- **Names**: players only ever see "Points" / "Pts" (the totals are whole numbers unless a room shared points or a smaller room scaled to a half or a quarter; display rounds to one decimal). Setup names the two options "Standard points (FFA: 10, 8, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1; other formats: 1st = largest room size, −1 per place; smaller rooms scaled)" and "Custom points table" (`SCORING_SYSTEM_OPTION_LABELS`, `scoring.ts`); the Admin settings recap names Standard points that way for a tournament with `pointsVersion` and as "Standard points (1st = largest room size, −1 per place)" for one without (`scoringSystemLabel`). The persisted keys `'fairpoints'` / `'positional-points'` and the `totalFP` field keep their old names.
- **Group stage standings (per match; `groupAverageVersion` 2, `group-standings.ts`).** In the group-stage rounds only (qualification tables and Swiss keep the rule of the next bullet):
  1. A group ranks by **points per match played**: the points of the matches counted for the unit ÷ their number (`TournamentStanding.averagePoints`; `matches` the count, `totalFP` still the total). A round is one match, whatever its game count. `rankingPoints` is the one value `sameStanding` and `compareStandings` rank by, so every reader agrees; a unit with no counted match keeps `totalFP: null` and sorts last.
  2. **A bye is not a match**: an odd group's rotating bye and an idle group give no points and don't count.
  3. **An absent opponent makes the match void for the unit that turned up** (no points, not counted), **unless the unit has no counted match at all**: then each such match counts for it as a **win** (the points a played clean win gives it: the winner's points of a room of 2 under Standard points / Custom table, a Win for each game of the round under Match results, a Best of N as one match; no score, no room share, not a played match for head to head), until it has one counted match, when those rounds read void again. The standings cell reads "W/O" with a hover saying why (`StandingRoundResult.walkover`; `absentOpponentWins`); **the absent unit gets a counted match with 0 points** (whatever the table: with a Loss worth more than 0, an absence is below a loss; its outcome has no score, rank or share). Both absent: each a counted 0. A unit absent in only some games of a multi-game round is present (its 0 there is a played game, "Absentees").
  4. **A removed unit's matches are void for the whole group**, past and future: every result against it is dropped from its opponents' lines; its remaining matches already turn into byes. A room in play that loses a unit to a removal is void for the other, before or after the other unit's score is typed. Only a removal made while a group round was in play counts (`WithdrawnUnit.round`, written for every withdrawal; one with no recorded round counts as made in the group stage): a qualifier removed from the bracket leaves the group's results as they were.
  5. **A reserve swapped in takes over the seat's line**: the outgoing unit's results so far (absences at 0 included) count for the replacement; nobody else's results change. `WithdrawnUnit.replacedBy` is followed through chains (A → B → C).
  6. **Tie-break order inside a group**, for units level on points per match: (1) **head to head**: the points they took from their matches against each other, a mini-table of only those matches for three or more (both legs of a double round robin), re-applied to any smaller group still level on it until a cycle makes no progress; the match points are those of the tournament's scoring system, and voided matches and no-shows are not matches played; (2) the **room share** (average score relative to the room average, as elsewhere); (3) the **organiser's pick**, through the group cut-off tie resolution, which exists only when all three leave units level (`sameGroupStanding`). `headToHead` (the first mini-table) and `headToHeadLevel` (where the unit's final level starts) are stored on the standing. **Across groups** (the tier order that sorts group winners, runners-up and so on, and Rankings' order of units out in the group stage) it is points per match, then room share: no head to head.
  Why: a group of 5 (4 matches and a bye) used to out-score a group of 4 on total points when the winners were ordered for the bracket; units who met a gap (a removed or absent opponent) took a free win the rest of the group had to earn. A tournament without the switch keeps the next bullet and today's tie-break exactly.
- **A bye counts as a win.** (A group stage with the switch above is the exception: there a bye is not a match.) Each counted round, a unit with a bye (`state.byes[round]`, or `round.groupByes` in Group Stage) and a unit alone in a room (`isUncontestedRoom`: its opponent was removed, or an odd unit was left alone) earns 1st-place points under both systems, with no score and no share entry (`walkoverNames`, `advancement.ts`). Only once the round has begun (`roundHasBegun`: an earlier round, or the current one once the tournament has started), so a schedule that hasn't started has no points. A Group Stage bye goes to the unit's own group, and only when that group plays a match that round (a group whose round robin ran out is idle, not on a bye). Advancement is unaffected: a lone unit already advances.
- **Team scoring** (team formats, chosen in Setup, fixed at generation): *Sum of all members* adds the members' scores; *Save your Buddy* reads only the designated defender's score; *One score per team* stores one score per team under the individual key (`r{ri}-rm{room}-p{pos}`, `game{n}-{team}` in the Final). `scoredTeamSize()` (`scoring.ts`) is the single switch between per-member and per-unit score keys.
- **Shared points.** When units in a pooling room score the same, the admin can **share the points** instead of picking who ranks next (a "Share points" button on the room tie card, next to the "ranks next" buttons). Never automatic.
  - *Where:* every pooling round (`isPoolingRound`, `pooling.ts`): warm-ups, qualification table, Swiss and group stage (`isQual || isSwiss || isNoElim`, never Semis or the Final). Never an elimination round, Kings Valley, a waterfall graph round or a standings cut-off tie: a cut needs an order.
  - *Who:* every tied unit not yet ranked. The admin can rank some first and share the rest (A, B, C tied at 2nd: "A ranks next", then share: A 2nd, B and C =3rd).
  - *Value:* the exact average of the points the covered places earn (`pointsForSharedPlace`, `advancement.ts`), from Standard points or the Custom table: FFA with a largest room of 8, 2nd and 3rd shared in a room of 7, each earns (7.5 + 5.5) / 2 = 6.5 (a shared place in a room of 3 in 2v2v2v2 earns (2.5 + 1) / 2 = 1.75). Under the earlier rule (no `pointsVersion`) the places are read place by place. The average of the already rounded half points is not rounded again. Nothing is rounded in the state; displays show one decimal only when fractional (`formatStandingValue`). Head-to-head, a share is a draw: 1.5 each under Standard points, the average of the table's first two values under Custom.
  - *Places:* `orderRoomWithPlaces` (`scoring.ts`) gives ranked units their own places, then the sharing units the next place together (`sharedBy` > 1), the next unit the place after them all. A shared round result carries `shared: true`; the room card shows "=2", the Standings and Rankings "=2nd".
  - *Seeding:* shared units go to the tier of the shared place (`buildAdvancementTiers`), in every pooling round, warm-ups included (where no points exist: both are 2nd for the next draw).
  - *Stored* in the tie's list as `[...ranked, '#shared', ...shared]` (`SHARED_TIE_MARK`; a unit name cannot contain `#`); only `scoring.ts` reads it (`readTieResolution`; `tieResolutionList` returns the ranked part) and `shareTiedPoints` (`mutations.ts`) writes it, only for the exact set of unranked tied units. Resolved for Next Round (`isTieResolved`).
  - *Cleared* like a "ranks next" pick when a score edit changes the tie (`invalidateStaleTieResolutions`), and also when another unit joins the tied score: a share holds only while the shared and ranked names are exactly the tied units. The tie-break above still orders units on equal points.
- **Tie-breaker: room share relative to the room average.** (Inside a group of a tournament with `groupAverageVersion`, head to head comes first, see "Group stage standings".) Units with equal points are ordered by the higher average *relative room share* (`roomShare` on `TournamentStanding`): per round played in a room, the unit's score ÷ its room's total × the number of scored units in the room (1.00 = an average score in that room; an all-zero room gives everyone 1.00), averaged over those rounds; byes and lone-room wins add no entry. A raw room share (score ÷ room total) would favour small rooms (about 33% against 25% on average), so it is not used. It changes who qualifies and Swiss/qual reseeding order. Older saved standings snapshots have no `roomShare`; there, equal points count as equal. The legacy app had no such tie-breaker.
- **Per-round detail.** `TournamentStanding.rounds` (`StandingRoundResult`: `roundIndex`, `score`, `rank` (the place in the room), `points`, `bye`, and `shared` only when the place was shared) lists every counted round with a result, in round order; a bye has a null score and rank. A counted round without a result has no entry. The Standings table is built from it (`docs/views.md`).
- **Shared ranks and cut-off ties.** `rankStandings()` gives units a shared rank only when points **and** share are equal (`sameStanding()`; both compared within a tolerance of 1e-9, because a total that includes a shared place is a floating-point sum: `samePoints`, `sameRoomShare`). A cut-off tie cluster (the entries `sameStanding` with the boundary unit) exists, and needs the admin's pick in Bracket, only then. With a waterfall Qual source there is one such cluster per band boundary a tie straddles (see "Waterfall bracket"). A unit the admin placed never shares a rank with a neighbour (A, B, C tied, only A picked: A first, B and C share the next rank).
- **Displays count a current-round room only once it's fully scored.** `describeStandings()` (the single source for the Standings tab, Bracket column, follow banner and Rankings badge) computes standings with `liveRound: curRound`: a contested room of the current round counts only when every assigned unit has a score; past rounds count as scored. Real advancement is unchanged and still uses whatever is scored.

## Line-up corrections
The Admin "Correct current round" panel (`lineup-edit.ts`) fixes the round about to be played: **move** a unit to another room, **eliminate** a unit by hand, **reinstate** a unit eliminated in the previous round. Applied as one draft (`applyLineupEdit`), validated as a whole, so a head-to-head room of 2 is corrected by "eliminate X + reinstate Y" together.
- **Scope**: only the current round and only while it has no score (scores are keyed by room position; history stays as played). The one exception is the single move of a unit marked back after the draw (`moveUnitToRoom`, `docs/rules.md`, "Absentees"): it needs only the two rooms of the move to have no score, can be in any round in play, and also runs in a Custom bracket round. Pooling rounds (qual-table, Swiss, no-pooling warm-ups) of any bracket format: moves only, no elimination (use Remove), nobody reinstatable; fixed-draw pooling rounds are refused, and a group stage has no line-up correction: the only change to who plays whom is the swap between groups (below). Bracket rounds (elimination, Semis, Final): single elimination only.
- **Rules**: the round's `advTotal` and room count never change; no room is emptied; byes can be eliminated but nobody moves into or out of a bye; a room is refused only if the edit made it too small (under 2, or no more than `advPerRoom`) or too big (over `roomSize.max`), so existing odd states (a lone room after a removal, an over-cap reserve) don't block unrelated edits; in an elimination round the total must stay above `advTotal` (otherwise lower the count via the advancement edit); the Final keeps at least 2. Reinstating is limited to units present in the previous round's line-up, absent from this one and still in the roster (`computeRankings` derives elimination from "not in the next round's assignments", so rankings follow with no change).
- **Marker**: a unit placed, moved or reinstated this way carries `RoundAssignment.manual: true` and shows a ✋ badge in Bracket ("Set by organiser"). `roomHistory` for the round is rewound and re-recorded.
- **Group stage: swap between groups** (`group-swap.ts`, the Standings tab's "⇄ Swap groups"). Two units of different groups trade places: each takes the other's seat in the other's group, in every group round (`state.groups[].members`, each round's `matches[].pair` and `groupByes`, the drawn `assignments` and `byes`). A seat stays in its group, so the group sizes, the round count and every match's place are unchanged and the round robin stays complete (a double round robin follows in both legs). **Window** (`groupSwapWindow`, asked by the Standings tab and the mutation): a group stage with a schedule generated, either before Confirm & Start with Round 1 drawn, or started with the first group round in play and no later round drawn ("Round 2 is drawn — groups are fixed from here."); after Back to draft Round 1 has no line-up and the window stays closed until the schedule is generated again ("Generate the schedule again to swap groups."). Refused for two units of one group and for a name in no group (a reserve, a removed unit). **During Round 1** any swap is allowed; the rooms of the two units (a unit on a bye has none) are cleared of what the old line-up produced, after a confirm that names the matches: their scores (a team format's per-member keys included), absences and room tie resolutions. The other rooms keep theirs. Save your Buddy defender changes are kept (they belong to the team, not the room). `roomHistory` for the round is rewound and re-recorded, and the group cut-off, exit and band tie resolutions (keyed by group and rank) are dropped. Settings, confirmations, withdrawals and the roster are untouched, and nothing marks a swapped unit (no ✋). A swap does not survive a new Generate Schedule or a pre-start roster change that redraws the schedule, which draw the groups again.

## Going back a round
"← Previous" only moves the view back one round; nothing is undone, so the later round stays drawn. "Next Round" from the earlier round then does one of two things (`drawnAheadStatus()`, `redraw.ts`), and the Bracket says which in a note above the action bar:
- **Re-draw**: when the next round is drawn and nothing in any drawn later round has a score, Next Round discards those draws and seeds the next round again from the current results. With nothing changed the result is identical; after a score correction it is exactly the draw a first advance from the corrected results would have made, in every format, double elimination and waterfall included. `discardDrawsAfter()` first undoes the discarded draws' `roomHistory` entries (not those of a fixed-draw round, which are folded in at generation), pooling-bye counts, byes and tie resolutions, and rebuilds the pending pools (`pendingBracketSeeds`) and lucky losers of every later round by replaying the recorded results of the rounds before the corrected one (`replayBracketPools()`, `bracket-replay.ts`): a double elimination or waterfall round sends each of its units to one destination, computed from its stored scores, byes and routing, and the qualification exit sends the table through the Qual source, so the pools are a function of those results. A unit that withdrew is left out of the replay and a swapped unit is its replacement, as in the live pools.
- **Steps forward unchanged** ("locked"): when any drawn later round has a score. Nothing is re-drawn or re-routed, so a correction in the earlier round does not change the later draw. It also steps forward when the recorded results do not explain the pools as they are (a removal made while viewing an earlier round, a hand-edited database): `replayExplainsPools()` compares what every later round holds (its pool, line-up and byes, less the units of the rounds about to be advanced from again) with the replay, and any difference locks.

Pending ties in the current round still block Next Round in both cases.

With columns, "round" means column. "← Previous" returns to the first round of the previous column; Next Round re-draws every column after it from that column's results when no round of a drawn later column has a score (`drawnAheadStatus`, `discardDrawsAfter`: the drawn column counts as one). A score in any round of a drawn later column locks it.
In dependency play "← Previous" and Next Round work on presses, not columns: see "Dependency play" under "Waterfall bracket". The drawn-ahead note is replaced by a note about a reopened press.

## Absentees
Registered units that don't show up are common. A tournament generated with `gamemodeConfig.absenceVersion` 1, 2 or 3 (generation writes 3, beside `pointsVersion` and `cutVersion`) asks about each score of 0; one without the field behaves as before (no question, no mark, no per-player weighting, vacant team slots included). `usesAbsences(state)` (`absences.ts`) is the one switch for the question and the marks. Version 2 adds the Custom bracket routing below (`routesAbsenteesByLastPlace`), version 3 the likely absentees (`tracksLikelyAbsentees`: the draws, the "?" mark and the move of a unit marked back); a tournament of version 1 keeps routing an absentee by its rank, one of version 2 draws as before.
- **The question.** Each 0 typed in a round in play (the column in play; in dependency play every round drawn and not sent on) asks: did this unit (or member) play, or was it absent? A blank score never means absent, and the Final and the grand final never ask. The answer lives in `state.absences`, keyed by the score key the 0 is stored under (`r{ri}-rm{room}-p{pos}`, with `-g{n}` in a multi-game round and `-m{i}` for a member; no name, so a rename leaves it alone). An answer counts only while that key's score is still 0 (`isMarkedAbsent`): a changed score deletes it (`setRoundScore`), so a corrected 0 asks again; Back to draft, Clear roster, Previous's discarded draws, a swap (the outgoing seat) and a line-up edit delete the answers they touch. The bar on the Bracket asks (`docs/views.md`); an unanswered 0 blocks Next Round (`pending-absences`, "Answer every score of 0 before advancing.") in every mode but One score per team, where it shows without blocking and counts as played.
- **Who is absent from a room.** An individual or a team with one score: its key is marked. Sum of members and Save your Buddy: every member that exists (a vacant slot has no key) is marked. In a multi-game room (Semis) only a unit absent in every game; absent in some games it is present, with 0 for those games (ABS). In every team mode a team whose members are all absent is an absent team. Members of a team with one score can also be marked by hand from the ABSENT panel, for display only (scoring never reads it).
- **Present units are scored as the room they really were.** `scoreRoom` returns the present units only, so places, points (the table read at the present size, `presentUnitCount`), ties, lucky losers and the fair cut are worked out over them. The relative score of a sum-of-members room is per player in play: `(score ÷ players) ÷ (room total ÷ room players)`, where a team's players are its members that exist and were not absent (a vacant slot counts like an absent member); with equal players it is the same number as before. A tie at 0 is held back while any of its units still has a question, and an answer re-checks the room's tie decisions.
- **The absentee itself.** In a points round it gets a missed round: 0 points, and the round is left out of its tie-break average (the standings cell reads ABS, display only). A room with exactly one present unit is a walkover for it (a win, no score, no room share: `isWalkoverRoom`; `isUncontestedRoom` keeps its line-up meaning for the displays); a room with none gives nobody a win. In a round nobody is cut from (a warm-up, a qualification or Swiss round that is not the last) it stays in the draw. In an elimination round it never takes a spot the cut decides: the fair cut sends it to the units that go out (a winners round of a double elimination drops it to the losers bracket), a spare spot goes to a lucky loser, and when there are more absentees than eliminations everyone present advances and the next round is short. Kings Valley moves it down like a last place; a custom bracket (waterfall) ranks it after every present unit of its room (`roomFinishOrder`, `buildAdvancementTiers`) and, from `absenceVersion` 2, **never lets it take a seat above the room's last place**: the present units are routed by their place among the present, and an absentee keeps the seat of its rank only when that rank's destination is the destination of the room's last drawn seat (`absenteeDestination`, `waterfall-bracket.ts`); otherwise it is eliminated. A room of 8 routing 1-4 to Semi A and 5-8 to Lower, with 5 absent: the 3 present go to Semi A (it arrives one short), 4 absentees go to Lower and 1 is out. 1-2 to A, 3-4 to B, 5-8 to C with 6 absent: 2 present to A, 4 absentees to C, 2 out, and B arrives 2 short. With the last place out and 5 absent, every absentee is out. Absentees no more than the last band are routed as before. It applies to every Custom bracket round, and the drawn size counts present and absent together (`roomUnitCount`). One rule serves the routing (`routeWaterfallRound`, so the live graph edit's replay and the expected draw too), Rankings (`outOfCompleteRooms` puts such an absentee in ELIMINATED once its room is scored) and the Bracket (its row gets the red stripe, `absenteeLeavesCustomBracket`). A tournament of version 1 routes an absentee by its rank, as before. Head-to-head: one side absent is a walkover for the other, both absent gives no winner and the fair cut fills the spot from a lucky loser; in a group stage the present unit takes the walkover win and the absentee a missed round (with `groupAverageVersion`: the match is void for the present unit and a counted 0 for the absentee, see "Group stage standings").
- **Smaller-room seats.** The present units of a room with absentees count a smaller-room seat, the absentees none (`docs/seeding.md`).
- **Likely absentees** (`absenceVersion` 3, `likely-absentees.ts`). A unit is likely to be absent from the next round when it was **absent from the last round it was drawn into and played** and has not been marked back since. A unit that played counts as present whatever it was before; a bye is not a round played, and a round it is seated in without a score of its own yet (an unanswered 0 included, except in One score per team, where a 0 is never asked and counts as played) is skipped, so a unit absent in round 1 and drawn into round 2 is still likely absent until it scores. A team is likely absent when the absentee rules say so (every member absent, or its one score absent) and is back as soon as any member is. A replacement swapped in at an absent seat is not (the absence was the swapped-out unit's). Version 2 and earlier have none.
- **The draws place them first** (`placeLikelyAbsentees`, `docs/seeding.md`): in every draw of rooms of 3 or more, one to a room, judged by each room's expected size (its planned seats less the likely absentees already placed): never two in a room while another has fewer, then the room with the most seats left; the others are drawn as usual into the seats left. Rooms of 2 (head to head) keep today's draw, and so does a group stage's exit.
- **Marking back** (`state.backFromAbsence`: player name → the round index of the absence it answers). The organiser clicks the "?" of a likely absentee in MANAGE PLAYERS or MANAGE TEAMS when the unit says it is back (`clickConfirmationAndSeat`), or its chip in the Bracket's "Likely absent" line, which lists the likely absentees of the column in play (`docs/views.md`, Bracket); a score in a later round makes it present again by itself. The mark is by player name, so it follows a rename and a team is back once any member is marked; a later absence has a later round index, so an old mark is harmless. Back to draft and Clear roster delete it.
- **Back after the draw** (`seatReturningUnit`, `returning-unit.ts`). When the round the unit is in was drawn after its absence, no one is re-drawn: only the returning unit may move, to the room with the fewest expected players (the unit taken out of its room first, the other likely absentees counted as missing); an equal room keeps it, then the room it has not met, then the lowest. A room with a score, a room the move would take above the round's largest size (a Custom bracket room, above its declared size) or the room it leaves below the smallest size (at least 2; an elimination room above the places it advances) is not used; when no room fits it stays, now counted as present. A warm-up, qualification or Swiss round with an adaptive draw, a single elimination round and a Custom bracket round can take the move; a group stage, double elimination, Kings Valley, the Final and fixed draws do not. A Custom bracket round at its declared size has no room that can grow, so the move only matters in a short round (its `advTotal` follows the new room sizes). Only the two rooms of the move lose their answers and tie decisions. The organiser is told after the click: "Ann is back: moved to Room C.", "Ann is back and stays in Room B.", or "Ann is back. Room B already has scores, so Ann stays there."; before the draw nothing is said.
- **Rankings.** An eliminated absentee ranks last of its round, below every present unit eliminated in it, in any room (an explicit sort key after the Kings Valley room; organiser's decision 2026-10-29). Absentees of the same round follow the pooling standings and otherwise share a rank. Its where line ends "· absent". In STILL IN a unit absent from its room in the round in play reads the same.
- **Left to the organiser.** Removing or replacing an absentee stays manual (✕ Remove, ⇄ Swap). Going back is the way to answer a 0 of an earlier round (Previous).

## Roster changes during a tournament
Rename, swap, remove and reserve fill live in Rankings (`mutations.ts`). Removing a unit also drops it from every `pendingBracketSeeds` pool, so a unit that was only waiting for a later round is never drawn into it. Renaming a player (`renameIndividual`) changes the name everywhere it is stored (`renameInSchedule`, `rename-unit.ts`): the roster, every round's rooms (a fixed draw's unreached rounds included), byes, `pendingBracketSeeds`, groups, their fixtures and standings, room history (pairs re-sorted), lucky losers, tie decisions, Final scores, anonymous finalists and the confirmation mark, so the old name never comes back in a later round. A team keeps its id when renamed or when a member is, so team renames have no such reach to make. A swap is the one-for-one reserve substitution of an absentee, and works in every format, group stage and waterfall included: the replacement takes the outgoing unit's seat in the current round (a unit on a group bye included), or, for a unit already routed to a later round but not drawn into it yet (waiting in a `pendingBracketSeeds` pool), the slot it earned there (`tierRank`, `pct`); its future group fixtures are relabelled too. A swap of a unit marked absent keeps its seat's 0 and absent mark: the replacement missed that round too; typing its real score clears the mark (the ABSENT panel lists that absence under neither name). A swap is refused once a round that eliminated someone has been advanced (`swapWindowOpen`: `computeRankings` lists at least one eliminated unit with a rank; a unit shown eliminated while its round is still in play has no rank yet and does not close the window), in every format. A unit sent to a losers' bracket or waiting in a pool is not eliminated. A swap also works for a late joiner of the pooling round in play, who sits in no room yet (`replaceLateJoiner`): the substitute is the late joiner and is drawn in at the next advance. The duplicate-name check of a swap ignores the replacement's own reserve entry, so a reserve team can be swapped in (it still refuses a name used by another reserve or roster team). Adding a reserve as an extra unit is allowed until the pooling phase ends, with the restrictions of Reserve admission above; **before Confirm & Start a removal redraws the schedule too** (`removeUnitBeforeStart`, `pre-start-edits.ts`; the unit is still recorded as absent from the start), and when generation can't redraw for the smaller roster (below the format's minimum, a graph built for the exact count) the plain removal stands, Round 1 keeps a short room, and the organiser is told why; a removal is never refused. **A group stage is the exception**: its settings (a total, qualifiers per group) would stay on a roster generation refuses, so a refused redraw there clears the drawn schedule (`scheduleCleared`, `withoutDrawnSchedule`), keeping the roster and the settings, and the organiser changes the setting in Setup and clicks Generate again. Reloading the roster in Setup after Generate redraws the schedule for the new roster, or drops it when it can't (the format changed, a count below the minimum, no stored settings): `reloadRosterBeforeStart`. The Setup form's "Confirm & Start" is disabled while round 1's seats are not exactly the roster (`scheduleMatchesRoster`); filling a vacant team slot, rename and remove are not covered by the swap window. Duplicate player and team names are refused everywhere a name is entered (team member names excepted). Names are Firebase keys, so a name containing `.`, `#`, `$`, `/`, `[`, `]` or a control character is refused everywhere one is typed (`findUnstorableNames`, `roster.ts`; team member names included): the roster load in Setup, rename, swap, walk-up and team edits in Rankings. The domain mutations (`renameIndividual`, `swapIndividual`, `swapTeam`, `updateTeam`, `fillTeamSlot`) return the state unchanged and `addWalkUpIndividual` returns `'unstorable-name'`; `updateTeam` only looks at names the update introduces, so a name stored earlier does not lock its team. Names already stored in older tournaments are not migrated. A removed or swapped-out unit that played at least one match shows as "DNF" in final standings (`state.withdrawnUnits`); one that never played (`hasPlayedAnyMatch`: a seat counts only with a score the unit was not absent for) appears only in the admin ABSENT panel. In a fixed-draw tournament, a removal or swap patches the unreached published rounds (an orphaned opponent gets a bye, rooms are renumbered).

**A team that leaves the roster keeps the scores of its past rooms.** A removed or swapped-out team is in neither `players` nor `reserves`, so scoring reads its member slots from the stored score keys (`r{ri}-rm{room}-p{pos}[-g{n}]-m{i}`, the Final's `game{n}-{team}-m{i}`), its defender from `defenderChanges` by its id, and its absence marks from the same keys; a slot with no key reads 0. Before this was fixed (a bug of every phase for Sum of members and Save your Buddy) every slot of such a team read as vacant, so its past rooms re-ranked and showed 0. Stored tournaments display corrected standings, nothing is re-advanced. A past room still names the team it was played by: `unitDisplay` reads its name and members from `WithdrawnUnit`.

**Player confirmation** (`confirmations.ts`, `state.confirmations`). The organiser marks players as confirmed in the organiser tools; viewers never see it. The mark is stored by name (a player's, a team member's or a reserve's, never a team id), so it belongs to the person: it survives reloading the roster in Setup, Back to draft, a reserve joining and a swap (the incoming name brings its own mark; the outgoing name keeps its mark). A rename moves it (`renameIndividual`, `renameTeamMember`); removing a unit leaves its mark in place, so the same name added back is still confirmed; Clear roster clears every mark. Nothing else reads it: generation, room distribution, advancement, Setup's counts and the roster counts ignore it. A score also confirms a player, worked out from the stored scores and never saved (`scoredPlayers`): a score of play in any game of a pooling, group, bracket or Semis room, or of a Final (an anonymous Final game counts for the finalist behind the alias). It is above 0, or a 0 that was answered "played" (in One score per team, where a 0 is never asked, any 0 counts; a 0 not answered yet, or answered absent, does not, and in a tournament without absence questions a 0 never does). An individual's score confirms the individual; in a team format scored as "sum of members" the member who scored (whoever holds that slot now); in every other team rule (One score per team, Save your Buddy) every member of a team that has a score. **In an `absenceVersion` 3 tournament a likely absentee (`docs/rules.md`, "Absentees") reads "?" and nothing confirms it** (not a manual mark, not an earlier score), until the organiser marks it back (✓, source `'back'`, which an earlier score does not lock); a team with a member back is back and its other members read as they would without the absence. The toggle's click: a likely absentee's "?" marks it back and confirms it, a back mark's ✓ takes the mark away, any other toggle confirms or unconfirms by hand. A player confirmed by a score cannot be unmarked (the toggle is locked); the manual mark, if any, is kept underneath and shows again if the score is corrected to 0. Back to draft keeps the manual marks but clears the scores, so the confirmations that came from scores go with them.
