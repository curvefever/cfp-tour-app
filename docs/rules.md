# Tournament rules

Current rules of the app's tournament engine (`src/domain/tournament/`). Read when a task touches generation, advancement, scoring, formats or phases. Build history and reasoning: search `HANDOFF_LOG.md` for the entry names cited below.

## Game formats
`formats.ts`, `GAME_FORMATS`. A "unit" is a player (individual formats) or a team.

| Format | Unit | Room size | Odd-count strategies (default) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ffa-individual` | player | 6–8, ideal 8 | — |
| `team-2v2v2v2` | team of 2 | 3–4, ideal 4 | — |
| `team-3v3v3` | team of 3 | 2–3, ideal 3 | — |
| `team-3v3` | team of 3 | 2 (head-to-head) | `none`/`bye`/`flex` (`bye`) |
| `individual-1v1` | player | 2 (head-to-head) | `none`/`bye` (`bye`) |

FFA counts that can't fill rooms of 6–8 are seated in smaller rooms by design (9 → [5,4], 17 → [6,6,5]). Planned advancement counts (what a schedule chooses itself) are always seatable; counts the organiser enters are seated as they come.

An unset odd-count strategy means the format's default. "Flex" lets `team-3v3` seat a 3-team room (room size 2–3). "None" refuses to generate on a count that can't be split evenly.

Room counts and sizes for any headcount come from `distributeRooms()` (`room-distribution.ts`); a round's advancement target splits into `advPerRoom` per room plus `luckyCount` lucky-loser slots (`splitAdvancement`). When removals leave a round with a different headcount than planned, `fitRoundToPool` reshapes it at advance time.

## Pooling phases (`poolingPhase`)
- `none` — 2 no-elimination warm-up rounds.
- `qual-table` — qualification rounds (3 by default) feeding one cumulative standings table; the top `qualAdv` advance. With a waterfall bracket the table is a source of the graph and `qualAdv` is derived from it (see "Waterfall bracket").
- `swiss` — 3–7 fold-paired rounds, same standings engine as `qual-table`. Every Swiss round is `floor(n/2)` rooms of 2 plus `n % 2` byes, whatever the odd-count strategy.
- `group-stage` — round-robin groups (single or double), top N per group advance. Pairings are known at generation (`round.matches`/`round.groupByes`).

Rules:
- Swiss and Group Stage are **head-to-head only** (`individual-1v1`, `team-3v3`), gated in Setup and in `generateTournament()`: fold-pairing and round-robin always pair exactly 2 units.
- **Non-counting rounds** (qual-table/Swiss only): a tournament can mark its leading N rounds `excludeFromStandings`; they're played and reseed normally but `computeQualificationStandings` skips them.
- **Reserve admission** (`addReserveUnit`, `mutations.ts`): a reserve or walk-up can join until the pooling phase ends (`reserveOpen`). While a pooling round is in play (warm-up, qualification or Swiss) the round stays as drawn: the joiner sits it out (0 points if it counts) and is drawn in with everyone else when the round advances, so no over-full room is ever created, in any bracket format. At that advance a no-elimination pooling round takes in every roster unit not already advancing (`withUndrawnRosterUnits`, `transitions.ts`), except at the last qualifying round, where the standings decide and the joiner, ranked last with 0 points, is normally cut. From 2 missed counted rounds (`missedCountedRounds`: qualification/Swiss rounds up to and including the one in play, not the leading non-counting ones) the admin confirms first (`confirm-late-join`). The odd-count strategy "None" refuses a joiner who would leave a count that is not a multiple of the room size (`'strict-room'`). Group stage (`'group-stage'`), a fixed draw (`'fixed-draw'`) and a waterfall without a pooling phase (`'waterfall-bracket'`) still refuse. In a tournament with no pooling phase the window ends with its first bracket round: a reserve added then is seated in that round, in the least-full room (the over-cap confirm, `'strict-room'` under "None"); during a warm-up the joiner is drawn into the first bracket round. **First-round seeding:** a joiner's level is unknown, so in the round they enter they are seeded as an average unit (see `docs/seeding.md`): in the middle tier of the round they missed in the room-based reseed, at the end of the fold in Swiss so they meet a middle-ranked unit, and never given the bye (a bye counts as a win; if only joiners are left to take one, the usual rule applies). From their second round on their own results seed them, and their standings keep counting the missed rounds as 0. In a waterfall, a join or removal during qualification also reshapes the pooling rounds not yet reached for the new head count at once (`refitPoolingRound`, `pooling.ts`), so the Bracket shows the room sizes the next draw will use; the graph rounds and the last qualifying round's `standingsRoutes` are left alone.
- **Draw publication** (qual-table/Swiss, Setup "Draw publication"): `adaptive` (default) reseeds from live results; `fixed` publishes the whole pooling schedule at generation — see `docs/seeding.md`.

## Bracket phases (`scheduleLogic`)
- **`single-elimination`** — room-based cuts to Semis and a Final. **Semis size**: two rooms of the ideal size (FFA 16), or everyone entering the bracket when that is fewer, down to two rooms of the format's *smallest* room (FFA 12: 14 → [7, 7], 13 → [7, 6], 12 → [6, 6]; 2v2v2v2 6; 3v3v3 4; head-to-head 4); the Final stays at its room size (FFA 8), so 4 per Semis room. When everyone entering already fits the Semis there is no elimination round (`singleEliminationBracketPhase`); explicit targets still add rounds. A "Semis size override" wins over the default. Below the floor generation **refuses** (it never raises the request): confirmed count under the floor, "Advance to bracket" under the floor (`Advance to bracket (11) is below the minimum of 12 for single elimination (Semis needs 2 rooms of at least 6).`), and a Group Stage that sends fewer units than the floor to the bracket (`Group stage sends 3 players to the bracket; …`). A request above the confirmed count still clamps down to everyone.
- **"Advance to bracket" below a bracket format's floor is refused in every format**, naming the floor: single elimination 2 × smallest room, Kings Valley and shared-Final double elimination 2 × ideal room (FFA 16), race double elimination 4; waterfall has no floor here (its graph decides). Group Stage ignores the field (its entry is groups × qualifiers per group), so a leftover hidden value there never refuses generation.
- Single elimination and the shared-Final variant below accept optional explicit round targets and per-round seeding overrides (Setup → "Advanced round overrides"). The targets (and "Advance to bracket", `qualAdv`) stay editable live, in the Admin "Correct current round" panel (`live-advancement-edit.ts`):
  - **While pooling runs** (qual-table, Swiss, no-pooling warm-ups; single elimination and shared-Final): the bracket is rebuilt from scratch like Setup does, pooling rounds untouched (fixed draws included). In single elimination the Semis size follows the new "Advance to bracket" (`followsEntryCount`) unless the tournament has a Semis size override. The targets list is fully editable, length included (blank = automatic); with seeding overrides its length must stay the same (they are index-aligned). `qualAdv` (qual-table/Swiss only) is refused, not clamped, outside the format minimum and the roster count. A stale standings-cutoff tie resolution (`qual-cutoff`) is dropped when `qualAdv` changes.
  - **Single elimination mid-bracket**: values only, from the current round (until advanced, scores allowed) to the last round before Semis; the number of rounds never changes. The current round keeps its rooms; later rounds are rebuilt (`eliminationRound`, existing seeding overrides kept) and Semis is refitted to the last value. Tie resolutions stay (they order equal scores, wherever the cut moves).
  - Shared-Final double elimination is editable only until its bracket starts; race double elimination, Kings Valley, waterfall and group stage are not editable. A saved edit writes the settings snapshot (`settings.qualAdv`, `settings.eliminationRoundTargets`, `gamemodeConfig.explicitTargets`) so "Copy settings" copies what was played; an automatic curve edited mid-bracket becomes an explicit list.
- **`double-elimination`** — head-to-head race bracket (winners bracket, losers bracket, grand final). Not available with Flex.
- **`double-elimination-shared-final`** — multi-unit double elimination feeding one shared Final. A unit dropped from the winners bracket reaches a losers-bracket round within at most 2 winners-bracket rounds (`MAX_WB_ROUNDS_BEFORE_LB`, `double-elimination.ts`).
  - **Losers-bracket round sizes are always seatable.** When a losers round's count (previous survivors plus pending drops) can't be seated, three levers are tried in order: (1) the previous losers round's survivor target moves; (2) the round is deferred, up to the 2-round limit above; (3) the feeding winners round's target moves, keeping the curve strictly decreasing and the next winners round seatable. Each takes the nearest value that works, the smaller cut on a tie; organiser-set targets (`eliminationRoundTargets`) are never changed. A few small FFA configurations (17–19 entrants with 3–4 LB qualifiers) can't be fixed by any lever and fall back to smaller rooms by design.
- **`kings-valley`** — see below.
- **`waterfall-bracket`** — see below.

### Kings Valley (`kings-valley.ts`)
A room ladder: rooms are ranked top to bottom. Each round, a room's top finishers promote to the room above, its bottom finishers demote to the room below, the rest stay; the lowest room with a real match eliminates its bottom finishers instead of demoting them. Room 1's promote band stays in room 1.
- Promotions are `max(1, round(size × 0.25))` per room (`KINGS_VALLEY_MOVE_FRACTION`). The bottom room's cut is the one nearest half of it (`KINGS_VALLEY_ELIMINATION_FRACTION = 0.5`) that leaves a **seatable** total (every room within the format's minimum and maximum), the smaller cut on a tie. Demotions are then sized top-down so the moves land exactly on the next round's rooms (promotions are only lowered when a room couldn't supply its demotions). One function, `kingsValleyRoundMoves` (bands plus next room sizes), drives generation, the real advance and the Bracket exit chips, so chips, placeholders and the real seating agree. Room sizes are the `distributeRooms()` layout of the survivors; a room below the format's minimum is never planned.
- A room with fewer than 2 units (only after a removal) **holds**: nobody in it moves or is cut, and the cut comes from the lowest room that still has a real match (the plain per-room maths, no seatable-cut solving).
- The ladder ends in a single-room Final, capped at `MAX_KINGS_VALLEY_ROUNDS = 14` rounds.
- **Size limit.** Past the cap the forced Final would exceed the room maximum, so generation refuses a field above `kingsValleyMaxEntrants(roomSize)`: 32 teams in 2v2v2v2, 24 in 3v3v3, 60 players in FFA. The message names the limit and the 14 rounds, and suggests lowering "Advance to bracket" or adding a qualification phase. The re-plan after a reserve (`fitKingsValleyTail`) is not refused: it may still force a larger Final.
- **Accepted:** a removal can leave the current round (and rarely the next) with a room below the minimum; the organiser can fill it with a reserve.
- If a removal or reserve makes the survivor count differ from the plan, the rest of the ladder (Final included) is re-planned at the next advance (`fitKingsValleyTail`, `transitions.ts`); room movement uses `sequentialSeed`.
- **Unavailable for head-to-head formats** (`individual-1v1`, `team-3v3` including Flex): with rooms of 2 it cuts one unit per round and can't reach a Final within the cap above 16 units. Hidden in Setup and refused by generation; head-to-head Kings Valley tournaments generated earlier still play out.
- No Setup fields: the organiser tunes nothing.

### Waterfall bracket (`waterfall-bracket.ts`)
Organiser-authored rank-band routing: a graph (a `ROUNDS:`/`ROUTES:` text format, edited through a click-to-route table in Setup, `src/features/admin/waterfall/`) in which each room's finishers split by rank into bands, each routed to any later round or eliminated. No byes or lucky losers; adding a reserve is blocked when there is no pooling phase (with one, a late joiner is taken, see Reserve admission; a swap, which keeps the head count, is always allowed).

**Unrouted ranks are eliminated.** A room's `ROUTES:` line may leave ranks unrouted, the same as the `Qual` line: a rank no band covers is eliminated, and a room with no line at all is all-eliminated (`buildRoomDestinations`, `validateAndOrderWaterfallGraph`). Bands must still not overlap or name a rank outside the room, and explicit `->eliminated` bands still parse. A non-Final round must send at least one rank on (`Round "R2" sends nobody on -- route at least one rank to a later round, or make it the Final.`), so a forgotten route fails loudly; a forgotten route *into* a round is still caught by "a round's declared size must equal what routes into it". The editor, the saved graph text, generation and the live "Edit graph" all follow this one rule.

**The qualification table as a source (`Qual`).** After a `qual-table` pooling phase the graph's `ROUTES:` has a reserved `Qual` line that routes the final standings by rank like a room (`Qual: 1-16->R1, 17-24->R1C`): ranks 1–16 go to R1 (2 rooms of 8), 17–24 to R1C, everyone else is eliminated. `Qual` has no room letter, is never declared in `ROUNDS:` (a round named `Qual` is an error in every pooling phase), and a `Qual` line under any other pooling phase is refused at generation. Ranks the line doesn't route are eliminated, explicit `->eliminated` bands (also in the middle) are allowed, and the eliminated tail grows or shrinks with the head count (graph made at 26 routing 1–16: 10 out; at 31: 15). A graph with no `Qual` line (older saved graphs, the examples, copied settings) is read as `Qual: 1-<entry round total> -> <entry round>`; this is done in one place, `validateAndOrderWaterfallGraph(…, { standingsSource: true })`, and the editor's draft load uses the same reading.
- **"Advance to bracket" is derived**, not entered: `cfg.qualAdv` = the highest non-eliminated routed Qual rank (the form's value is ignored). Generation refuses a graph that routes more ranks than there are players (`The graph sends qualification rank 24 on, but only 20 players are registered …`); every declared round must be fed by `Qual` or another round, and a round's declared size must equal what routes into it (Qual ranks count).
- **Where the routing lives**: `standingsRoutes` on the last qualification round (index = standings rank − 1, absolute round index or `'eliminated'`, same convention as `waterfallRoutes`; length = highest routed rank). At the pooling exit (`advanceFromStandings`, `routeStandings`) the ordered table (cut-off tie orders applied) is routed from the standings, not from the top-`qualAdv` list, with no bye logic and no `tieredSeed`: the next round is drawn from its pool by `seedRoundFromPendingPool` like every other waterfall round, so a band sent to a multi-room round is spread over its rooms, ranks 1 and 2 in different rooms. A round fed by a lower band waits in `pendingBracketSeeds` until its turn; Rankings counts those units as alive.
- **Ties at every band boundary** need a tie-break: one cluster per distinct tied standing that straddles a boundary (a position whose destination differs from the next one's, eliminated counting as a destination). Its resolution fully orders it, so one cluster settles every boundary inside it. Key `qual-cutoff` for the cluster holding the last routed rank, `qual-band-<first rank>` for the others; the Bracket banner names the destinations ("Qualification tie between R1 and R1C").
- **Head-count shortfall**: if withdrawals during qualification leave fewer ranked units than the highest routed rank, Next Round is blocked with a message naming both numbers and pointing to "Edit graph" in the Admin tab. A late joiner during qualification does not trigger it: the waterfall's rounds are not re-planned (that would discard live graph edits) and the exit routes the standings by rank, so an extra unit only lengthens the eliminated tail (graph routing 24 at 37 registered plus a joiner: 14 out). A reserve can also replace a no-show one for one (swap).
- **Withdrawals once the bracket is running: the empty seat ripples down.** A waterfall round accepts fewer units than the graph declares, never more (more is `'malformed-waterfall-round'`, pointing to "Edit graph"). The same holds for the first round after a pooling exit without a Qual source (Swiss, group stage, warm-ups). A round that arrives short keeps its room count; the missing seats come out of the last rooms first, one per room in turn, so room sizes differ by at most 1 (2 rooms of 8 with 15 units: [8, 7]; with 14: [7, 7]; with 13: [7, 6]). `round.rooms`/`players` hold the real shape, `advTotal` counts the ranks the real rooms still send on, and `declaredRooms` keeps the graph's own room sizes while the round is short (`fitWaterfallRound`, `waterfall-bracket.ts`; never `distributeRooms`). Each room routes the ranks it has, so a gap in a bottom band that is `eliminated` is absorbed there and the next round arrives full; a gap in a band that routes on arrives one short in the destination, and so on down the graph. A room left with one unit routes its rank-1 band uncontested; a round nobody reaches, or a Final below 2, blocks ("too-few-units"). No next-in-line unit moves up. Seated and waiting withdrawals follow the same rule (removal also clears the unit from every pending pool, see Roster changes). The head-count shortfall above applies only to withdrawals during qualification.

- **Round names** may use letters, digits, spaces and dashes (up to 30 characters in the editor), starting and ending with a letter or digit: "Round 5 Upper", "Semi-B". Runs of spaces collapse to one and names match case-insensitively. `.`, `,`, `:`, `>` and `=` stay out (graph syntax), and `Qual` and `eliminated` are reserved (the editor refuses to rename a round to `Qual` too). One normaliser, `normalizeRoundLabel`, is applied wherever a label is read (ROUNDS, ROUTES addresses, every destination, rename), so the saved text round-trips.
- **Columns: rounds played together.** Every waterfall round belongs to a column, numbered from 1 (the qualification table is column 0). Rounds of one column are drawn together when the column is entered, scored in any order, and advanced by one Next Round; NOW sits on the column. `assignWaterfallColumns` (`waterfall-bracket.ts`) is the one function that numbers them, for validation, generation, the live edit and the editor.
  - **Automatic.** A round with no number of its own goes in column 1 when no waterfall round routes into it (the entry round, or a round fed only by `Qual`); otherwise in the column after the latest column of the rounds that route into it. The live graph: [Round 5 Upper, Round 5 Lower], [Semi A, Round 6], [Round 7], [Semi-B], [Final]; the 32-player example: [5], [SemiA, 6B, 6C], [7A], [SemiB], [Final]. The Final is always alone in its column.
  - **Manual.** A ROUNDS line may end with `COLUMN <n>` (a whole number from 1), alone or with `FINAL`, in either order, each once, any letter case: `Semi A = 4 COLUMN 3`. It must be later than the column of every round that routes into it. Refused with a message, never moved: `Round "B" can't be in column 2: round "A" routes into it and is in column 2.` and `Column 4 has no round -- renumber the later columns or set a round to column 4.` (a gap between 1 and the last column). A numbered round is stored as such (`TournamentRound.waterfallColumnSet`), so a number set equal to the automatic column still pins the round against a later routing change.
  - **Play order** is the routing walk's order (today's order) stably sorted by column, so no existing graph changes its order and every column is contiguous in `state.rounds`. A live edit's reached rounds come first, as given.
  - **Stored at generation**: `TournamentRound.waterfallColumn` (and `waterfallColumnSet`) on every waterfall round, written by `waterfallBracketPhase`. A tournament generated before columns existed has none: it keeps playing one round at a time, and once its bracket has started a `COLUMN` number in a live edit is refused (`This tournament's rounds are played one at a time -- columns can't be set once its bracket has started.`). Before the bracket is entered, a save gives it columns. `round-columns.ts` holds the helpers every `curRound` comparison goes through (`currentColumn`, `isCurrentRound`, `isRoundReached`, `isRoundDone`, `nextColumnStart`, `previousColumnStart`, `roundInPlayOf`); `curRound` is the first round of the current column.
  - **Playing a column.** `enterNextWaterfallColumn` (`transitions.ts`) folds the arrivals into the pools, fits and draws every round of the next column (a round that doesn't fit leaves the state unchanged) and sets `curRound` to its first round. `advanceWaterfallBracket` routes every round of the current column, and a tie anywhere in it blocks Next Round (`hasPendingColumnTies`). Next Round does not require every room to be scored: the Bracket asks first, see `docs/views.md`. Withdrawals, swaps and defender changes for a unit apply to the round of the column that holds it (`roundInPlayOf`).
  - **Live edit with columns.** A reached round (the whole current column counts) keeps its column; a different `COLUMN` number on it is refused (`Round "A" has already started in column n.`); an unreached round can't use a started column (a manual number there: `Column n has already started -- choose column <first free> or later.`; an automatic one goes to the first column after the current one). If the edit changes the line-up of rounds of the current column, each is re-drawn while it has no score and a score in any of them refuses the edit, naming it; the saved-graph message names several re-drawn rounds joined with " + ".

The graph can still be corrected from the Admin tab while the tournament runs (`RunningWaterfallPanel.tsx`, `waterfall-live-edit.ts`): any round's routing can change, including already-played rounds, as long as replaying the edited routing reproduces every played round's recorded line-up exactly. A reached round (index ≤ the current round) keeps its label, room count, room size and Final flag -- only its routing can change; an unreached round can be freely resized, renamed, added or removed. If the current round's own line-up would change under the new routing, its rooms are re-drawn -- but only while it has no score yet; once a score is entered there, a line-up-changing edit is refused. With a Qual source, the Qual block is editable too: while qualification runs a save rewrites the last qualification round's `standingsRoutes` and `cfg.qualAdv` (the highest rank is checked against the units still in the roster, not the registered count), and once the bracket is entered the Qual routing is replayed into the pools and the first waterfall round's line-up is compared like any other reached round. `state.rounds` is the source of truth for what's actually running, not the `settings.waterfallGraph` snapshot (kept in sync on every save, so "Copy settings from a past tournament" still copies the corrected graph). No edit history -- a mistaken edit can only be corrected with another edit, not undone.

After a withdrawal the replay stays exact. At the Qual exit the final standings are stored on the last qualification round (`standingsExitOrder`: name and room share in rank order, tie resolutions applied); once the bracket is entered the replay routes that stored order, not a table recomputed from the current roster (whose ranks would shift when a unit is removed and pull the next rank into the gap). A tournament that exited before this was stored falls back to the recomputed table. Names in recorded line-ups that are no longer registered are ignored on both sides of the comparison and dropped from rebuilt pools; a unit that was swapped out maps to its replacement (`WithdrawnUnit.replacedBy`, followed through further swaps). The shape check of a reached round compares the declared room sizes (`declaredRooms ?? rooms`), so a round drawn short can still be saved with its graph shape, and its `advTotal` is recounted from the edited bands.

## Semis and Final
Both support 1–4 games, summed ("Semis format"/"Finals format"). Final scores live in `finalScores`; multi-game Semis use room score keys with a `-g{n}` segment. A plain (non-grand-final) Final can have games flagged anonymous (`round.anonymousGames`): those games are scored under `Finalist-N` placeholders, then "Connect & reveal" merges them onto the real finalists. Not available for grand-final race Finals or team formats.

## Lucky losers
In an elimination round with `luckyCount > 0`, each room's best non-advancing unit is a candidate, ranked by its score relative to its room's average (score ÷ room total × units in the room, 1.00 = average), so room size gives no head start; the top `luckyCount` advance (`advancement.ts`). A room whose scores are all zero offers no candidate. Candidates with equal shares are split by room order.

## Scoring (pooling standings only)
Selectable per tournament when `poolingPhase !== 'none'`; applies to qual-table, Swiss and Group Stage standings. Semis/Final use raw score sums.
- **Standard points** (default; `'fairpoints'` in persisted state): each counted round a unit earns `standardPoints(place, maxRoomSize)` = `maxRoomSize + 1 − place` (floored at 0), with `maxRoomSize` the tournament's `gamemodeConfig.roomSize.max`: 8, 7, …, 1 in FFA, 4, 3, 2, 1 in 2v2v2v2, 3, 2, 1 in 3v3v3, win 2 / loss 1 head-to-head. The same place earns the same points whatever the size of the room (last of a room of 3 in 2v2v2v2 earns 2, like 3rd of 4). Points are **summed** over the counted rounds, higher is better; a counted round the unit didn't play earns 0, so a late arrival is not compensated.
- **Custom points table** (`'positional-points'`): an organiser table (`positionalPointsTable`, highest place first, e.g. `10,8,6,5,4,3,2,1`, must cover the largest room and never increase), summed like Standard points, higher is better. Its first value is also what a bye earns.
- **Names**: players only ever see "Points" / "Pts" (the totals are whole numbers unless a room shared points). Setup and the Admin settings recap name the two options "Standard points (1st = largest room size, −1 per place)" and "Custom points table" (`SCORING_SYSTEM_OPTION_LABELS`, `scoring.ts`). The persisted keys `'fairpoints'` / `'positional-points'` and the `totalFP` field keep their old names. The points rule is not versioned: every tournament, archived ones included, recomputes with the current rule.
- **A bye counts as a win.** Each counted round, a unit with a bye (`state.byes[round]`, or `round.groupByes` in Group Stage) and a unit alone in a room (`isUncontestedRoom`: its opponent was removed, or an odd unit was left alone) earns 1st-place points under both systems, with no score and no share entry (`walkoverNames`, `advancement.ts`). Only once the round has begun (`roundHasBegun`: an earlier round, or the current one once the tournament has started), so a schedule that hasn't started has no points. A Group Stage bye goes to the unit's own group, and only when that group plays a match that round (a group whose round robin ran out is idle, not on a bye). Advancement is unaffected: a lone unit already advances.
- **Team scoring** (team formats, chosen in Setup, fixed at generation): *Sum of all members* adds the members' scores; *Save your Buddy* reads only the designated defender's score; *Survival Teams* stores one score per team under the individual key (`r{ri}-rm{room}-p{pos}`, `game{n}-{team}` in the Final). `scoredTeamSize()` (`scoring.ts`) is the single switch between per-member and per-unit score keys.
- **Shared points.** When units in a pooling room score the same, the admin can **share the points** instead of picking who ranks next (a "Share points" button on the room tie card, next to the "ranks next" buttons). Never automatic.
  - *Where:* every pooling round (`isPoolingRound`, `pooling.ts`): warm-ups, qualification table, Swiss and group stage (`isQual || isSwiss || isNoElim`, never Semis or the Final). Never an elimination round, Kings Valley, a waterfall graph round or a standings cut-off tie: a cut needs an order.
  - *Who:* every tied unit not yet ranked. The admin can rank some first and share the rest (A, B, C tied at 2nd: "A ranks next", then share: A 2nd, B and C =3rd).
  - *Value:* the exact average of the points the covered places earn (`pointsForSharedPlace`, `advancement.ts`), from Standard points or the Custom table: FFA with a largest room of 8, 2nd and 3rd shared, each earns (7 + 6) / 2 = 6.5. Nothing is rounded in the state; displays show one decimal only when fractional (`formatStandingValue`). Head-to-head, a share is a draw: 1.5 each under Standard points, the average of the table's first two values under Custom.
  - *Places:* `orderRoomWithPlaces` (`scoring.ts`) gives ranked units their own places, then the sharing units the next place together (`sharedBy` > 1), the next unit the place after them all. A shared round result carries `shared: true`; the room card shows "=2", the Standings and Rankings "=2nd".
  - *Seeding:* shared units go to the tier of the shared place (`buildAdvancementTiers`), in every pooling round, warm-ups included (where no points exist: both are 2nd for the next draw).
  - *Stored* in the tie's list as `[...ranked, '#shared', ...shared]` (`SHARED_TIE_MARK`; a unit name cannot contain `#`); only `scoring.ts` reads it (`readTieResolution`; `tieResolutionList` returns the ranked part) and `shareTiedPoints` (`mutations.ts`) writes it, only for the exact set of unranked tied units. Resolved for Next Round (`isTieResolved`).
  - *Cleared* like a "ranks next" pick when a score edit changes the tie (`invalidateStaleTieResolutions`), and also when another unit joins the tied score: a share holds only while the shared and ranked names are exactly the tied units. The tie-break above still orders units on equal points.
- **Tie-breaker: room share relative to the room average.** Units with equal points are ordered by the higher average *relative room share* (`roomShare` on `TournamentStanding`): per round played in a room, the unit's score ÷ its room's total × the number of scored units in the room (1.00 = an average score in that room; an all-zero room gives everyone 1.00), averaged over those rounds; byes and lone-room wins add no entry. A raw room share (score ÷ room total) would favour small rooms (about 33% against 25% on average), so it is not used. It changes who qualifies and Swiss/qual reseeding order. Older saved standings snapshots have no `roomShare`; there, equal points count as equal. The legacy app had no such tie-breaker.
- **Per-round detail.** `TournamentStanding.rounds` (`StandingRoundResult`: `roundIndex`, `score`, `rank` (the place in the room), `points`, `bye`, and `shared` only when the place was shared) lists every counted round with a result, in round order; a bye has a null score and rank. A counted round without a result has no entry. The Standings table is built from it (`docs/views.md`).
- **Shared ranks and cut-off ties.** `rankStandings()` gives units a shared rank only when points **and** share are equal (`sameStanding()`; both compared within a tolerance of 1e-9, because a total that includes a shared place is a floating-point sum: `samePoints`, `sameRoomShare`). A cut-off tie cluster (the entries `sameStanding` with the boundary unit) exists, and needs the admin's pick in Bracket, only then. With a waterfall Qual source there is one such cluster per band boundary a tie straddles (see "Waterfall bracket"). A unit the admin placed never shares a rank with a neighbour (A, B, C tied, only A picked: A first, B and C share the next rank).
- **Displays count a current-round room only once it's fully scored.** `describeStandings()` (the single source for the Standings tab, Bracket column, follow banner and Rankings badge) computes standings with `liveRound: curRound`: a contested room of the current round counts only when every assigned unit has a score; past rounds count as scored. Real advancement is unchanged and still uses whatever is scored.

## Line-up corrections
The Admin "Correct current round" panel (`lineup-edit.ts`) fixes the round about to be played: **move** a unit to another room, **eliminate** a unit by hand, **reinstate** a unit eliminated in the previous round. Applied as one draft (`applyLineupEdit`), validated as a whole, so a head-to-head room of 2 is corrected by "eliminate X + reinstate Y" together.
- **Scope**: only the current round and only while it has no score (scores are keyed by room position; history stays as played). Pooling rounds (qual-table, Swiss, no-pooling warm-ups) of any bracket format: moves only, no elimination (use Remove), nobody reinstatable; fixed-draw pooling rounds and group stage are refused. Bracket rounds (elimination, Semis, Final): single elimination only.
- **Rules**: the round's `advTotal` and room count never change; no room is emptied; byes can be eliminated but nobody moves into or out of a bye; a room is refused only if the edit made it too small (under 2, or no more than `advPerRoom`) or too big (over `roomSize.max`), so existing odd states (a lone room after a removal, an over-cap reserve) don't block unrelated edits; in an elimination round the total must stay above `advTotal` (otherwise lower the count via the advancement edit); the Final keeps at least 2. Reinstating is limited to units present in the previous round's line-up, absent from this one and still in the roster (`computeRankings` derives elimination from "not in the next round's assignments", so rankings follow with no change).
- **Marker**: a unit placed, moved or reinstated this way carries `RoundAssignment.manual: true` and shows a ✋ badge in Bracket ("Set by organiser"). `roomHistory` for the round is rewound and re-recorded.

## Going back a round
"← Previous" only moves the view back one round; nothing is undone, so the later round stays drawn. "Next Round" from the earlier round then does one of two things (`drawnAheadStatus()`, `redraw.ts`), and the Bracket says which in a note above the action bar:
- **Re-draw**: when the next round is drawn and nothing in any drawn later round has a score, Next Round discards those draws and seeds the next round again from the current results. With nothing changed the result is identical; after a score correction it is exactly the draw a first advance from the corrected results would have made. `discardDrawsAfter()` first undoes the discarded draws' `roomHistory` entries (not those of a fixed-draw round, which are folded in at generation), pooling-bye counts, byes, lucky losers and tie resolutions.
- **Steps forward unchanged** ("locked"): when any drawn later round has a score, or a double elimination or waterfall round from the current one up to the one before the last drawn round has been advanced from (it already pushed its units into the bracket pools). Nothing is re-drawn or re-routed, so a correction in the earlier round does not change the later draw. A bracket round that is only drawn, not yet advanced from, still re-draws: the first bracket round after pooling re-draws when the last pooling round is corrected.

Pending ties in the current round still block Next Round in both cases.

With columns, "round" means column. "← Previous" returns to the first round of the previous column; from a column to the one before it Next Round steps forward unchanged (a waterfall round that has been advanced from is always "locked"); from the last qualification round back over a drawn column 1 with no score in any of its rounds, Next Round re-draws every round of that column (`drawnAheadStatus`, `discardDrawsAfter`: the drawn column counts as one, and only the rounds before it have been advanced from). A score in any round of column 1 locks it.

## Roster changes during a tournament
Rename, swap, remove and reserve fill live in Rankings (`mutations.ts`). Removing a unit also drops it from every `pendingBracketSeeds` pool, so a unit that was only waiting for a later round is never drawn into it. A swap is the one-for-one reserve substitution of a no-show, and works in every format, group stage and waterfall included: the replacement takes the outgoing unit's seat in the current round (a unit on a group bye included), or, for a unit already routed to a later round but not drawn into it yet (waiting in a `pendingBracketSeeds` pool), the slot it earned there (`tierRank`, `pct`); its future group fixtures are relabelled too. A swap is refused once anyone has been eliminated (`swapWindowOpen`: `computeRankings` lists at least one eliminated unit), in every format. A unit sent to a losers' bracket or waiting in a pool is not eliminated. Adding a reserve as an extra unit is allowed until the pooling phase ends, with the restrictions of Reserve admission above; filling a vacant team slot, rename and remove are not covered by the swap window. Duplicate player and team names are refused everywhere a name is entered (team member names excepted). Names are Firebase keys, so a name containing `.`, `#`, `$`, `/`, `[`, `]` or a control character is refused everywhere one is typed (`findUnstorableNames`, `roster.ts`; team member names included): the roster load in Setup, rename, swap, walk-up and team edits in Rankings. The domain mutations (`renameIndividual`, `swapIndividual`, `swapTeam`, `updateTeam`, `fillTeamSlot`) return the state unchanged and `addWalkUpIndividual` returns `'unstorable-name'`; `updateTeam` only looks at names the update introduces, so a name stored earlier does not lock its team. Names already stored in older tournaments are not migrated. A removed or swapped-out unit that played at least one match shows as "DNF" in final standings (`state.withdrawnUnits`); one that never played appears only in the admin "No-shows" panel. In a fixed-draw tournament, a removal or swap patches the unreached published rounds (an orphaned opponent gets a bye, rooms are renumbered).
