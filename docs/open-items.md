# Open items

Known gaps, accepted limitations and undecided questions. Read when planning a feature, diagnosing a bug, or closing out a build (update it when a build closes or changes an item). Larger future projects are in `docs/roadmap.md`.

## Known gaps
- **The expected draw covers only the next column; later rounds keep placeholder labels.** The Bracket shows names for the next column once the round in play is scored (`expectedNextColumn`); a column after that still shows "Round 3 · 2nd"-style places, since its line-up depends on results not yet played. A follow box naming a unit's expected next room is in `docs/roadmap.md`.
- **`AppShell.render.test.tsx` prints "Timeout calling onTaskUpdate" from the vitest worker** when the whole render sweep runs (about 70 s on the review machine). The tests all pass; it looks like the sweep starving the worker's reporting channel, so a runner that treats an unhandled error as a failure may need the sweep split or its timeout raised. It depends on the machine's load: under load the file can end with that error and exit 1 although every test passes, and a re-run passes (the same file alone ended with exit 1 once, 94.9 s, and with exit 0 on the next run, 58.2 s).
- **Partial automated test coverage** — nothing exercises interactions (the organiser tools, `window.prompt` flows, score entry), the `TournamentProvider` and the sync hooks, or the server functions, which are covered through their pure validators and by live checks; what is covered: `docs/platform.md`, "Tests". Not live-tested: the refusal of a screenshot upload or delete on a finished tournament.
- **Phone layout gaps:**
  - the "Delete screenshot" ✕ on a lobby thumbnail is 26 px (Setup and the running Admin's "Info for visitors", `InfoContentPanel.tsx`), under the 44 px every other phone control has. It sits over the thumbnail's corner, and a 44 px target would cover much of a 150 × 90 px picture; moving it out of the picture (a row under it) is not designed;
  - at 360 px the admin's five-tab row scrolls sideways (345 px of tabs in 332 px; it fits from 375 px, `docs/views.md`, "Phone layouts"). Viewers have four tabs and fit;
  - links inside a sentence (the standings explanation, "Tournaments" in the Admin list's footnote) stay plain text links, not 44 px targets.
- **Kings Valley refuses a field larger than its size limit, with no automatic way around it.** The ladder plays at most 14 rounds (`MAX_KINGS_VALLEY_ROUNDS`), and each round removes only part of the bottom room. So past a certain field the Final it would reach still holds more units than a room can seat, and generation refuses the tournament (`kingsValleyMaxEntrants`, `kings-valley.ts`): 24 units with rooms of 2–3 (2v2v2, 3v3v3), 32 with rooms of 3–4 (2v2v2v2), 40 with rooms of 4–5 (2v2v2v2v2), 60 players in FFA. Today the organiser's only way through is a qualification phase in front (or a lower "Advance to bracket"); 31 teams of 2v2v2 with no pooling phase, for example, can't be generated. A later build could let the ladder go faster for large fields or add the qualification step itself; nothing is designed yet.
- **Kings Valley and waterfall rounds are not covered by the fair cut.** A tournament with `cutVersion` makes the fair cut in single elimination, Semis and double-elimination winners and losers rounds; Kings Valley (a ladder cut only from the bottom room) and waterfall (the organiser's own routes by place) keep their own rules and have not been studied for room-size bias. Tournaments without `cutVersion` keep the earlier cut, in which units near the cut get through more often from a smaller room (log entry "Fair elimination cut"). Double elimination and head-to-head flex rounds were not simulated; tests cover them (their small percentile pools, often one room, are accepted).
- **A waterfall's routing is fixed by place once the tournament has started, whatever the rooms turn out to hold.** Each band sends "places a to b" of a room to a later round as authored, so the routes don't adapt to what the fair cut and absentee marking take into account: rooms of different sizes in one round (a smaller room's places are worth more, the bias the fair cut corrects elsewhere), and rooms emptied by absentees. With absentee marking as planned (`docs/roadmap.md`, "Marking a unit absent"), an absentee is ranked last of its room and routed by that rank, so a room with fewer present units than its advancing bands sends an absentee on: a room of 8 routing its top 4 on, with 5 absent, sends its 3 present units and the first absentee. Some waterfall designs could gain from fair-cut and absentee rules (routes by present places, or a share of each band decided across rooms) instead of being held rigid by the graph drawn before the start. Nothing is designed yet; open until the organiser decides how far a waterfall should adapt.
- **An absentee can only be entered as a score.** The score counts as a last place and the absentee counts in the room size of the relative score, of scaled points and of the fair cut, so a room with absentees is a hidden smaller room that the smaller-room spread doesn't see. Real events have many (`docs/real-scores.md`, "Absentees"). Marking a unit absent is in `docs/roadmap.md`.
- **Before Confirm & Start, the Bracket an admin sees marks Round 1 "NOW".** The tab is shown from Generate (read-only until the start), and the round header takes the current round as being played; a pre-start label ("Next" or none) is not built.
- **A live graph edit can move a Qual band boundary into an unresolved tie.** After the bracket is entered, tie resolutions are kept, but they only cover the old boundaries; a new boundary inside a tied cluster is then decided silently by the default order. Rare, and a changed line-up of a reached round is still caught by the replay compare.

## Accepted limitations (not building without an organiser request)
- **After a deploy, an edit typed in a tab loaded before it and not yet sent is refused** (the build check); it is typed again after the reload. A viewer tab keeps the old display rules until it is reloaded.
- **iOS Safari does not draw the Bracket's styled scrollbar** (`game-scrollbar`): it ignores scrollbar styling and shows its own overlay bar only while scrolling, so an iPad viewer (an iPhone shows one box at a time and has no such strip) gets no permanent cue that the rounds strip extends sideways. No other cue (edge fade, arrow) is built.
- **Player confirmations are readable by anyone who can read the database**, like the rest of the tournament state; only the display is admin-only (`state.confirmations`, `docs/rules.md`, "Roster changes").
- **A refilled team slot gives the slot's earlier scores to the newcomer** for the automatic confirmation (a score counts for whoever holds the member slot now, as in `scoring.ts`), so a member who joins after the slot scored shows as confirmed.
- **Two admins editing the same Setup field at the same moment: the last save wins.** A name tag ("Alex is editing") warns, and the field being typed in is protected from incoming changes, but nothing merges two edits. Two tabs of one account share one presence key, so a blur in one tab can hide the other's tag until its next heartbeat; a tab that crashes leaves its tag for up to 30 s, and its entry stays in the database (a few bytes) until that admin next focuses a field or the tournament is deleted. The waterfall graph editor has no name tag (it has no text control), and the Grand Final losers-bracket target shows its tag on the label it shares with the winners-bracket target.
- **The rules text saves when its box is left**, not while typing, so a snapshot from the database never cuts into it; an admin who closes the tab with the cursor still in the box may lose that last edit.
- **Screenshots are stored as base64 text** in `media/{id}/{imageId}` of the Realtime Database (shrunk to about 5-10 KB each, at most 4 per tournament, read once per browser), not in Firebase Storage. Plenty for the free plan; revisit if an organiser wants larger images.
- **The Tournaments page and the Admin list have no pagination or search**; every `index/` entry is read as a whole (a few hundred bytes each).
- **No extra reserves for a group stage, a fixed draw, or a waterfall without a pooling phase, once the tournament has started.** Before Confirm & Start the schedule is redrawn for the joiner instead. After it, adding a reserve is refused there (a group schedule, a fixed draw's published rounds and a pooling-less waterfall's graph are built for the exact head count); roadmap entries cover each. A no-show is replaced one for one with ⇄ Swap, until the first elimination. A joiner added after Previous, while the next round is already drawn and locked (`drawnAheadStatus` 'locked'), joins the round after that one.
- **Reloading the roster in Setup resets it to the text boxes.** Before the start, "Load roster & reserves" replaces the roster with what is typed, so earlier pre-start swaps and removals come back (their no-show entries stay), and every unit gets a new id; the schedule is then redrawn or cleared. Existing behaviour, now followed by a redraw; an organiser could be surprised by the swapped-out team returning.
- **A refused pre-start redraw leaves a short room.** Removing a unit before the start from a schedule generation can't redraw (a pooling-less waterfall built for the exact count, a count below the format's minimum) removes it anyway: Round 1 keeps the gap, as after the start, and Confirm & Start stays possible.
- **A withdrawn unit's name used again for a new entrant** (the individual swap prompt only refuses names in the current roster) makes the live graph edit's replay map old line-ups to the new person. Edge case, not fixed.
- **Bracket preview after a waterfall withdrawal.** The Bracket tab projects an undrawn waterfall round from its routing (`predecessorsOf`, `bracket.ts`), so after a withdrawal it can still list a slot for a rank the short source room no longer has (e.g. "R1.B 8th"), until the round is drawn. Cosmetic; found at the planner's final review.
- **Standings displays ignore a partly scored current-round room, but advancing with an unscored unit still uses that partial room for the real cut.** The Standings tab, Bracket column, banner rank and Rankings badge wait for a room's last score; `roomBasedComputeAdvancement` does not.
- **FFA counts that can't fill rooms of 6–8 are seated in smaller rooms** (9 → [5,4], 17 → [6,6,5]), by the organiser's decision; planned counts avoid this, organiser-entered counts can't. A few Shared-Final FFA configurations (17–19 entrants with 3–4 LB qualifiers) also fall back to it.
- **A removal can leave the current Kings Valley round, and rarely the next, below the minimum** (holding fallback; e.g. 7 [4,3] with a removal from room 2 has no seatable cut). The organiser can fill the room with a reserve.
- **Anonymous Finals exclude grand-final (race) Finals and team formats.** Race progression (`progressGrandFinalRace`, `finals.ts`) reads the two real finalists' score keys, so a placeholder-scored game would stall it; team Finals read a real `TournamentTeam` via `buildTeamMap()`, which a placeholder never has. See "Anonymous Finals matches, v1" in the log.
- **Live corrections have gaps by design** (mid-tournament corrections): no edit history or undo; no live edits of seeding method, pooling round count, Semis/Final sizes or LB qualifiers; no mid-bracket advancement edits for shared-Final double elimination; no line-up edits of scored rounds, fixed-draw pooling rounds, or in group stage, either double elimination, Kings Valley or waterfall; no moving into or out of byes. Details: `docs/rules.md`, "Line-up corrections".
- **Equal lucky-loser shares are split silently by room order.** `pickLuckyLosers` sorts candidates by relative room share and the sort is stable, so two candidates with exactly equal shares at the cut-off are separated by the earlier room, with no tie shown or resolved by the organiser. Rare with real scores; nothing flags it.
- **The followed player or team is stored once per browser**, not per tournament, so it carries over to another tournament, where the Bracket reads "Not in this bracket" until the box is emptied.
- **A tournament finished before the winners were stored has no winner on its card**; Reopen, then Finish, gives it one.
- **An ended CFP session is reported, not renewed.** Why tokens end (their lifetime, whether a login elsewhere ends older ones) is not known; a silent renewal waits on the answer from CFP (`docs/roadmap.md`, "How CFP handles login tokens"). A reload with a dead token cannot tell "expired" from "CFP unreachable": both give an anonymous tab. A role removed mid-session keeps its own message ("does not have Tournament Hub admin access"), since signing in again would not help.

## Awaiting organiser feedback
- **Two levels of "where do I stand"** — the Bracket standings column and the Standings tab. The Bracket's "Top N in standings →" badge and Rankings' pool-rank badge are gone (the standings column's exit icon says where the standings lead; Rankings says where each unit stands in the tournament, in the Bracket's round names: "Standings · #25" there, "Out in standings · #25" in the Bracket banner). Open until the organiser judges whether both remaining levels are needed.
- **Waterfall depth in Rankings.** Units eliminated in a waterfall are ordered like any other round (later round in play order first, then place, relative share, pooling standings); neither room depth nor graph level is used. A waterfall graph can take any shape, so no single depth order fits every graph (e.g. two parallel rounds such as R6B and R6C in the 32-player example, now played together as one column). Within a column the rounds are ordered by play order like any others. Open until the organiser settles a rule.
- **For an admin on a wider screen (701 px and up), the sticky "Next Round" panel covers the Bracket's sticky scrollbar** while the page is scrolled to the middle of a bracket taller than the screen (`BracketView.tsx`, the panel is `sticky bottom-2.5 z-20` and sits over the bar). Viewers have no panel and always see the bar. Open until the organiser decides whether the bar should sit above the panel for admins. A phone has no such bar: one box shows at a time and nothing scrolls sideways.

## UX ideas, organiser-interested but not requested
- **A tie for 3rd on the Rankings podium.** When two units share 3rd, the podium holds one and the second "3rd" heads the list below with the same number. Accepted for now; a fourth card or a shared card is an option.
- **Waterfall graph editor** (current editor: click-to-route table):
  - reusable saved templates (a named copy of the graph text plus a storage place) — partly served now: "Copy settings from a past tournament" (Setup panel) copies a past tournament's whole graph text (and every other setting) onto a new one, from the tournament list or a pasted link/id; still no named-template list independent of a past tournament;
  - starter-template wizard (pick a shape and a player count, get a graph that adds up);
  - drag-and-drop canvas (largest option, not discarded);
  - showing every validation problem at once (`validateAndOrderWaterfallGraph` reports only the first);
  - rooms of different sizes inside one round (the text format only allows `N x size`);
  - the qualification table (`Qual`) for Swiss or group-stage standings, which keep a single entry round;
  - rank-order seating inside a destination instead of the balanced draw, or routing a band to a specific room;
  - the qualification table drawn as a node in a graph diagram (there is no diagram, only the editor's round list).
- **Redesign leftovers the organiser left open** (parked, not requested): the exit icon's glyph (the boards' arrow with stacked lines is built; a list-shaped glyph and a pill-style pop-up are alternatives); the emoji marks ✋ ⚖ ⚠ on rows and banners (not replaced by drawn icons); the Setup control changes of the Q17 board (toggle buttons, steppers, a roster list with add buttons: the text boxes stay); the folded-round ideas "your place, big" and "standings after the round".
- **Header band.** The loaded tournament's tabs sit on a lighter band as built, confirmed by the organiser; the organiser may want it improved later, no direction yet.
- **Final podium colours.** Gold, silver and bronze on the 1st–3rd rows of the Final card (needs colour sampling first).
