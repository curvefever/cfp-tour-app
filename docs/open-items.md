## Schedule, formats and Custom bracket
- **Limitation — A Custom bracket's graph can't be saved while the drawn rounds aren't a prefix of its rounds, or while a press is reopened.** In dependency play (`docs/rules.md`, "Dependency play") the rounds drawn so far are a prefix of the bracket's rounds in the common shape; when they are not (one round of a column is sent on and drawn what another round, earlier in the list, still waits for), `applyWaterfallGraphEdit` refuses with a message naming the round to play first, because the save's replay and its locked prefix assume a prefix. A reopened press is refused too, naming its rounds: press Next Round to send them on again, then save. The refusal lifts as soon as the round is drawn or the press is sent on.
- **Limitation — The graph editor's column hints can suggest a column the save then refuses.** `RunningWaterfallPanel.tsx` builds its started columns from the rounds drawn so far, while the save counts the undrawn rounds of a column with any drawn round as started too, so the editor can offer the number of such a column. The save's message says why.
- **Gap — Kings Valley and waterfall rounds are not covered by the fair cut.** A tournament with `cutVersion` makes the fair cut in single elimination, Semis and double-elimination winners and losers rounds; Kings Valley (a ladder cut only from the bottom room) and waterfall (the organiser's own routes by place) keep their own rules and have not been studied for room-size bias. In a Custom bracket the routes stay fixed by place whatever the rooms turn out to hold: rooms of different sizes in one round send the same places on (a smaller room's places are worth more, the bias the fair cut corrects elsewhere). Absentees are handled (a Custom bracket of `absenceVersion` 2 never lets an absentee take a seat above its room's last place, `docs/rules.md`, "Absentees"); what stays open is a band's seats shared across rooms by present size, which the organiser parked. Nothing is designed yet. Tournaments without `cutVersion` keep the earlier cut, in which units near the cut get through more often from a smaller room (log entry "Fair elimination cut"). Double elimination and head-to-head flex rounds were not simulated; tests cover them (their small percentile pools, often one room, are accepted).
- **Limitation — The numbering of a Custom bracket's default round names follows local actions only.** The Setup renumbers "Round n" names when the pooling rounds change, but only for an edit made in this tab (a Setup field, Load or Clear roster, Copy settings; `docs/rules.md`, "Round names"). So: (1) a roster change made outside Setup before the start (the Players tab) changes a group stage's or a Swiss's round count without renumbering; the names catch up at the next Setup edit. (2) `updateSetup` works out the renumbering from the Setup the tab last rendered, so two edits in one tick before a re-render can read a stale graph. (3) A local edit made in the short moment before another tab's renumbered graph and number arrive can renumber a second time. All three are rare and accepted; renumbering from the synced Setup instead moved the pictures twice when two tabs were open.
- **Gap — Copy settings moves pictures by the source-to-target rename map, so pictures this tournament already had under those names move too.** After a copy, `renameNotePictureRounds` renames the copied rounds (the source's numbering to this tournament's) over the whole `info/notePictures`, not only the copied pictures: a picture the target already held under "Round 4" moves with the copied one. The move runs only when the source has pictures. Rare (a copy is normally the first thing done to a new tournament); a precise move would need the copy to return the ids it placed.
- **Gap — A live graph edit can move a Qual or Groups band boundary into an unresolved tie.** After the bracket is entered, tie resolutions are kept, but they only cover the old boundaries; a new boundary inside a tied cluster is then decided silently by the default order. Rare, and a changed line-up of a reached round is still caught by the replay compare.
- **Gap — Kings Valley refuses a field larger than its size limit, with no automatic way around it.** The ladder plays at most 14 rounds (`MAX_KINGS_VALLEY_ROUNDS`), and each round removes only part of the bottom room. So past a certain field the Final it would reach still holds more units than a room can seat, and generation refuses the tournament (`kingsValleyMaxEntrants`, `kings-valley.ts`): 24 units with rooms of 2–3 (2v2v2, 3v3v3), 32 with rooms of 3–4 (2v2v2v2), 40 with rooms of 4–5 (2v2v2v2v2), 60 players in FFA. Today the organiser's only way through is a qualification phase in front (or a lower "Advance to bracket"); 31 teams of 2v2v2 with no pooling phase, for example, can't be generated. A later build could let the ladder go faster for large fields or add the qualification step itself; nothing is designed yet.
- **Limitation — FFA counts that can't fill rooms of 6–8 are seated in smaller rooms** (9 → [5,4], 17 → [6,6,5]), by the organiser's decision; planned counts avoid this, organiser-entered counts can't. A few Shared-Final FFA configurations (17–19 entrants with 3–4 LB qualifiers) also fall back to it. To revisit with "Room-size policy" (`docs/roadmap.md`).
- **Limitation — A removal can leave the current Kings Valley round, and rarely the next, below the minimum** (holding fallback; e.g. 7 [4,3] with a removal from room 2 has no seatable cut). The organiser can fill the room with a reserve.
- **Limitation — Anonymous Finals exclude grand-final (race) Finals and team formats.** Race progression (`progressGrandFinalRace`, `finals.ts`) reads the two real finalists' score keys, so a placeholder-scored game would stall it; team Finals read a real `TournamentTeam` via `buildTeamMap()`, which a placeholder never has. See "Anonymous Finals matches, v1" in the log.
- **Limitation — An upper and lower bracket takes 16 at 17–23 in FFA and 32 above 32.** The bracket has two or four rooms (FFA 12–16 and 24–32; the other formats' ranges in `docs/rules.md`, "Upper and lower bracket"), so the counts between the two shapes enter the lower one and the units over the largest are out at the pooling phase. A third shape for the gap was tried on paper: `Round n+1 - Lower` is not seatable at most of those counts. Nor is there a fair cut across rooms of different sizes (a room of 7 and a room of 8 send the same top places on), or a shared Final variant. The two fixed shapes are what a flexible upper and lower bracket would lift (`docs/roadmap.md`, "A flexible upper and lower bracket").
- **Limitation — A hand-written Custom graph can have a round no one reaches.** A round fed only by ranks past the last places of short rooms (`docs/rules.md`, "Waterfall bracket", the short entry round) keeps its declared shape, sends nobody on and blocks at runtime with `too-few-units`. The built-in upper and lower bracket never produces one.
- **Idea — Waterfall graph editor** (current editor: click-to-route table):
  - reusable saved templates (a named copy of the graph text plus a storage place) — partly served now: "Copy settings from a past tournament" (Setup panel) copies a past tournament's whole graph text (and every other setting) onto a new one, from the tournament list or a pasted link/id; still no named-template list independent of a past tournament;
  - starter-template wizard (pick a shape and a player count, get a graph that adds up);
  - drag-and-drop canvas (largest option, not discarded);
  - showing every validation problem at once (`validateAndOrderWaterfallGraph` reports only the first);
  - rooms of different sizes inside one round (the text format only allows `N x size`; the short entry round, `docs/rules.md`, "Waterfall bracket", covers a round a few seats short, which is what the upper and lower bracket uses);
  - the qualification table (`Qual`) for a Swiss in a hand-made Custom graph: the engine takes the line (the built-in upper and lower bracket, and what "Adjust rounds by hand" writes from it, have one) but the Custom bracket's editor does not offer it on a graph with no line, which keeps a single entry round and today's draw; group-stage standings have the `Groups` line;
  - rank-order seating inside a destination instead of the balanced draw, or routing a band to a specific room;
  - the qualification table drawn as a node in a graph diagram (there is no diagram, only the editor's round list).

## Scoring, standings and ties
- **Gap — Group stage per-match standings keep to the minimum** (`docs/rules.md`, "Group stage standings"): no minimum number of matches to rank or qualify (a unit with every match void for removals sorts last with no total, and stays in the exit table; one whose opponents were only absent takes a win for each), no make-up matches and no replaying a void match, and a swapped-in reserve inherits the seat's whole line (there is no way to start it fresh). Swiss and qualification tables keep total points and bye wins, and match results are refused there (`docs/roadmap.md`).
- **Gap — `totalScore` of a Best-of pooling round sums the wins.** `materializeStandings` adds each round's `score`, which is the games won on a Best-of round. Nothing reads `totalScore` today (the tables show points and the tie-break), so nothing is wrong; a reader that wants the score total of such a round would need the totals of the counted games.
- **Decision pending — Ranking units eliminated in a group stage with groups of different sizes.** In live tournament `1793555835749`, Curveblast finished 9th with the worst results of every team, because it was in a group of 3: units eliminated in the same round are ordered by their group place first, and a 3rd of a group of 3 ranks above the 4ths of the groups of 4. The organiser would rather rank them by relative score when group sizes differ, but keep the group place as the first distinction when every group is the same size, and wants to talk it through before any change (no different behaviours without a reason). The organiser decides it when this build is planned, not before. Same question as the group exit's (`docs/roadmap.md`, "A group exit cut that compares teams across groups"): how to compare units across groups of different sizes.
- **Decision pending — Should a 0 in Survival Teams block until answered?** Absentee marking asks "absent or played?" for every 0 and holds the room until answered. In Survival Teams (One score per team) a real 0 is common, especially with two teams, so there the decision bar is a non-blocking indicator and an unanswered 0 counts as a score of 0, not an absence (`docs/rules.md`, "Absentees"). The organiser wants to review whether that is right or whether a 0 should block there too.
- **Limitation — Standings displays ignore a partly scored current-round room, but advancing with an unscored unit still uses that partial room for the real cut.** The Standings tab, Bracket column, banner rank and Rankings badge wait for a room's last score; `roomBasedComputeAdvancement` does not.
- **Limitation — Equal lucky-loser shares are split silently by room order.** `pickLuckyLosers` sorts candidates by relative room share and the sort is stable, so two candidates with exactly equal shares at the cut-off are separated by the earlier room, with no tie shown or resolved by the organiser. Rare with real scores; nothing flags it.
- **Limitation — Two simultaneous cross-group ties at the chance spots are one cluster.** `group-exit-cutoff` merges every tie of the selection, so the organiser picks one more name than strictly needed (`isTieResolved` wants n − 1 picks of the merged list). It never asks for fewer.

## Seeding and draws
- **Idea — Pair likely absentees together in head-to-head draws.** Rooms of 2 keep their draw: likely absentees are not placed there (`docs/rules.md`, "Absentees"), so two of them can meet two present units and leave them with a walkover each. Pairing the likely absentees together would leave the other rooms full; it mostly concerns Swiss and pooling rounds of head-to-head formats. Not built; for after the tournament.
- **Limitation — A group stage's exit draws without placing likely absentees.** The first bracket round after the last group round is drawn by the exit order with the group-mates apart (`avoidSameGroupInFirstBracketRound`), which works over the whole list, so units absent from the last group round are not spread over the rooms there. Every other draw of rooms of 3 or more places them (`docs/seeding.md`).
- **Limitation — A group stage's total exit seeds the bracket by direct tiers, then the chance spots in the order they were taken.** `docs/rules.md`, "Group stage exit": the bracket seeding order beyond that is the existing one. A waterfall graph cannot route group ranks (a graph routes qualification-table ranks only), so no per-group routing exists.

## Roster, reserves, absentees and corrections
- **Limitation — The strict odd-count check of a team removal reads `curRound`.** `removeTeamUnit`'s check at `RankingsRoster.tsx:268` looks at the round `curRound` is in, so it misses a team seated in another round in play in dependency play; column play already has the same gap for a later round of a column. It only applies to the strict odd-count strategy.
- **Gap — Absentees are asked only in a round in play, and only for a score of 0.** A 0 of an earlier round is answered through Previous; a score typed as 1 for an absentee (as some events did) is a score. The Final and the grand final never ask, and Save your Buddy and One score per team are not weighted per player (the room average absorbs a lower target there). Removing or replacing an absentee is manual; "Redraw this round without them" while no score is in yet is not built. The ABS cell in the standings is display only in every format but a group stage with per-match standings (there the absentee has a counted match worth 0), and the room's points header shows nothing for it outside that stage. The absentee simulation of the room-size fairness study (`scripts/seeding-simulation/`) is not built. An absentee marked in the first round of a tournament is not told apart from a no-show once removed, except through `hasPlayedAnyMatch` (a unit absent all along lands in the ABSENT panel's "from the start" rows, not DNF).
- **Gap — Past rooms show a team's current members.** A member swap changes the team's members for every round, so a past room names who is in the slot now, not who played it (scores are stored by slot and stay; a rename behaves the same). There is no per-round member history.
- **Limitation — No extra reserves for a group stage, a fixed draw, or a waterfall without a pooling phase, once the tournament has started.** Before Confirm & Start the schedule is redrawn for the joiner instead. After it, adding a reserve is refused there (a group schedule, a fixed draw's published rounds and a pooling-less waterfall's graph are built for the exact head count); roadmap entries cover each. A no-show is replaced one for one with ⇄ Swap, until the first elimination (in a team format one player of a team can be replaced at any time with ⇄ on the member, `docs/rules.md`, "Individual reserves and member swaps"). A joiner added after Previous, while the next round is already drawn and locked (`drawnAheadStatus` 'locked'), joins the round after that one.
- **Limitation — Live corrections have gaps by design** (mid-tournament corrections): no edit history or undo; no live edits of seeding method, pooling round count, Semis/Final sizes or LB qualifiers; no mid-bracket advancement edits for shared-Final double elimination; no line-up edits of scored rounds, fixed-draw pooling rounds, or in group stage, either double elimination, Kings Valley or waterfall (the one exception is the single move of a unit marked back after the draw, `docs/rules.md`, "Absentees"); no moving into or out of byes. Details: `docs/rules.md`, "Line-up corrections". The organiser has asked for the swap and the undo parts: `docs/roadmap.md`, "Line-up corrections in every round" and "Undoing an organiser decision".
- **Limitation — Reloading the roster in Setup resets it to the text boxes.** Before the start, "Load roster & reserves" replaces the roster with what is typed, so earlier pre-start swaps and removals come back (their no-show entries stay), and every unit gets a new id; the schedule is then redrawn or cleared. Existing behaviour, now followed by a redraw; an organiser could be surprised by the swapped-out team returning. To revisit with "One place for the roster" (`docs/roadmap.md`).
- **Limitation — A refused pre-start redraw leaves a short room.** Removing a unit before the start from a schedule generation can't redraw (a pooling-less waterfall built for the exact count, a count below the format's minimum) removes it anyway: Round 1 keeps the gap, as after the start, and Confirm & Start stays possible. A group stage is the exception: its schedule is cleared and the organiser generates again.
- **Limitation — Player confirmations are readable by anyone who can read the database**, like the rest of the tournament state; only the display is admin-only (`state.confirmations`, `docs/rules.md`, "Roster changes").
- **Limitation — A refilled team slot gives the slot's earlier scores to the newcomer** for the automatic confirmation (a score counts for whoever holds the member slot now, as in `scoring.ts`), so a member who joins after the slot scored shows as confirmed.

## Tournaments, Admin and Setup
- **Limitation — Two admins editing the same Setup field at the same moment: the last save wins.** A name tag ("Alex is editing") warns, and the field being typed in is protected from incoming changes, but nothing merges two edits. Two tabs of one account share one presence key, so a blur in one tab can hide the other's tag until its next heartbeat; a tab that crashes leaves its tag for up to 30 s, and its entry stays in the database (a few bytes) until that admin next focuses a field or the tournament is deleted. The waterfall graph editor has no name tag (it has no text control), and the Grand Final losers-bracket target shows its tag on the label it shares with the winners-bracket target.
- **Limitation — The rules text saves when its box is left**, not while typing, so a snapshot from the database never cuts into it; an admin who closes the tab with the cursor still in the box may lose that last edit.
- **Limitation — The Tournaments page and the Admin list have no pagination or search**; every `index/` entry is read as a whole (a few hundred bytes each).
- **Limitation — A tournament finished before the winners were stored has no winner on its card**; Reopen, then Finish, gives it one.

## Platform, sync and tests
- **Gap — `AppShell.render.test.tsx` prints "Timeout calling onTaskUpdate" from the vitest worker** when the whole render sweep runs (about 70 s on the review machine). The tests all pass; it looks like the sweep starving the worker's reporting channel, so a runner that treats an unhandled error as a failure may need the sweep split or its timeout raised. It depends on the machine's load: under load the file can end with that error and exit 1 although every test passes, and a re-run passes (the same file alone ended with exit 1 once, 94.9 s, and with exit 0 on the next run, 58.2 s).
- **Gap — Timing-sensitive render tests.** `SessionExpired.render.test.tsx` (and once `NewVersionBanner.render.test.tsx`) sometimes fail in a full `pnpm test` run under load and pass alone; a failure there is not a sign the change broke them. Making their waits robust is not done.
- **Gap — Partial automated test coverage** — nothing exercises interactions (the organiser tools, `window.prompt` flows, score entry), the `TournamentProvider` and the sync hooks, or the server functions, which are covered through their pure validators and by live checks; what is covered: `docs/platform.md`, "Tests". The engine tests do check a tournament's consistency after corrections (`__tests__/consistency.ts`). Not live-tested: the refusal of a screenshot upload or delete on a finished tournament.
- **Limitation — After a deploy, an edit typed in a tab loaded before it and not yet sent is refused** (the build check); it is typed again after the reload. A viewer tab keeps the old display rules until it is reloaded.
- **Limitation — An ended CFP session is reported, not renewed.** Why tokens end (their lifetime, whether a login elsewhere ends older ones) is not known; a silent renewal waits on the answer from CFP (`docs/roadmap.md`, "How CFP handles login tokens"). A reload with a dead token cannot tell "expired" from "CFP unreachable": both give an anonymous tab. A role removed mid-session keeps its own message ("does not have Tournament Hub admin access"), since signing in again would not help.
- **Limitation — Screenshots are stored as base64 text** in `media/{id}/{imageId}` of the Realtime Database (shrunk to about 5-10 KB each, at most 4 per tournament, read once per browser), not in Firebase Storage. Plenty for the free plan; revisit if an organiser wants larger images.
