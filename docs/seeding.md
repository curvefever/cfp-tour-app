# Room seeding

How units land in rooms from round to round (`src/domain/tournament/seeding.ts`, plus `fixed-draws.ts`). Read when a task touches transitions between rounds, reseeding, or Bracket's future-round projections, or when explaining "how did this player end up in this room". Build history and reasoning: search `HANDOFF_LOG.md` for "Diversity + rematch-aware room reseeding" and the entries named below.

## Which mechanism runs where
| Mechanism | Used for |
|---|---|
| `randomSeed` | Round 1 only: a shuffle via the injectable `RandomSource`. |
| `swissFoldPair` | Adaptive Swiss rounds: fold-pairs by rank (1 vs `half+1`, …), with a rematch-avoidance swap when one clears both pairs. |
| `tieredSeed` | Every ordinary room-based transition: no-elim warm-up, qual-table, single-elimination and team-format cuts, up to the round that seeds Semis. |
| `tieredBracketSeed` | Double elimination's winners/losers-bracket routing (race and shared-Final variants). |
| `sequentialSeed` | Kings Valley's ladder moves, including the first hop into Kings Valley: slices an ordered list into declared room sizes, no reordering. |
| Fixed draws | Qual-table/Swiss with draw publication `fixed`: the whole pooling schedule is computed at generation. |
| `distributeTierMajorPool` | Bracket's future-round **display** projection only (`projectFutureRoundSlots`). |
| `snakeSeed` | Not called by the app; kept as a tested primitive. |

## `tieredSeed`
Survivors are grouped into rank tiers across all rooms (tier 0 = every room's winner, tier 1 = every runner-up, …). A room's dominance margin (`pct`, score share) only breaks ties inside a tier. The next round's rooms are filled one wave (a tier, or part of one) at a time; each wave is assigned to rooms by minimising a weighted cost of:
- repeat pairings against everyone already placed and all prior rounds, recency-weighted (a pair that met last round costs ~10% more than one that met long ago), and
- how far each room's running total lands from balanced.

A room's tiers come from `buildAdvancementTiers`: a unit's tier is its place in its room, and units that shared a place (shared points, `docs/rules.md`, "Scoring") share that place's tier, in every pooling round.

The weighting tapers from diversity-heavy (warm-up and early cuts) to balance-heavy by the round that seeds Semis. Match history is kept in `state.roomHistory` (pair key → last round index shared), also written by double elimination. A re-draw ("Next Round" after "Previous") first rewinds the discarded draws' entries, and entries at or after the round being drawn are ignored, so a stale or planned entry is never treated as a past repeat.

**Smaller-room seats** (`smaller-room-seats.ts`). Under Standard points the same place earns the same points in any room size, so a seat in a room smaller than the round's largest is worth about half a point a round. In a **counted pooling round** (a qualification or Swiss round that counts toward the standings, `isCountedPoolingRound`) with rooms of different sizes, `tieredSeed` therefore fills the smaller rooms apart from the larger ones: `splitForSmallerRooms` picks who takes the smaller-room seats, the units with the fewest such seats so far first (`smallerRoomSeatsBefore`: derived from the line-ups of the counted pooling rounds before the round being drawn, never stored, so a re-draw or a removal needs no rewind; "smaller" is read from the line-up as drawn, a bye is no seat). Among the units tied at the boundary count the picks are strided over the seeding order, so the smaller rooms keep a mix of tiers. The two groups are then filled by the same wave logic, each over its own rooms (`fillRoomsByWaves`; the balance mean is per group). A late joiner (below) comes last for smaller-room seats in the round they enter and gets one only if every other unit already has one; from the next round their real count applies. Seat counts end within 1 of each other whenever the shape allows; rematch avoidance and room balance work inside that. A two-seat smaller room (3v3v3) can come out as two units of one tier: the balance term allows "1 + 1" next to "0 + 2", accepted at review. Warm-ups, rounds that don't count, elimination, waterfall, double elimination and Kings Valley keep the plain wave fill.

Behaviour worth knowing when explaining it to players: the balance term is often an exact tie (whenever a wave's candidates share a tier, and for any two-room destination starting from zero), so the taper acts almost like pure diversity in common shapes; balance only visibly matters with 3+ destination rooms. A pure `balance` seeding override (diversity weight exactly 0) ignores repeat history entirely, so it can produce more repeat opponents than a balance-heavy blend. Details: "Seeding system explainer + seeding-behavior findings" in the log.

Units with no room in the round just played are not tiered by a result. A bye keeps tier 0 with share 1 (it counts as a win). A late joiner (`lateJoinersOf`, `late-joiners.ts`: roster units neither seated in that pooling round nor on its bye) goes to the middle tier, `floor(largest room / 2)` (rooms of 7: the 4th place; 8: the 5th; head-to-head: the losers' tier), with the mean share, `rooms / units seated` (`sourceRoom` 0); from their second round on their own result tiers them. No pooling bye choice (`selectPoolingBye`, the generic bye slice) takes a joiner unless nobody else is left.

## `tieredBracketSeed`
The same wave/cost/taper approach for double elimination, where a target round can pool units from several source rounds (a losers-bracket round mixes fresh winners-bracket drops with the previous losers-bracket round's survivors). Each contribution is tagged with its room-rank tier when pushed, so the pooled entries are directly comparable. The taper (`doubleEliminationApproachProgress`) is computed per bracket side, with the terminal round at 1.

## `swissFoldPair` and late joiners
A joiner added during a Swiss round is appended last in the fold order (in add order), so index k pairs them with index k + half, a middle-ranked unit; they never get the bye.

## Fixed draws (`fixed-draws.ts`)
Computed once from roster order, never from results. Swiss uses `circleMethodSchedule` (zero repeat pairings); qual-table uses a greedy wave fill with `assignWaveToRooms` and all tiers equal. A counted round with mixed room sizes is split as above, with the seat counts of the counted rounds built so far and no late joiners (a fixed draw refuses them after the start); rounds that don't count are filled as before. Stored per round on `TournamentRound.fixedRoomAssignments` (not in `state.assignments`, which means "round reached"); `transitions.ts` uses it directly. `roomHistory`/`poolingByeCounts` for the whole schedule are folded in at generation, and rebuilt after a removal or swap patches the unreached rounds.

## Future-round projection (Bracket display only)
`projectFutureRoundSlots` (`bracket.ts`) labels future slots with their expected origin. Where the target round has several rooms the real draw spreads the units over them, so a slot names the source round and the place only ("Round 2 · 1st", "Round 5-1 · 2nd", "Round 5 Upper · 3rd": `roomKnown` false on `room-rank` and `waterfall-rank`); a one-room target (fixed by routing) keeps the exact origin ("Room 2A, Rank 1", "Winner of Room B", a waterfall slot names its source round by the round's own label, "Round 5 Upper · Room B, Rank 2" or, for a one-room source, "Semi A, Rank 3", "★ Lucky loser (any room)", "Dropped from WB Round 6", "Qualifier from standings (seed TBD)"). The candidate pool is built tier-major and spread across target rooms by `distributeTierMajorPool` (round-robin with a rotating start), so every projected room shows exactly its declared size and a spread of ranks and source rooms. Accepted trade-off: the real transition (`tieredSeed`/`tieredBracketSeed`) can place units differently once the round is reached. Where the origin can't be known ahead of time the slot shows "—", and every round built on it does too. Group Stage and fixed-draw rounds show their real pairings; Kings Valley future rounds show room sizes only.

**The expected draw** (`expected-draw.ts`). `expectedNextColumn(state)` returns the draw of every round of the next column as Next Round would make it, as soon as it is determined: the tournament has started, there is a next column, nothing of it is drawn or published yet (no `assignments`, `fixedRoomAssignments` or group-stage `matches`), every contested room of the column in play has every score in (`isRoomScored`, `scoring.ts`), and `advanceTournamentRound` returns `advanced` (a pending tie or any refusal gives null). It runs the real advance on the state, which is pure, and reads the next column's rooms, line-ups and byes from the result; nothing is written. The Bracket shows those rounds as future rounds with the units' names and an "EXPECTED" tag, to admins and viewers alike (`docs/views.md`, item 5), and the real round replaces them when drawn. Only the next column: later rounds keep the place labels above.
