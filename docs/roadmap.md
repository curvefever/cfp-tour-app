# Curve Fever Pro Tournament Hub — Future Projects Roadmap

This file contains future projects and architectural questions not yet scoped or built. An entry moves to `HANDOFF_LOG.md` once built (with the current-state docs updated), then is removed from this file. Smaller, shorter-horizon gaps and decisions are in `docs/open-items.md` instead.

---

## Architectural questions without yet-requested implementation

- **Players confirm their own participation in the app** — raised by the organiser 2026-10-18 with the confirmation marks. Today only an admin marks a player (`state.confirmations`, by name; a score also confirms). The later version lets a player confirm themselves, which needs a way to tie a signed-in account to a roster name, a write path that is not admin-only, and a decision on what an unconfirmed player at the deadline means (reserves, a smaller format). Nothing in generation reads confirmations yet; "generate with confirmed players only" and a "remove unconfirmed" button belong to the same project.

- **Kings Valley early-exit / skip-ahead** — the part of the original item 7 that the waterfall/rank-band project (2026-09-21) explicitly did *not* cover: `kingsValleyBracketPhase`/`kingsValleyComputeAdvancement` run an unconditional promote/stay/demote/eliminate cycle every round with no early-exit branch, and share no primitive with the `winnersTo`/`pendingBracketSeeds` machinery the waterfall bracket reuses. Raised by the organiser (2026-10-01) as worth planning: cumulative with the ladder, e.g. the top room's winner qualifies straight for the Final in the same round the bottom room loses two. Kept separate from the Kings Valley room-sizing fix of the same date; that fix's per-room move solver (`kings-valley.ts`) is where a "straight to the Final" outflow band would plug in, as a second out-of-ladder outflow beside elimination.

- **Auto-derived waterfall bracket schemas / letting the app choose the best format** — v1 of the waterfall/rank-band bracket is fully organiser-authored (a `ROUNDS:`/`ROUTES:` text mini-language, generation-time only). The organiser noted (2026-09-21) that a later project could have the app derive such a structure from the field size itself, or judge whether a waterfall-style format is the best fit for a given field at all. Not designed, not precluded — the mini-language's parsed graph is the natural target representation for any future generator. See "Waterfall / rank-band bracket phase" in `HANDOFF_LOG.md`.

- **"What do I need?" line for a followed player during pooling** — raised during the Standings rethink (2026-10-05) and parked by the organiser: during qualification/Swiss/group-stage rounds, tell a followed player what result in their current room would keep them inside the qualifying cut (e.g. "needs to finish top 2 in Room C to stay safe"). Speculative by nature: the answer depends on every other room's results, so it would need a clear definition first (guaranteed vs likely, worst case over the other rooms) and wording that never promises more than the standings can know. Natural home: Bracket's follow banner, beside the standings rank.

- **Podium in the downloadable rankings image** — raised by the organiser 2026-10-15 after phase D: once a tournament is finished, the PNG from "Download image" should show the podium as the Rankings page does (second · first · third cards above the list) instead of the plain three-column list from 1st (the visual redesign decided on no podium there, Q15). Needs a canvas drawing of the three cards (gold, silver and bronze bars; `finalStanding().podium` already holds them in order) and a taller header in `rankings-image-layout.ts`.

- **Flexible FFA room sizes (4 to 10), Final included** — raised by the organiser 2026-10-16 when the Semis was allowed to shrink to the units advancing (12–16 in FFA). Rooms of 6–8 stay the preferred sizes, but the organiser wants the app to accept anything from 4 to 10 when a count needs it, and possibly a Final other than 8. Touches `roomSize` in `formats.ts`, `distributeRooms` (`room-distribution.ts`), the Semis floor (`getMinimumBracketUnits`, `schedule-generation.ts`) and every place that reads `roomSize.min`/`max` as a hard limit (live line-up edits, reserve over-cap confirm, Kings Valley's size limit).

- **Late joiners in a group stage** — raised by the organiser 2026-10-16 with the late-joiner build. Adding a reserve is refused in a group stage because membership and the round-robin schedule are built at generation. Taking one needs the groups rebuilt mid-stage (a joiner into the smallest group, the future fixtures regenerated, the standings kept). Touches `pooling.ts`, `addReserveUnit` and the group-stage rounds.
- **Late joiners in a fixed draw** — raised 2026-10-16. A fixed draw publishes every pooling round at generation, so a joiner has no seat. Taking one means patching the published future rounds (`patchFutureFixedDrawRounds`, `fixed-draws.ts`, as a removal or swap already does) so the joiner is paired in each of them without breaking the room-history guarantees the draw was built for.
- **A waterfall without a pooling phase taking a joiner** — raised 2026-10-16. Such a waterfall's graph is built for the exact entrant count. The organiser wants a joiner accepted while nobody is eliminated, with the graph adjusted by hand afterwards ("Edit graph"). Open questions: where the joiner is seated, and what the graph editor shows for a first round that is over its declared size.

- **Waterfall dependency play: a round starts as soon as its own feeders finish** — raised by the organiser 2026-10-17 with simultaneous waterfall columns. Columns are played as one step: every round of a column must finish before the next column starts. In tournament `1792226417678`, Semi A (fed by Round 5 Upper) and Round 6 share a column, so Semi A holds up Round 7, although only Round 6 feeds Round 7 (the 32-player example has the same shape: Semi A beside R6B/R6C, R7A fed only by those two). Dependency play would let several rounds be live at once, each drawn when its pool is complete and advanced on its own, which replaces the single current round (`curRound`) with a per-round status everywhere the current round is read: score entry, Next Round and Previous, line-up corrections, withdrawals, live graph edits, NOW and folding in the Bracket.
