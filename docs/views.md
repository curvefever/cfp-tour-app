# Views and viewer design

What each tab does today, and the principles viewer-facing work is judged against. Read when a task touches the UI (`src/features/*`).

## Design intent for viewer-facing tabs
The standard for new viewer-facing work. Every player plays online, alone, at their own computer: no live events, no audience, no stream overlay.

**Three viewer questions**, in order of importance: (1) **where do I play**: my room and who I'm facing, with zero clicks; (2) **what's at stake for me**: am I inside the qualifying cut, and by how much; (3) **how is everyone else doing**: the fun of following the whole tournament.

**Each viewer tab has one job.** **Tournaments** lists the tournaments that are on and is where a visit lands when no tournament loads. **Info** is one tournament's date, format, lobby screenshots and rules. **Bracket** answers all three at a glance: rooms and results for (1) and (3), and during pooling a compact standings column plus the follow banner's rank for (2). **Standings** is the full detail behind that column: every figure, the tie-breaker and tie marks, for anyone who wants to check the numbers; it exists only when the format has a standings phase. **Rankings** is who's still in and the final order, plus roster management for admins (before the tournament starts the same tab is "Players", the roster so far). Past tournaments are Finished ones on the Tournaments page; there is no Archive.

**Glance in Bracket, detail in Standings.** Bracket shows only what a player needs at a glance (rank, name, points, the cut); explanations, extra columns and tie marks belong in Standings.

- **No staggered reveal**: every score reaches every connected viewer at the same instant.
- **"Follow a player," never "who are you."** Anyone can follow any name.
- **A collapsed round is a display state, not a data reduction.** Reopening shows exactly the detail the current round's display uses.
- **Future rounds default to fully visible**, not collapsed or faded; a viewer may fold any round, but the app never does so by default.
- **One typeface.** The whole app uses Asap in weights 400, 600, 700 and 800 (Google Fonts, linked in `routes/__root.tsx`, set on `body` in `styles.css`); the Rankings PNG draws with it too (`rankings-image.ts` loads the weights before drawing). The recurring text styles (page title 28/700, view label 18/800 caps, column title, room header, panel label) are class strings in `components/ui/typography.ts`.

## Two looks
The viewer tabs (Tournaments, Info, Bracket, Standings, Rankings/Players) use the **game look**: navy grounds, solid blue room headers, chunky buttons, a result stripe beside every name. The header and tab bar and the organiser's screens (Admin: the tournament list, Setup, running Admin) use the **middle look**: navy-petrol ground, bordered rounded panels, small caps section labels. One set of components serves both: they read the semantic `--app-*` tokens (`styles.css`), and a `data-look` attribute (`game` or `middle`) on a wrapper switches them. `AppShell` sets it from the active tab (Admin = middle, every other tab = game) and pins the header and tab bar to the middle look. The raw brand palette is `--color-cfp-*` (the brief's codes, exact); components use the semantic tokens or the palette's named colours, never colour literals (the Rankings PNG draws on a canvas and is the one exception until it is rebuilt). Per look: buttons are 4 px (middle) or 2 px (game) radius and weight 700 (middle) or 800 (game), with a darker bottom edge; panels have a 1 px border and no shadow; form controls are 40 px high at 15 px, with sentence-case 14/700 field labels. Where the boards and the brief differ, the brief wins.

## Tabs
Tab rules live in `shell/tabs.ts` (`visibleTabs()`, `effectiveTab()`, `loadedPhase()`), from the loaded tournament's phase: Tournaments is always listed; Info and Rankings/Players only while a tournament is loaded; Bracket only once it has started (ongoing, or finished after being played); Standings only then and with a standings phase; Admin only for a signed-in admin. A stored tab that is not available is displayed as a fallback (Standings → Bracket, anything else → the tournament's Info, or the list when nothing is loaded) and never overwritten, so an admin whose session restores a moment after load returns to Admin by itself. Viewer-facing empty states never point at Admin. The **header** (`AppHeader.tsx`, middle look, sticky) holds the "CFP Tour Hub" button (opens Tournaments), the tabs in plain words (no emoji) in `nav aria-label='Tournament sections'`, and for a signed-in admin "Signed in · <role>" (`AccountMenu.tsx`), a button that opens a one-item menu "Sign out (<name>)". Tournaments stands apart; the loaded tournament's tabs sit together on a lighter band (`role='group'`, "This tournament"); with no tournament loaded, a divider sets Admin apart. It shows no tournament name and no round counter. **Exit** lives only on Info, as the outlined button "✕ Exit tournament view". Every page opens with a **heading** (`PageHeading.tsx`): the tournament's name (or "Tournaments" / "Admin" for the lists) with the view's name in small caps underneath ("Bracket", "Standings", "Rankings" or "Players", "Info", "Setup" before the start and "Admin" after).

**What loads on a visit** (`visit.ts`, `resolveVisit`): a link `?t=<id>` always opens that tournament, or, when a viewer cannot see it (a draft, a production test run, deleted, an unknown id), shows "This tournament isn't available." and opens Tournaments. Without a link: exactly one visible tournament Starting soon or Ongoing loads (Info before Confirm & Start, Bracket after); several open the Tournaments page; none loads the most recently finished tournament if it finished under 24 hours ago, on Rankings; otherwise Tournaments. **Exit** unloads the tournament, opens Tournaments and sticks across reloads, remembered with the phase at Exit: it is forgotten as soon as a different tournament would load, an exited tournament that was Starting soon loads again once it is Ongoing, and one exited while Ongoing never loads on its own again. The **last tab** is remembered with its tournament id and restored only when that tournament loads again and it is not finished. Once a tournament is loaded its id is in the address bar for everyone, so a reload and sharing keep it. Landing tab per phase: Draft → Admin, Upcoming / Starting soon → Info, Ongoing → Bracket, Finished → Rankings.

1. **Tournaments** (`TournamentsView.tsx`, `tournaments-list.ts`) — three sections from the live list (`index/`), in this order: **Ongoing**, **Upcoming** (soonest first; a "Starting soon" mark appears on the card from 1 hour before the scheduled time until Confirm & Start, `phase-display.ts`), **Finished** (newest first; no countdown). Each card: title, a "Test run" mark where it applies, date and time in the viewer's time zone (en-GB, "Sat 18 Oct, 20:00", with a relative part except on finished cards; "Date to be announced" without one), and the format line with the unit count. Empty texts: "No tournament currently ongoing", "New tournaments coming out soon!", "No tournament completed yet". Opening a card loads that tournament (a finished one lands on Rankings). Drafts, and on production test runs, are not listed, even to an admin. While signed out, a "Log in as admin" link sits in the bottom-right corner of the viewport (`CfpLoginModal`); it is gone when signed in. Signing in from it opens the Admin tab; signing out returns here. There is no rules link on this page.
2. **Info** (`info/InfoView.tsx`, `tournament-info.ts`) — only while a tournament is loaded: the page heading (with the Exit button), a title panel (until the Info redesign), date and time in the viewer's own time zone with a relative part ("in 2 days"), a format line generated from the settings ("1v1 · Swiss · single elimination", no round counts), up to 4 lobby screenshots (thumbnails; a click opens the full size; each image is fetched once and cached in the browser), the tournament's rules text and the general rules link `https://info.curvefever.pro/tournaments/tournament-rules`. The rules text has light formatting (`rules-text.ts`, rendered as React elements only): a blank line starts a paragraph, "- " starts a list, `**bold**`, `[text](https://address)`; only http(s) links are links, and a `javascript:` link or raw HTML shows as plain text.
3. **Setup** (`SetupView.tsx`, before start only) — game format, schedule logic, pooling phase, odd-count strategy, team scoring, scoring system ("Standard points (1st = largest room size, −1 per place)" or "Custom points table"), draw publication, Semis/Final game counts and size overrides, advanced round overrides, waterfall graph editor (after a qualification table it starts with a "Qualification table" source block, one slot per final standing routed like a room's ranks; in the editor a rank with no destination reads "out" in red, for a room's ranks and the table's alike, there is no "unset" state and no "Clear destination" (send ranks to "Eliminated" to reset them), and the "Where everyone goes" panel lists a room's out ranks as "R1 → out"; its header reads "Players registered: N · sent on: K · eliminated: N − K", and "Advance to bracket" becomes a read-only line, "24, set by the waterfall graph"; a leftover `Qual` line under another pooling phase offers "Remove the Qual line"), roster and reserves ("↺ Clear roster" keeps the config), and a schedule preview before "Confirm & Start". Fields and options that don't fit the chosen format are hidden. A hidden field's stored value is ignored by generation; a schedule logic or pooling phase that no longer fits is reset on a format change, and generation refuses a stale one with a message. The Tournament Name panel holds the title (required to start) and the scheduled date and time (`ScheduledTimeField.tsx`, typed in the browser's time zone, which the label names); the "Info for visitors" panel (`InfoContentPanel.tsx`, also shown in the running Admin) holds the rules text, saved when the box is left, and the lobby screenshot upload and delete (disabled when the tournament is finished). `CopySettingsPanel.tsx` copies settings from another tournament, picked from the list or by pasted link/id: everything but the title, roster, reserves and reserve individuals, and also the rules text and screenshots (up to the limit of 4). A tournament generated or set up before the Setup form was stored with it copies from its snapshot; one without a `settings` snapshot says so. **Name tags**: a small "Alex is editing" next to the label of a field (or the Roster, Reserves and Individual reserves titles) another admin is in, refreshed within a second or two and gone 30 s after they leave or close the tab (`presence/`, `docs/platform.md`); Setup and the rules text only, not Bracket. Setup fields update live from other admins; the field being typed in is protected.
4. **Admin** (`AdminTournamentList.tsx` with no tournament loaded, then `LoadedTournamentAdmin.tsx`: `TournamentStatusPanel.tsx`, `SetupView.tsx` before start, `RunningAdmin.tsx`, `RunningAdminStatus.tsx`) — shown only to a signed-in admin (no tab, no login prompt otherwise); signed-in status and sign out are in the header's account menu. With no tournament loaded it lists the drafts, published and ongoing tournaments (status, date, a "Test run" mark) and "New tournament" (with the discreet test-run flag); finished ones are reached through Tournaments. A loaded tournament shows "← All tournaments" and its **status panel** (`admin-actions.ts` decides the buttons, `tournament-lifecycle.ts` the refusals): Draft → Publish (needs a title and a scheduled time, and says which is missing); Published → Unpublish (before it starts); Confirm & Start in Setup (a second one is refused by the server); Finish tournament; Reopen (from Finished); Delete (a draft with a confirmation, a test run from any status); Back to draft in the running Admin (plain confirm; keeps id, roster and settings, clears the played rounds; the only way to delete a started real tournament is Reopen → Back to draft → Unpublish → Delete). Once the Final is complete a "Final complete — check the last scores, then Finish tournament." reminder shows in Admin and Bracket until the tournament is finished (Finished is a button, never automatic, so a wrong last score stays correctable). A **Finished** tournament is read-only for everyone, admins included (score entry and corrections disabled, the server refuses every patch except annotations); Reopen lifts it. Scheduled time and rules text stay editable in every other status. The running Admin keeps the tournament name, a read-only settings recap (`TournamentSettingsRecap`), a waterfall bracket's graph (`RunningWaterfallPanel.tsx`, only when the running tournament has one): read-only by default, "Edit graph" opens the same click-to-route editor as Setup with reached rounds' shape locked (their routing still editable) and a caution notice always visible while editing -- see `docs/rules.md`, "Waterfall bracket" -- a "Correct current round" panel (`live-corrections/`: `CorrectCurrentRoundPanel`, `LineupSection`, `AdvancementSection`; each half shows only while in scope, and a locked line-up says why, e.g. "Round 5 has scores — line-up locked") with a caution that changes apply immediately for everyone watching -- see `docs/rules.md`, "Line-up corrections" and "Bracket phases" -- and the "Tournament progress" timeline.
5. **Bracket** (`BracketView.tsx`) — every round as a collapsible column (past rounds collapsed by default). Double elimination groups a winners-bracket round with the losers-bracket rounds that follow it in one box (`bracketBoxes()`, `bracket.ts`); labels come from `bracketRoundLabels()`. Rooms are shown as letters (`roomLetter()`); logic keeps 1-indexed numbers. Contents:
   - A round played over several games shows an "N games" tag in its column header (`roundGameCount()`, `bracket.ts`), visible to every viewer before the round starts. The race Grand Final shows "up to N games" (WB target + LB target − 1).
   - "Follow a player/team" search: the text resolves on every keystroke to one unit, whose rows are highlighted; the page and the rounds strip never scroll. While the standings are being decided, the follow banner adds the followed unit's standings rank (" · #6 in standings", " · #2 in Group B"); nothing after pooling.
   - **Standings column** (`StandingsColumn.tsx`, shown by `describeStandings()`): right after the last pooling round (from schedule generation on: before "Confirm & Start" every row is unranked), a compact block that is not a round (`bg-surface-low`, a stronger border): the shared context line (`standingsContextLine()`), then rank, name and the points total (a whole number) per row, a cut line (with a waterfall Qual source, a labelled line at each band instead, in this column and on the Standings tab, named by one helper, `standingsBandLabel`: "→ R1", "→ R1C", "out"), a result stripe per row (green through, red out), the followed unit highlighted, one block per group in group stage, and a "Full table →" link to the Standings tab. Rows past the cut get the red result stripe (`ResultStripe`, `stripeTone()` in `result-row.ts`, the same stripe as an eliminated unit in a Bracket room); rows are flush and alternate between two grounds (`rowSurfaceClasses()`), and names never change colour. No room share, score, played or tie marks; an unresolved cut tie shows a shared rank, a resolved one distinct ranks. Foldable to a slim band, per browser only. 
   - **Cut-off tie cards** (admins) stay in Bracket, in the warning tone ("⚖ Qualification tie at the cut-off"), distinct from the red in-room "⚠ Tie-break required" cards.
   - Score entry for the current round (admins), including per-game tabs and a Total tab for multi-game rounds; lone units get a "No opponent" note instead of an input where that's a bye. Team rows show member names in a small line; they take one input per member, labelled with the member name (and 🛡 for the defender under Save your Buddy), and while a row is being scored that labelled-input set replaces the member line. Under Survival Teams a row takes one input per team and keeps the member line. A score saves when its box is left (Tab, Enter or clicking elsewhere), so the room re-sorts once rather than on every digit.
   - A ✋ badge ("Set by organiser") beside a unit placed, moved or reinstated through a line-up correction, in room cards (current and past rounds) and the Final. Bracket only: Standings has no room cards.
   - A note above the action bar (admins) when the round after the current one is already drawn (after "← Previous"), naming that round as its column header does: "is already drawn. Next Round re-draws it from this round's results.", or, when a later round has scores or a bracket round ahead has been advanced from, "is already drawn and locked. Next Round returns to it unchanged; a correction here won't change its draw." (`drawnAheadStatus()`; see `docs/rules.md`, "Going back a round").
   - Tie-break cards at the top (admins) and "⚠ TB?" / "⚖ TB" badges.
   - **Notes** (`AnnotationsPanel.tsx`, `annotations.ts`): a discreet notes icon in the follow row opens the tournament's annotations, newest first (up to 1000 characters each), stored on the tournament (`annotations`). Viewers see the icon only when notes exist; admins always see it, and can add and delete notes during the tournament and after it finishes (the one thing a Finished tournament still accepts). No unread dot; Rankings has no notes.
   - **Exit chips** under each room ("1–4 → Final", "5 ★ lucky loser?", "6–8 out", "1–2 ↑ Room A", "1 stay") and a round summary where no per-room split applies (the last pooling round reads "Top 24 in standings → Round 4"; with a waterfall Qual source it lists the bands instead, "1–16 → R1 · 17–24 → R1C · 25+ out", the open tail left out when nobody is ranked past the routed ranks), all from one domain description, `roundExitRule` (`room-exits.ts`). Scored rows get a result stripe from the same bands (green advance, lucky and promote; yellow stay; orange demote; purple drop; red eliminate; names never change colour); waterfall and double-elimination rows also get a destination tag ("→ SemiB", "→ LB Round 4").
   - Lucky-loser explanation plus live "who's leading" standings on rounds with lucky-loser slots; a one-sentence Kings Valley footnote.
   - Future-round slots labelled with their projected origin (see `docs/seeding.md`); a round the waterfall Qual source feeds shows "Qualification #17" per slot instead of the generic qualifier label.
   - Anonymous Finals: a "🎭 Anonymous matches" card beside the Final, and "🔓 Connect & reveal" once all games are scored.
6. **Standings** (`standings/StandingsView.tsx`, `TournamentStandings.tsx`, `standings-text.ts`) — right after Bracket; hidden unless the tournament has a standings phase (`hasStandingsPhase()`: a qual-table, Swiss or Group Stage round in the schedule), which exists from schedule generation on, before "Confirm & Start" (every row unranked, "Standings start after Round N."). The full detail behind the Bracket column, from `describeStandings()` (`standings-display.ts`): the shared context line, then a written explanation box generated from the tournament's own settings by `standingsExplanation()` (points per place from `roomSize.max` or the Custom table, "A bye counts as a win; a missed round earns 0", the non-counting rounds, "Points are added up; most points ranks first. The top N go through" with N from the display's cut, "qualified" wording once final, the tie-break in plain words; a head-to-head tournament says "a win earns 2 points and a loss 1", and the "whatever the size of your room" note is dropped when every room has the same size), then one table per qual/Swiss table or group (a table with a waterfall Qual source shows a labelled separator at every band boundary instead of the single cut line, and every band tie's members get the tie mark): Pos, Player or Team, per counted round (non-counting rounds are not shown; a group shows its own rounds, `tableRoundIndexes()`) a "Round N" group of **Score | Rank | Pts** from `entry.rounds` (a bye shows "Bye", "—" and its points; a round without a result shows 0 points once the round is over, "—" before), then **Points** (the total) and **Tie-break** (the relative room share as a whole percent, "147%"). Pts is semibold, Score and Rank muted and smaller, and the Points total bold in the primary colour behind a left border. The table scrolls sideways inside its panel (`TableScroll`, `min-w-0` on the panel); the page itself never overflows. A dashed "cut" row after the cut; rows above it in the advance tone, rows below the cut with the red stripe; the followed unit highlighted; ⚖ beside units placed by a resolved cut tie, "⚠ TB?" beside a still-unresolved cut-tie cluster (`cutTieMarks()`). Standings count a current-round room only once it is fully scored (see `docs/rules.md`, "Scoring").
7. **Rankings / Players** (`RankingsView.tsx`, `RankingsRoster.tsx`) — labelled "Players" before Confirm & Start (the roster so far) and "Rankings" from then on; the player-information tab from roster list to live ranking: still-active units with pool-rank badges ("#4 · 10 pts"; from the display standings, so they match the Bracket column and Standings tab; a unit routed to a round that isn't drawn yet, waiting in its pending pool, is listed here too, with "Waiting for <round>" in place of its room), finalists and eliminated units (an eliminated unit's rank always continues below every unit not eliminated, so the 7 teams cut at a 16-of-23 qualification cut rank 17–23; units eliminated at a standings cut are ordered by those standings, and units level in them share a rank unless the organiser placed them in a cut tie; every other elimination goes later round first, then Kings Valley room (a lower room first), place in the room (competition rank by score; level scores share a place), room share relative to the room average (the Standings tie-break measure, so a small room gets no head start), then the pooling standings as the last tie-break; a unit level on all of these shares a rank, and Final ties share a rank too), final standings (with DNF), PNG export (`rankings-image.ts`). For admins it also holds roster management (`ManageRoster`: rename, swap, remove, designated defender, fill a vacant slot from reserves; the ⇄ Swap buttons are disabled with "Swaps closed — eliminations have started" once anyone is eliminated, and a swap of a unit that is neither seated nor waiting alerts instead of doing nothing), the reserve window (`ReservePanel`, smallest-room placement) and the "No-shows" panel.
