# Views and viewer design

What each tab does today, and the principles viewer-facing work is judged against. Read when a task touches the UI (`src/features/*`).

## Design intent for viewer-facing tabs
The standard for new viewer-facing work. Every player plays online, alone, at their own computer: no live events, no audience, no stream overlay.

**Three viewer questions**, in order of importance: (1) **where do I play**: my room and who I'm facing, with zero clicks; (2) **what's at stake for me**: am I inside the qualifying cut, and by how much; (3) **how is everyone else doing**: the fun of following the whole tournament.

**Each viewer tab has one job.** **Bracket** is the default and answers all three at a glance: rooms and results for (1) and (3), and during pooling a compact standings column plus the follow banner's rank for (2). **Standings** is the full detail behind that column: every figure, the tie-breaker and tie marks, for anyone who wants to check the numbers; it exists only when the format has a standings phase. **Rankings** is who's still in and the final order, plus roster management for admins. **Archive** is past tournaments.

**Glance in Bracket, detail in Standings.** Bracket shows only what a player needs at a glance (rank, name, points, the cut); explanations, extra columns and tie marks belong in Standings.

- **No staggered reveal**: every score reaches every connected viewer at the same instant.
- **"Follow a player," never "who are you."** Anyone can follow any name.
- **A collapsed round is a display state, not a data reduction.** Reopening shows exactly the detail the current round's display uses.
- **Future rounds default to fully visible**, not collapsed or faded; a viewer may fold any round, but the app never does so by default.
- **One typeface.** The whole app uses Asap (Google Fonts, linked in `routes/__root.tsx`, set on `body` in `styles.css`); the Rankings PNG draws with it too (`rankings-image.ts` loads the weights before drawing).

## Tabs
1. **Setup** (`SetupView.tsx`, before start only) — game format, schedule logic, pooling phase, odd-count strategy, team scoring, scoring system ("Standard points (1st = largest room size, −1 per place)" or "Custom points table"), draw publication, Semis/Final game counts and size overrides, advanced round overrides, waterfall graph editor, roster and reserves ("↺ Clear roster" keeps the config), and a schedule preview before "Confirm & Start". Fields and options that don't fit the chosen format are hidden. A hidden field's stored value is ignored by generation; a schedule logic or pooling phase that no longer fits is reset on a format change, and generation refuses a stale one with a message. `CopySettingsPanel.tsx`, right after the Tournament Name panel, copies settings from a past tournament, picked from the shared Archive (entries with saved settings) or by pasted link/id: everything but the title, roster, reserves and reserve individuals. Only a tournament carrying a `settings` snapshot (taken at generation) can be copied; older ones say so.
2. **Admin** (`RunningAdmin.tsx`, `RunningAdminStatus.tsx`, plus the "Admin Access" panel in `AppShell.tsx`) — signed-in status and sign out, tournament name, a read-only settings recap (`TournamentSettingsRecap`), a waterfall bracket's graph (`RunningWaterfallPanel.tsx`, only when the running tournament has one): read-only by default, "Edit graph" opens the same click-to-route editor as Setup with reached rounds' shape locked (their routing still editable) and a caution notice always visible while editing -- see `docs/rules.md`, "Waterfall bracket" -- a "Correct current round" panel (`live-corrections/`: `CorrectCurrentRoundPanel`, `LineupSection`, `AdvancementSection`; each half shows only while in scope, and a locked line-up says why, e.g. "Round 5 has scores — line-up locked") with a caution that changes apply immediately for everyone watching -- see `docs/rules.md`, "Line-up corrections" and "Bracket phases" -- the "Tournament progress" timeline, and Save to Archive / Reset / Save & Start New with title-collision and unsaved-changes prompts.
3. **Bracket** (`BracketView.tsx`) — every round as a collapsible column (past rounds collapsed by default). Double elimination groups a winners-bracket round with the losers-bracket rounds that follow it in one box (`bracketBoxes()`, `bracket.ts`); labels come from `bracketRoundLabels()`. Rooms are shown as letters (`roomLetter()`); logic keeps 1-indexed numbers. Contents:
   - A round played over several games shows an "N games" tag in its column header (`roundGameCount()`, `bracket.ts`), visible to every viewer before the round starts. The race Grand Final shows "up to N games" (WB target + LB target − 1).
   - "Follow a player/team" search: the text resolves on every keystroke to one unit, whose rows are highlighted; the page and the rounds strip never scroll. While the standings are being decided, the follow banner adds the followed unit's standings rank (" · #6 in standings", " · #2 in Group B"); nothing after pooling.
   - **Standings column** (`StandingsColumn.tsx`, shown by `describeStandings()`): right after the last pooling round (from schedule generation on: before "Confirm & Start" every row is unranked), a compact block that is not a round (`bg-surface-low`, a stronger border): the shared context line (`standingsContextLine()`), then rank, name and the points total (a whole number) per row, a cut line, advance/out colours, the followed unit highlighted, one block per group in group stage, and a "Full table →" link to the Standings tab. Rows past the cut are muted while the standings are live and, once final, get the red left bar of an eliminated row (grey text, never struck through; the same `resultClasses()` style as an eliminated unit in a Bracket room, `result-row.ts`). No room share, score, played or tie marks; an unresolved cut tie shows a shared rank, a resolved one distinct ranks. Foldable to a slim band, per browser only. The archive detail shows it without the link and without folding.
   - **Cut-off tie cards** (admins) stay in Bracket, in the warning tone ("⚖ Qualification tie at the cut-off"), distinct from the red in-room "⚠ Tie-break required" cards.
   - Score entry for the current round (admins), including per-game tabs and a Total tab for multi-game rounds; lone units get a "No opponent" note instead of an input where that's a bye. Team rows show member names in a small line; they take one input per member, labelled with the member name (and 🛡 for the defender under Save your Buddy), and while a row is being scored that labelled-input set replaces the member line. Under Survival Teams a row takes one input per team and keeps the member line. A score saves when its box is left (Tab, Enter or clicking elsewhere), so the room re-sorts once rather than on every digit.
   - A ✋ badge ("Set by organiser") beside a unit placed, moved or reinstated through a line-up correction, in room cards (current and past rounds) and the Final. Bracket only: Standings has no room cards.
   - A note above the action bar (admins) when the round after the current one is already drawn (after "← Previous"), naming that round as its column header does: "is already drawn. Next Round re-draws it from this round's results.", or, when a later round has scores or a bracket round ahead has been advanced from, "is already drawn and locked. Next Round returns to it unchanged; a correction here won't change its draw." (`drawnAheadStatus()`; see `docs/rules.md`, "Going back a round").
   - Tie-break cards at the top (admins) and "⚠ TB?" / "⚖ TB" badges.
   - **Exit chips** under each room ("1–4 → Final", "5 ★ lucky loser?", "6–8 out", "1–2 ↑ Room A", "1 stay") and a round summary where no per-room split applies, all from one domain description, `roundExitRule` (`room-exits.ts`). Scored rows are coloured from the same bands (advance, lucky, drop, promote, stay, demote, eliminate); waterfall and double-elimination rows also get a destination tag ("→ SemiB", "→ LB Round 4").
   - Lucky-loser explanation plus live "who's leading" standings on rounds with lucky-loser slots; a one-sentence Kings Valley footnote.
   - Future-round slots labelled with their projected origin (see `docs/seeding.md`).
   - Anonymous Finals: a "🎭 Anonymous matches" card beside the Final, and "🔓 Connect & reveal" once all games are scored.
4. **Standings** (`standings/StandingsView.tsx`, `TournamentStandings.tsx`, `standings-text.ts`) — third tab, after Bracket; hidden unless the tournament has a standings phase (`hasStandingsPhase()`: a qual-table, Swiss or Group Stage round in the schedule), which exists from schedule generation on, before "Confirm & Start" (every row unranked, "Standings start after Round N."). The full detail behind the Bracket column, from `describeStandings()` (`standings-display.ts`): the shared context line, then a written explanation box generated from the tournament's own settings by `standingsExplanation()` (points per place from `roomSize.max` or the Custom table, "A bye counts as a win; a missed round earns 0", the non-counting rounds, "Points are added up; most points ranks first. The top N go through" with N from the display's cut, "qualified" wording once final, the tie-break in plain words; a head-to-head tournament says "a win earns 2 points and a loss 1", and the "whatever the size of your room" note is dropped when every room has the same size), then one table per qual/Swiss table or group: Pos, Player or Team, per counted round (non-counting rounds are not shown; a group shows its own rounds, `tableRoundIndexes()`) a "Round N" group of **Score | Rank | Pts** from `entry.rounds` (a bye shows "Bye", "—" and its points; a round without a result shows 0 points once the round is over, "—" before), then **Points** (the total) and **Tie-break** (the relative room share as a whole percent, "147%"). Pts is semibold, Score and Rank muted and smaller, and the Points total bold in the primary colour behind a left border. The table scrolls sideways inside its panel (`TableScroll`, `min-w-0` on the panel); the page itself never overflows. A dashed "cut" row after the cut; rows above it in the advance tone, rows below muted while live and, once final, with the red eliminated bar and grey text; the followed unit highlighted; ⚖ beside units placed by a resolved cut tie, "⚠ TB?" beside a still-unresolved cut-tie cluster (`cutTieMarks()`). Standings count a current-round room only once it is fully scored (see `docs/rules.md`, "Scoring").
5. **Rankings** (`RankingsView.tsx`, `RankingsRoster.tsx`) — the player-information tab from roster list to live ranking: still-active units with pool-rank badges ("#4 · 10 pts"; from the display standings, so they match the Bracket column and Standings tab), finalists and eliminated units (an eliminated unit's rank always continues below every unit not eliminated, so the 7 teams cut at a 16-of-23 qualification cut rank 17–23; units eliminated at a standings cut are ordered by those standings, and units level in them share a rank unless the organiser placed them in a cut tie; every other elimination goes later round first, then Kings Valley room (a lower room first), place in the room (competition rank by score; level scores share a place), room share relative to the room average (the Standings tie-break measure, so a small room gets no head start), then the pooling standings as the last tie-break; a unit level on all of these shares a rank, and Final ties share a rank too), final standings (with DNF), PNG export (`rankings-image.ts`). For admins it also holds roster management (`ManageRoster`: rename, swap, remove, designated defender, fill a vacant slot from reserves), the reserve window (`ReservePanel`, smallest-room placement) and the "No-shows" panel.
6. **Archive** (`ArchiveView.tsx`, `ArchiveDetail.tsx`) — the shared archive, visible to everyone including anonymous visitors: a live list (title, date, player or team count, rounds played) and a read-only detail view reusing the Bracket/Rankings renderers, with annotations shown to all. Tour Admins can also add and delete annotations and delete an entry (with a confirmation); entries are saved from Admin ("Save to Archive", with an overwrite confirmation, or automatically when the Final completes). One entry per tournament id. An admin whose browser still holds the old local-only archive sees a notice with a "Download (JSON)" button; there is no import and no per-entry export.
