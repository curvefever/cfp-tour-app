# Views and viewer design

What each tab does today, and the principles viewer-facing work is judged against. Read when a task touches the UI (`src/features/*`).

## Design intent for viewer-facing tabs
The standard for new viewer-facing work. Every player plays online, alone, at their own computer: no live events, no audience, no stream overlay.

**Three viewer questions**, in order of importance: (1) **where do I play**: my room and who I'm facing, with zero clicks; (2) **what's at stake for me**: am I inside the qualifying cut, and by how much; (3) **how is everyone else doing**: the fun of following the whole tournament.

**Each viewer tab has one job.** **Tournaments** lists the tournaments that are on and is where a visit lands when no tournament loads. **Info** is one tournament's date, format, lobby screenshots and rules. **Bracket** answers all three at a glance: rooms and results for (1) and (3), and during pooling a compact standings column plus the follow banner's rank for (2). **Standings** is the full detail behind that column: every figure, the tie-breaker and tie marks, for anyone who wants to check the numbers; it exists only when the format has a standings phase. **Rankings** is who's still in and the final order, plus roster management for admins (before the tournament starts the same tab is "Players", the roster so far). Past tournaments are Finished ones on the Tournaments page; there is no Archive.

**Glance in Bracket, detail in Standings.** Bracket shows only what a player needs at a glance (rank, name, points, the cut); explanations, extra columns and tie marks belong in Standings.

- **No staggered reveal**: every score reaches every connected viewer at the same instant.
- **"Follow a player," never "who are you."** Anyone can follow any name.
- **A collapsed round is a display state, not a data reduction.** Reopening shows exactly the detail the current round's display uses.
- **Future rounds default to fully visible**, not collapsed or faded; a viewer may fold any round, but the app never does so by default.
- **One typeface.** The whole app uses Asap in weights 400, 600, 700 and 800 (Google Fonts, linked in `routes/__root.tsx`, set on `body` in `styles.css`); the Rankings PNG draws with it too (`rankings-image.ts` loads the weights before drawing). The recurring text styles (page title 28/700, view label 18/800 caps, column title, room header, panel label) are class strings in `components/ui/typography.ts`.

## Two looks
The viewer tabs (Tournaments, Info, Bracket, Standings, Rankings/Players) use the **game look**: navy grounds, solid blue room headers, chunky buttons, a result stripe beside every name. The header and tab bar and the organiser's screens (Admin: the tournament list, Setup, running Admin) use the **middle look**: navy-petrol ground, bordered rounded panels, small caps section labels. One set of components serves both: they read the semantic `--app-*` tokens (`styles.css`), and a `data-look` attribute (`game` or `middle`) on a wrapper switches them. `AppShell` sets it from the active tab (Admin = middle, every other tab = game) and pins the header and tab bar to the middle look. The raw brand palette is `--color-cfp-*` (the brief's codes, exact); components use the semantic tokens or the palette's named colours, never colour literals (the Rankings PNG draws on a canvas and is the one exception until it is rebuilt). Per look: buttons are 4 px (middle) or 2 px (game) radius and weight 700 (middle) or 800 (game), with a darker bottom edge; panels have a 1 px border and no shadow; form controls are 40 px high at 15 px, with sentence-case 14/700 field labels. Where the boards and the brief differ, the brief wins.

## Tabs
Tab rules live in `shell/tabs.ts` (`visibleTabs()`, `effectiveTab()`, `loadedPhase()`), from the loaded tournament's phase: Tournaments is always listed; Info and Rankings/Players only while a tournament is loaded; Bracket only once it has started (ongoing, or finished after being played); Standings only then and with a standings phase; Admin only for a signed-in admin. A stored tab that is not available is displayed as a fallback (Standings → Bracket, anything else → the tournament's Info, or the list when nothing is loaded) and never overwritten, so an admin whose session restores a moment after load returns to Admin by itself. Viewer-facing empty states never point at Admin. The **header** (`AppHeader.tsx`, middle look, sticky) holds the logo and "CFP Tournament Hub" button (opens Tournaments), the tabs in plain words (no emoji) in `nav aria-label='Tournament sections'`, and for a signed-in admin "Signed in · <role>" (`AccountMenu.tsx`), a button that opens a one-item menu "Sign out (<name>)". Tournaments stands apart; the loaded tournament's tabs sit together on a lighter band (`role='group'`, "This tournament"); with no tournament loaded, a divider sets Admin apart. It shows no tournament name and no round counter. **Exit** lives only on Info, as the outlined button "✕ Exit tournament view". Every page opens with a **heading** (`PageHeading.tsx`): the tournament's name (or "Tournaments" / "Admin" for the lists) with the view's name in small caps underneath ("Bracket", "Standings", "Rankings" or "Players", "Info", "Setup" before the start and "Admin" after).

**What loads on a visit** (`visit.ts`, `resolveVisit`): a link `?t=<id>` always opens that tournament, or, when a viewer cannot see it (a draft, a production test run, deleted, an unknown id), shows "This tournament isn't available." and opens Tournaments. Without a link: exactly one visible tournament Starting soon or Ongoing loads (Info before Confirm & Start, Bracket after); several open the Tournaments page; none loads the most recently finished tournament if it finished under 24 hours ago, on Rankings; otherwise Tournaments. **Exit** unloads the tournament, opens Tournaments and sticks across reloads, remembered with the phase at Exit: it is forgotten as soon as a different tournament would load, an exited tournament that was Starting soon loads again once it is Ongoing, and one exited while Ongoing never loads on its own again. The **last tab** is remembered with its tournament id and restored only when that tournament loads again and it is not finished. Once a tournament is loaded its id is in the address bar for everyone, so a reload and sharing keep it. Landing tab per phase: Draft → Admin, Upcoming / Starting soon → Info, Ongoing → Bracket, Finished → Rankings.

1. **Tournaments** (`TournamentsView.tsx`, `tournaments-list.ts`) — three sections from the live list (`index/`), in this order: **Ongoing**, **Upcoming** (soonest first; a "Starting soon" mark appears on the card from 1 hour before the scheduled time until Confirm & Start, `phase-display.ts`), **Finished** (newest first; no countdown). Each card: title, a "Test run" mark where it applies, date and time in the viewer's time zone (en-GB, "Sat 18 Oct, 20:00", with a relative part except on finished cards; "Date to be announced" without one), and the format line with the unit count. Empty texts: "No tournament currently ongoing", "New tournaments coming out soon!", "No tournament completed yet". Opening a card loads that tournament (a finished one lands on Rankings). Drafts, and on production test runs, are not listed, even to an admin. While signed out, a "Log in as admin" link sits in the bottom-right corner of the viewport (`CfpLoginModal`); it is gone when signed in. Signing in from it opens the Admin tab; signing out returns here. There is no rules link on this page.
2. **Info** (`info/InfoView.tsx`, `tournament-info.ts`) — only while a tournament is loaded: the page heading (with the Exit button), a title panel (until the Info redesign), date and time in the viewer's own time zone with a relative part ("in 2 days"), a format line generated from the settings ("1v1 · Swiss · single elimination", no round counts), up to 4 lobby screenshots (thumbnails; a click opens the full size; each image is fetched once and cached in the browser), the tournament's rules text and the general rules link `https://info.curvefever.pro/tournaments/tournament-rules`. The rules text has light formatting (`rules-text.ts`, rendered as React elements only): a blank line starts a paragraph, "- " starts a list, `**bold**`, `[text](https://address)`; only http(s) links are links, and a `javascript:` link or raw HTML shows as plain text.
3. **Setup** (`SetupView.tsx`, before start only) — game format, schedule logic, pooling phase, odd-count strategy, team scoring, scoring system ("Standard points (1st = largest room size, −1 per place)" or "Custom points table"), draw publication, Semis/Final game counts and size overrides, advanced round overrides, waterfall graph editor (after a qualification table it starts with a "Qualification table" source block, one slot per final standing routed like a room's ranks; in the editor a rank with no destination reads "out" in red, for a room's ranks and the table's alike, there is no "unset" state and no "Clear destination" (send ranks to "Eliminated" to reset them), and the "Where everyone goes" panel lists a room's out ranks as "R1 → out"; its header reads "Players registered: N · sent on: K · eliminated: N − K", and "Advance to bracket" becomes a read-only line, "24, set by the waterfall graph"; a leftover `Qual` line under another pooling phase offers "Remove the Qual line"), roster and reserves ("↺ Clear roster" keeps the config), and a schedule preview before "Confirm & Start". Fields and options that don't fit the chosen format are hidden. A hidden field's stored value is ignored by generation; a schedule logic or pooling phase that no longer fits is reset on a format change, and generation refuses a stale one with a message. The Tournament Name panel holds the title (required to start) and the scheduled date and time (`ScheduledTimeField.tsx`, typed in the browser's time zone, which the label names); the "Info for visitors" panel (`InfoContentPanel.tsx`, also shown in the running Admin) holds the rules text, saved when the box is left, and the lobby screenshot upload and delete (disabled when the tournament is finished). `CopySettingsPanel.tsx` copies settings from another tournament, picked from the list or by pasted link/id: everything but the title, roster, reserves and reserve individuals, and also the rules text and screenshots (up to the limit of 4). A tournament generated or set up before the Setup form was stored with it copies from its snapshot; one without a `settings` snapshot says so. **Name tags**: a small "Alex is editing" next to the label of a field (or the Roster, Reserves and Individual reserves titles) another admin is in, refreshed within a second or two and gone 30 s after they leave or close the tab (`presence/`, `docs/platform.md`); Setup and the rules text only, not Bracket. Setup fields update live from other admins; the field being typed in is protected.
4. **Admin** (`AdminTournamentList.tsx` with no tournament loaded, then `LoadedTournamentAdmin.tsx`: `TournamentStatusPanel.tsx`, `SetupView.tsx` before start, `RunningAdmin.tsx`, `RunningAdminStatus.tsx`) — shown only to a signed-in admin (no tab, no login prompt otherwise); signed-in status and sign out are in the header's account menu. With no tournament loaded it lists the drafts, published and ongoing tournaments (status, date, a "Test run" mark) and "New tournament" (with the discreet test-run flag); finished ones are reached through Tournaments. A loaded tournament shows "← All tournaments" and its **status panel** (`admin-actions.ts` decides the buttons, `tournament-lifecycle.ts` the refusals): Draft → Publish (needs a title and a scheduled time, and says which is missing); Published → Unpublish (before it starts); Confirm & Start in Setup (a second one is refused by the server); Finish tournament; Reopen (from Finished); Delete (a draft with a confirmation, a test run from any status); Back to draft in the running Admin (plain confirm; keeps id, roster and settings, clears the played rounds; the only way to delete a started real tournament is Reopen → Back to draft → Unpublish → Delete). Once the Final is complete a "Final complete — check the last scores, then Finish tournament." reminder shows in Admin and Bracket until the tournament is finished (Finished is a button, never automatic, so a wrong last score stays correctable). A **Finished** tournament is read-only for everyone, admins included (score entry and corrections disabled, the server refuses every patch except annotations); Reopen lifts it. Scheduled time and rules text stay editable in every other status. The running Admin keeps the tournament name, a read-only settings recap (`TournamentSettingsRecap`), a waterfall bracket's graph (`RunningWaterfallPanel.tsx`, only when the running tournament has one): read-only by default, "Edit graph" opens the same click-to-route editor as Setup with reached rounds' shape locked (their routing still editable) and a caution notice always visible while editing -- see `docs/rules.md`, "Waterfall bracket" -- a "Correct current round" panel (`live-corrections/`: `CorrectCurrentRoundPanel`, `LineupSection`, `AdvancementSection`; each half shows only while in scope, and a locked line-up says why, e.g. "Round 5 has scores — line-up locked") with a caution that changes apply immediately for everyone watching -- see `docs/rules.md`, "Line-up corrections" and "Bracket phases" -- and the "Tournament progress" timeline.
5. **Bracket** (`BracketView.tsx`, with `BracketRounds.tsx`, `RoundBody.tsx`, `RoomCard.tsx`, `FinalColumn.tsx` and the rest of `bracket/`; game look) — every round as a column, past rounds folded by default. Double elimination groups a winners-bracket round with the losers-bracket rounds that follow it in one box (`bracketBoxes()`, `bracket.ts`, a "wave box" with "WINNERS BRACKET" / "LOSERS BRACKET" sub-labels in small caps); labels come from `bracketRoundLabels()` and are plain ("Round 3", "Semis", "Final", "Grand Final", a waterfall round's own label, "WB Round 3"), also in Rankings. Rooms are shown as letters (`roomLetter()`); logic keeps 1-indexed numbers. Contents:
   - **Column title bar** (`ColumnTitleBar.tsx`): the round's name, a NOW pill on the current round, an "N GAMES" tag for a round played over several games (`roundGameCount()`, `bracket.ts`; visible to every viewer before the round starts; the race Grand Final reads "UP TO N GAMES", WB target + LB target − 1) and the fold chevron. A folded round is one small box (`FoldedBox.tsx`, `foldedRoundSummary()`, `folded-round.ts`): DONE for a finished round, "N rooms", and the followed unit's room and place ("Room A · 3rd", "Bye").
   - **Room cards** (`RoomRow.tsx`, `room-title.ts`): the title comes from the round's own flags, never its label: "Final", "Semi A", "Group A · Room B", else "Room A" (waterfall rounds included). A row is place, result stripe, name, then the marks after it (★ lucky loser, ✋ set by organiser, "⚠ TB?" / "⚖ TB") and the score. Names never change colour; the stripe alone carries the result (`roomRowResult()`, `room-row-result.ts`): green advance, lucky loser and promote; yellow second destination and stay; orange demote; purple drop and third destination; red eliminate; dashed undecided. Rows of a room that is not complete are undecided and keep seating order until the room is complete, then sort once. A lucky-loser candidate is dashed with a ★ until the round is advanced, then green (chosen) or red. Pooling rooms and the Final have no stripe. Kings Valley rows have no text labels (the stripe carries it). Team rows show their members under the name ("A 23 · B 18", `team-member-line.ts`; the designated defender marked 🛡; Survival Teams shows names only). Rooms not drawn yet have a lighter header and dashed slots labelled with their projected origin (see `docs/seeding.md`; a round the waterfall Qual source feeds shows "Qualification #17"), and keep the exit icon when their bands are known. A unit sitting a round out is a one-row bye card (`ByeCard.tsx`); fixed draws and group stage use the same cards.
   - **Exit icon** (`ExitIcon.tsx`, `exit-lines.ts`): an arrow on a room's header and on the standings title bar. With a mouse the pop-up opens on hover and on keyboard focus and closes on leaving, blur and Escape; where there is no hover it opens on tap and has a close button. A bracket room lists its bands ("1–4 → Final", "5 ★ lucky loser?", "6–8 out", "1–2 ↑ Room A", "1 stay"; waterfall and double-elimination destinations by name), a pooling room what its places earn ("1st +8", "2nd +7 … 7th +2 points", sized to the room's actual size and the tournament's scoring, "All → standings"; a round that does not count is seeding only), a standings table each run with its destination ("1–24 → Round 4", "25–37 out"; with a waterfall Qual source, "1–16 → R1 · 17–24 → R1C · 25+ out"). All from one domain description, `roundExitRule` (`room-exits.ts`).
   - **Pts column**: pooling rooms of a counting round show SCORE and PTS, the points each place earned (`room-points.ts`), once the room is fully scored.
   - **Follow row**: the "Follow a player/team" box (the text resolves on every keystroke to one unit, whose rows are highlighted; the page and the rounds strip never scroll), the follow banner, the notes icon and a circled "?" at the far right. The banner (`follow-text.ts`) reads "Following Dune · Quarters · Room B · 4th → Semi B": the room, once the room is complete the place and where it leads (the room it was drawn into when the next round is already drawn), and while the standings can still change " · #14 in standings" ("#2 in group" in a group stage when the room's title already names the group; "Out in standings · #25" at the cut). There is no ×: emptying the box stops following. The followed unit's rows have their own surface. The follow is stored once per browser, so it carries over to another tournament ("Not in this bracket").
   - **"?" box** (`BracketInfoBox.tsx`, `bracket-legend.ts`): a pop-up colour key of this tournament only: Through, Out and Undecided always; Lucky loser ★, losers bracket and Kings Valley only where such rounds exist; a row naming the second (yellow) and the third (purple) destination where a waterfall or a Qual source sends units to several rounds; then a paragraph on how lucky losers work and a line on the arrow icon.
   - **Standings column** (`StandingsColumn.tsx`, shown by `describeStandings()`; 280 px): right after the last pooling round (from schedule generation on: before "Confirm & Start" every row is unranked), a block that is not a round: a title bar "STANDINGS" with TEMPORARY while the standings can still change and FINAL once the last pooling round is advanced, the exit icon and the fold chevron; then a framed block of flush rows (rank, a result stripe, name, the points total; a team's members under its name), a gap at the cut and a 12 px gap where a group starts (no cut line, no "cut" label), one block per group in group stage, and a "Full table →" link to the Standings tab. Row results come from `standingsRowResult()` (`result-row.ts`): live rows past the cut are red, like an eliminated unit; names never change colour. A row without a rank (a unit with no counted round yet, as in a group that has not played) has no stripe. No room share, score, played or tie marks; an unresolved cut tie shows a shared rank, a resolved one distinct ranks. Folded, it is a small box with the followed unit's "#rank" (empty without a follow); folding is per browser only.
   - **Lucky-loser race box** (`RoundNotes.tsx`, `still-playing.ts`): a dashed green box "★ LUCKY LOSER RACE · N SPOTS" under the rooms of a round with such spots, listing the candidates by their share of their room total with their room, and "Rooms B and C still playing." until every room is scored; the explanation is in the "?" box. Once the round is advanced the box is gone and the winners carry a solid ★.
   - **Tie cards** (admins, `TieBanners.tsx`): a red "⚠ Tie-break required — Room C (score 655)" card with one button per tied unit, at the top, and the orange "⚖ Qualification tie at the cut-off" card for a tie at a cut.
   - **Score entry** (admins): a 32 px box per unit (44 px on a phone), right-aligned; per-game tabs (buttons) and a Total tab for multi-game rounds; lone units get a "No opponent" note instead of an input where that is a bye. Team rows take one input per member, labelled with the member name (and 🛡 for the defender under Save your Buddy), and while a row is being scored that labelled-input set replaces the member line. Under Survival Teams a row takes one input per team and keeps the member line. A score saves when its box is left (Tab, Enter or clicking elsewhere), so the room re-sorts once rather than on every digit.
   - A note above the action bar (admins) when the round after the current one is already drawn (after "← Previous"), naming that round as its column header does: "is already drawn. Next Round re-draws it from this round's results.", or, when a later round has scores or a bracket round ahead has been advanced from, "is already drawn and locked. Next Round returns to it unchanged; a correction here won't change its draw." (`drawnAheadStatus()`; see `docs/rules.md`, "Going back a round"). The Next Round / Previous bar sticks to the bottom of the screen.
   - **Final** (`FinalColumn.tsx`, `FinalRow.tsx`): a room card titled "Final"; place ("1"…), name, one column per game and the total (or the games won in the race Grand Final, which also shows who is ahead). Anonymous Finals: a "🎭 Anonymous matches" card beside the Final, and "🔓 Connect & reveal" per finalist once all games are scored.
   - **"Final complete" banner** (`FinishReminder.tsx`, admins, until the tournament is finished): a green banner "Final complete — check the last scores, then Finish tournament." with "Only admins see this" and a "Finish tournament" button that runs the status panel's own action (`useStatusAction.ts`; no confirmation).
   - **Notes** (`AnnotationsPanel.tsx`, `annotations.ts`): the notes icon in the follow row opens the tournament's annotations in a pop-up, newest first (up to 1000 characters each), stored on the tournament (`annotations`). Viewers see the icon only when notes exist; admins always see it, and can add and delete notes (a confirmation on delete) during the tournament and after it finishes (the one thing a Finished tournament still accepts). No unread dot; Rankings has no notes.
6. **Standings** (`standings/StandingsView.tsx`, `StandingsTable.tsx`, `TournamentStandings.tsx`, `standings-text.ts`; game look) — right after Bracket; hidden unless the tournament has a standings phase (`hasStandingsPhase()`: a qual-table, Swiss or Group Stage round in the schedule), which exists from schedule generation on, before "Confirm & Start" (every row unranked, "Standings start after Round N."). The full detail behind the Bracket column, from `describeStandings()` (`standings-display.ts`). Top to bottom:
   - the page heading; the **follow banner** when a unit is followed (the same `FollowBar` as the Bracket's, content-width; the follow is stored once per browser): "Following Dune" with "#17 · → Round 4" (`standingsFollowText()`: the rank and where the standings send it, "out" for a unit past the cut, the destination round of its run with a waterfall Qual source), nothing after the name while the unit has no rank yet, "Not in these standings" for a unit in no table;
   - the **"How the standings work" box**, written from the tournament's own settings by `standingsExplanation()`, one paragraph each: where the standings are (`standingsProgressSentence()`: "After 2 of 3 rounds.", "These are the final standings.", "Standings start after Round N."), the points per place (from `roomSize.max`, or the Custom table; "whatever the size of your room" is dropped when every room has the same size; a head-to-head tournament says "a win earns 2 points and a loss 1"), "A bye counts as a win; a missed round earns 0." plus the non-counting rounds, "Points are added up; most points ranks first. The top N go through" (N from the display's cut; "qualified" once final; "of each group" in a group stage; "Everyone goes through" without a cut) and the tie-break in plain words;
   - **one table per qual/Swiss table or group**, each with a **title row**: the table's name in caps, the TEMPORARY / FINAL badge (`PhaseBadge.tsx`, nothing before the standings start) and the exit icon (`ExitIcon.tsx`, `standingsExitLines()`: "1–24 → Round 4", "25–37 out"; each group has its own). The table (`StandingsTable.tsx`) sits in a frame and scrolls sideways inside it; the page itself never overflows. The header is on the frame colour: a "Round N" head with a brand underline over its three columns (non-counting rounds are not shown; a group shows its own rounds, `tableRoundIndexes()`), then POS, PLAYER or TEAM, SCORE | RANK | PTS per round, POINTS and TIE-BREAK. The rows are 40 px (taller where a team's members show under its name), flush and alternating, the followed unit's row on its own ground with a ring: the place, then the result stripe (`standingsRowResult()`, `result-row.ts`: green through; yellow and purple for the second and third destination of a Qual source; red out; no stripe before the standings start or for a row without a rank, i.e. a unit with no counted round yet), the name (⚖ beside units placed by a resolved cut tie, "⚠ TB?" beside a still-unresolved cut-tie cluster, `cutTieMarks()`), Score and Rank muted, Pts bold, the Points total bigger, and the tie-break as a whole percent of the room average ("147%"). A round shows its values from `entry.rounds` (a bye shows "Bye", "—" and its points; a round without a result shows 0 points once the round is over, "—" before). Where a new group of rows begins (the rows through and the rows out, or each run of a Qual source's bands) a gap is drawn, the same as in the Bracket column; there is no cut line and no label. Standings count a current-round room only once it is fully scored (see `docs/rules.md`, "Scoring").
7. **Rankings / Players** (`RankingsView.tsx`, `RankingsLists.tsx`, `PlayersList.tsx`, `RankingsRoster.tsx`) — game look; labelled "Players" before Confirm & Start (the roster so far) and "Rankings" from then on. **Before the start** a viewer sees "37 players registered so far · starts Sat 18 Oct, 20:00" ("13 teams …"; without a date the line stops after "so far"), the roster numbered in three columns of alternating rows in natural alphabetical order (by the name shown, so a team by its team name), and a RESERVES title bar over the reserves in waiting order (`rosterRows`, `players-line.ts`). **While the tournament runs** two lists sit side by side, STILL IN and ELIMINATED, each on a title bar with a count tag: a row is its place (eliminated only), the result stripe, the name (a team's members under it) and the "where" line at the right (`ranking-where.ts`: "Quarters · Room B · 2nd" once the room is complete, "Quarters · Room B" before, "Waiting for Semis", "Round 3 · Room A · 7th", "Standings · #25" for a unit cut at the standings, "Standings · #3 in Group B" in a group stage, "Withdrawn"; round names are the Bracket's, `bracketRoundLabels`). Those still in are in natural alphabetical order and carry no number; a unit cut but not yet moved on keeps its red stripe in STILL IN until Next Round, as in the Bracket; the withdrawn close the ELIMINATED list with "—" and no stripe and are counted in its tag. There is no pool-rank badge: Rankings says where a unit stands in the tournament, the Standings tab where it stands in the pool. An eliminated unit's rank continues below every unit not eliminated (the 7 teams cut at a 16-of-23 qualification cut rank 17–23); units eliminated at a standings cut are ordered by those standings, and units level in them share a rank unless the organiser placed them in a cut tie; every other elimination goes later round first, then Kings Valley room (a lower room first), place in the room (competition rank by score; level scores share a place), room share relative to the room average (the Standings tie-break measure, so a small room gets no head start), then the pooling standings as the last tie-break; a unit level on all of these shares a rank, and Final ties share a rank too. **After the Final** (`finalStanding`, `ranking-podium.ts`): a podium of three cards, second · first · third, under a gold, silver or bronze bar ("Final · 429 over 3 games", "Final · 80" for one game, games won for the race Grand Final), then everyone from the fourth entry down in one three-column list with the where lines and no stripes; units level on a rank keep the same number, podium included, and when two share 3rd the second "3rd" heads the list below; the withdrawn come last without a number. **Download image** (page heading; `rankings-image.ts`, `rankings-image-layout.ts`, `rankings-image-rows.ts`): a 1200 px PNG at 2× scale on the game page colour, three columns filled column by column, the same lines as the page (still in A–Z, then eliminated, then withdrawn; after the Final first place down with gold, silver and bronze ranks), labelled "LIVE RANKINGS" while the tournament runs and "FINAL RANKINGS" after the Final; no podium and no members under a team name. **Organiser tools** (admins only, below the lists and on the Players tab before the start; a `data-look='middle'` block with a bar "ORGANISER TOOLS · Only admins see this part"): MANAGE PLAYERS or MANAGE TEAMS (`ManageRoster`: ✎ rename, ⇄ swap, ✕ remove as small icon buttons, plus designated defender and filling a vacant slot from reserves for teams; the ⇄ buttons are disabled and the panel header says "Swaps closed — eliminations have started" once anyone is eliminated, and a swap of a unit that is neither seated nor waiting alerts instead of doing nothing; the list is in plain string order), RESERVES (`ReservePanel`, smallest-room placement; once eliminations start: "Reserve window closed — eliminations have started.", a disabled input and "Waiting: …") and NO-SHOWS (name and a NO-SHOW pill). A finished tournament is read-only, so the block is gone for admins too.
